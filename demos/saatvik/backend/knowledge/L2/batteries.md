# UDAY Shakti lithium battery

Sources: https://www.saatvikgroup.com/batteries; PDF /docs/datasheets/Saatvik-UDAY-Shakti-Li-ion-Battery-Datasheet.pdf pp 1–2.

## What it is
- A lithium iron phosphate (LFP) inverter battery for homes and small businesses: 51.2 V, 100 Ah, 5.12 kWh. It stores solar energy for use in power cuts and at night, paired with a hybrid inverter such as the UDAY Plus (topic `inverters`).
- 4000+ cycles at 80% depth of discharge; over 95% efficiency; smart BMS with active balancing. Up to 16 modules in parallel. Wall mounted, vertical, indoor.
