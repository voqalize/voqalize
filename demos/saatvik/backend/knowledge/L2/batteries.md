# UDAY Shakti lithium battery

Sources: https://www.saatvikgroup.com/batteries; PDF /docs/datasheets/Saatvik-UDAY-Shakti-Li-ion-Battery-Datasheet.pdf pp 1–2 (document_id saatvik-uday-shakti-li-ion-battery-datasheet).

## What it is
- A lithium iron phosphate (LFP, LiFePO₄) inverter battery for homes and small businesses: 51.2 V, 100 Ah, 5.12 kWh. "It banks what your panels make through the day and gives it back when the grid goes — or when the sun does." Paired with a hybrid inverter such as the UDAY Plus (topic `inverters`; the page's "Pair with UDAY Plus" button opens /inverters/hybrid).
- 4000+ cycles at 80% depth of discharge; charge and discharge efficiency above 95% (datasheet: ≥ 95%); smart BMS with active balancing. Up to 16 modules in parallel. Wall mounted, vertical, indoor; IP50; natural cooling, no fans.
- Warranty: the datasheet's cover carries a "5* YEARS WARRANTY" badge (with an asterisk, terms not given); the page states no term. Say "the datasheet shows a 5-year warranty, subject to terms; Saatvik's team confirms the terms."
- For homes with solar, home backup, daily power backup, small shops and offices, areas with regular power cuts, residential and small commercial use.
