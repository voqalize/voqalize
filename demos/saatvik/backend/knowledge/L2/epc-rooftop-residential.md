# Residential rooftop solar (home EPC)

Sources: https://www.saatvikgroup.com/epc-solutions/rooftop/residential, https://www.saatvikgroup.com/epc-solutions, https://www.saatvikgroup.com/contact. Checked against the live pages 2026-10-14.

## The offer
Turnkey home solar, typically 1 to 10 kW, "with PM Surya Ghar subsidy support, net metering, and savings that start from day one". The overview page: "premium modules, on-grid inverters and net-metering", with structural assessment, subsidy paperwork and commissioning handled end to end. The page's tiles: 4.8 GW module capacity, 10+ countries served, 500+ MW EPC delivered.

## Figures Saatvik publishes (page and FAQs)
- Headline tiles: bill savings up to 90%; PM Surya Ghar subsidy up to ₹78K; 25-year module performance warranty; 4-year typical payback.
- A typical 3 kW home system produces around 360 units a month; as a rule of thumb 1 kW gives about 120 units a month. A monthly bill of 300–400 units is usually covered mostly by 3 kW.
- Roof needed: about 80–100 sq ft of shadow-free roof per kW. The free site survey sizes the system to consumption and roof area.
- Cost: a typical 3 kW system is roughly ₹1.8–2.2 lakh before incentives; PM Surya Ghar subsidy up to ₹78,000; most homeowners recover the outlay in about 4 years.
- A 3 kW system offsets roughly 4 tonnes of CO₂ a year, which the page likens to planting more than 100 trees.
- Installation: most home systems go up in 2–3 days.
- Warranty: the page says modules carry a 12-year product and 25-year performance warranty. That matches Mono PERC; N-TOPCon modules carry a 30-year performance warranty (topic `modules-ntopcon`). Name the module when quoting a warranty.
- Maintenance: very little — periodic panel cleaning and an annual health check; no moving parts.
These are Saatvik's typical figures; actual output, cost and payback depend on location, roof, tariff and consumption. For a personal estimate, offer the solar calculator (/solar-calculator; the residential page itself does not link to it) or the free site survey.

## How it works (the page's five steps, in order)
- 01 Site Survey & Shadow Analysis: roof structure, orientation and shading; size matched to monthly consumption, typically 1 to 10 kW.
- 02 System Design & Subsidy Paperwork: the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana application, DISCOM approvals and documents, end to end.
- 03 Installation in Days, Not Weeks: corrosion-resistant mounting, wiring, inverter set-up, safety earthing; most homes in 2–3 days.
- 04 Net Metering & Commissioning: Saatvik coordinates the bidirectional meter with the DISCOM.
- 05 Monitoring & Lifetime Support: app-based generation monitoring, scheduled health checks, after-sales service, for 25+ years.

## The page's FAQs (five; one opens at a time)
- Cost after subsidy: ₹1.8–2.2 lakh for 3 kW before incentives, subsidy up to ₹78,000, about 4 years to recover.
- Size: 120 units/month per kW; 300–400 units → 3 kW; 80–100 sq ft per kW; free site survey.
- Net metering: bidirectional meter, exports credited against imports, Saatvik files the net-metering application.
- PM Surya Ghar: yes — as a registered vendor Saatvik files on the National Portal for Rooftop Solar, coordinates the DISCOM inspection, and the subsidy goes to the homeowner's bank account after commissioning.
- Maintenance: cleaning and an annual check; 12-year product, 25-year performance warranty.

## Subsidy and net metering, in brief
Central financial assistance is ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 for the third, up to ₹78,000. Full detail, eligibility and caveats: topic `subsidy-and-net-metering`.

## Options for the home
- An on-grid system (or the SuryaConnect kit, topic `solar-kits`) works only while the grid is up. For backup in power cuts, a hybrid inverter with a battery (topics `inverters`, `batteries`).
