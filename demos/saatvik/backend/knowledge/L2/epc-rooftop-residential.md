# Residential rooftop solar (home EPC)

Sources: https://www.saatvikgroup.com/epc-solutions/rooftop/residential, https://www.saatvikgroup.com/epc-solutions, https://www.saatvikgroup.com/solar-calculator.

## The offer
Turnkey home solar, typically 1 to 10 kW, with PM Surya Ghar subsidy support and net metering. Saatvik handles the survey, design, subsidy paperwork, installation, net metering and after-sales, using modules from its own 4.8 GW manufacturing facilities, installed by the same EPC teams that build MW-scale projects. Saatvik has delivered 500+ MW of EPC.

## Figures Saatvik publishes (page and FAQs)
- Bill savings up to 90%.
- A typical 3 kW home system produces around 360 units a month; as a rule of thumb 1 kW gives about 120 units a month. A monthly bill of 300–400 units is usually covered mostly by 3 kW.
- Roof needed: about 80–100 sq ft of shadow-free roof per kW.
- Cost: a typical 3 kW system is roughly ₹1.8–2.2 lakh before incentives; PM Surya Ghar subsidy up to ₹78,000; most homeowners recover the outlay in about 4 years.
- A 3 kW system offsets roughly 4 tonnes of CO₂ a year, which the page likens to planting more than 100 trees.
- Installation: most home systems go up in 2–3 days.
- Warranty: the page says modules carry a 12-year product and 25-year performance warranty. That matches Mono PERC; N-TOPCon modules carry a 30-year performance warranty (topic `modules-ntopcon`). Name the module when quoting a warranty.
- Maintenance: very little — periodic panel cleaning and an annual health check; no moving parts.
These are Saatvik's typical figures; actual output, cost and payback depend on location, roof, tariff and consumption. For a personal estimate, use the solar calculator on the site or ask for a free site survey.

## How it works (the page's steps, in order)
- Site survey and shadow analysis: roof structure, orientation and shading; size matched to monthly consumption.
- System design and subsidy paperwork: the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana application, DISCOM approvals and documents, end to end.
- Installation in days: corrosion-resistant mounting, wiring, inverter set-up, earthing.
- Net metering and commissioning: Saatvik coordinates the bidirectional meter with the DISCOM.
- Monitoring and lifetime support: app-based monitoring, scheduled health checks, after-sales service.

## Subsidy and net metering, in brief
Saatvik is a registered vendor: it files the application on the National Portal for Rooftop Solar, coordinates the DISCOM inspection, and the subsidy is credited to the homeowner's bank account after commissioning. Central financial assistance is ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 for the third, up to ₹78,000. Full detail, eligibility and caveats: topic `subsidy-and-net-metering`.

## Options for the home
- An on-grid system (or the SuryaConnect kit, topic `solar-kits`) works only while the grid is up. For backup in power cuts, a hybrid inverter with a battery (topics `inverters`, `batteries`).
