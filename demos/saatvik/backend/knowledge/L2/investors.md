## Results Saatvik has announced (press releases)
FY26, year ended March 31, 2026 (audited, announced May 20, 2026):
- Revenue from operations ₹45,484 Mn, up 111% year on year; Q4 FY26 revenue ₹16,077 Mn, up 75%.
- EBITDA ₹5,811 Mn, up 62%; PAT ₹3,571 Mn, up 64%.
- Production 3,162 MW, effective capacity utilisation 84.07%.
- Order book about 5.89 GW at March 31, 2026.
- Debt-equity 0.65, from 1.34 in FY25.
- Solar pump revenue grew from ₹25 Mn to ₹472 Mn.
- Plans announced: Odisha Phase 2 cell capacity raised from 2.4 GW to 3.6 GW; EPE film capacity to scale from 2 GW to 5 GW; entry into ingot and wafer manufacturing at a proposed 6 GW.

Earlier FY26 quarters:
- Q3 and 9M FY26 (February 2026): 9M revenue ₹29,407.8 Mn (+137%); Q3 revenue ₹12,570.2 Mn (+143%); 9M EBITDA ₹4,693.4 Mn, margin 15.96%; 9M PAT ₹3,007.9 Mn; Q3 production 759 MW at about 81% utilisation; order book about 5.05 GW.
- H1 FY26 (November 2025): revenue ₹16,838 Mn (+133%); PAT ₹2,021 Mn; order book nearly 4.68 GW at September 30, 2025; debt-equity 0.44.
- Q1 FY26 (October 2025): production 685 MW at 81.47% utilisation; order book 4.05 GW at June 30, 2025.
