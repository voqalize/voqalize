## Raising a complaint (/customer-support)
The steps, in order:
- Raise the request by email or the helpline with the product serial number and purchase invoice; for a module, add photos of the defect.
- A ticket reference comes back within 24 working hours.
- Diagnosis: remote first, then an on-site visit if needed.
- Resolution by guidance, repair or replacement; the ticket closes after the customer confirms.
Typical resolution is about 7 working days; complex warranty claims can take longer.

## Warranty terms
- Modules: 12-year product warranty. Performance warranty 25 years for Mono PERC and 30 years for N-TOPCon, backed by Munich RE.
- Uday on-grid inverters: 10 years. UDAY Plus hybrid inverter: 5 years. Solar pumps: 5 years.
- The module warranty card (Saatvik Green Energy) gives a 10-year product warranty for poly modules and 12 years for half-cut modules, and these minimum outputs, measured at STC:
  - Poly: at least 97.5% in year 1, then no more than 0.70% a year, at least 80.7% at the end of year 25.
  - Mono PERC half-cut: at least 98% in year 1, then no more than 0.55% a year, at least 84.8% at the end of year 25.
  - N-TOPCon half-cut: at least 99% in year 1, then no more than 0.40% a year, at least 87.4% at the end of year 30.
- The card's exclusions include: no proof of purchase or product information; not following Saatvik's user manual; improper operation and maintenance; service by unqualified technicians; an altered or illegible nameplate or serial number; mobile installations (vehicles, ships, offshore; trackers and floating systems are allowed); voltage above the maximum system voltage or power surges; defective mounting structures; mould or similar external effects; unauthorised modifications or spare parts. The exact wording is in the card PDF.
- Saatvik's warranty blog lists what can void a solar warranty: installation by someone unauthorised, natural calamities, theft, not registering the warranty (registration windows of 30–90 days are typical), and lack of maintenance. Register the warranty soon after installation.
- The same blog says "Saatvik Solar offers 30 years of performance warranty"; the product pages give 30 years for N-TOPCon and 25 for Mono PERC, so quote the product pages.

## Care and cleaning
- Saatvik's cleaning blog: clean in the early morning or evening when the panels are cool; use a mild non-acidic cleaning solution, neutral water (pH 6.5 to 7.5), a low-pressure hose, a hand wiper, and a soft brush, cotton mop or soft sponge. Avoid bleach and harsh chemicals, which can damage the anti-reflective coating on the glass.
- Saatvik's warranty blog, on care after the warranty ends: clean panels every 15 days, check wiring and connections, test the inverter output, track energy production; inverters typically need replacing after 8–10 years.

## Installation do's and don'ts (Do's & Don'ts PDF)
- Face all panels south for maximum generation. Don't install in shadow-prone spots near trees, water tanks or buildings.
- Leave 100–150 mm clearance between panels and the roof for ventilation, and an 18–20 mm gap between panels.
- Fit a dedicated lightning arrester at the highest point, connected to an isolated earthing pit. Installers wear a full-body safety harness with a double lanyard and follow MNRE guidelines.
- Two people carry each panel. Use a proper crimping tool on original MC4 connectors; use rated AC and DC distribution boxes.
- Don't install panels that arrive cracked or with bent frames.
- Don't mix module wattages in one series string.
- Don't install the inverter where it gets direct sun or rain.
- Don't leave DC cables loose or touching the roof; don't put heavy loads on tin sheds (use clamp-based fixings).
- Don't skip net metering or warranty registration; file the commissioning report.

## On-grid kit installation guide (PDF)
- Written for PM Surya Ghar on-grid kits. On unboxing, check boxes, pallet and panels (glass, frame, junction box); report damage or shortage to the dealer or Saatvik with photos, and do not install damaged material.
- Anchor holes for M12 fasteners, 70 mm deep.
- Separate earthing for the lightning arrester, the DC side and the AC side; the lightning arrester sits at least 1 ft above the panels.

## Module installation manual (PDF)
- For installers: modules are rated for 1500 V DC systems, Class II. Multiply Isc and Voc by 1.25 when sizing fuses, cables and string voltage. Grounding with M5/M6 hardware at 3–7 Nm. Handling, mounting, clamping zones and the bifacial model tables are in the manual.
- Of the installation manuals linked, /docs/manuals/Saatvik-Installation-Manual-PV-Modules.pdf has its text drawn as outlines (its search text is OCR); /docs/product-documents/saatvik-pv-module-installation-manual.pdf carries a real text layer and is the one to quote.
