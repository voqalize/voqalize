# Battery energy storage (BESS)

Sources: https://www.saatvikgroup.com/bess, https://www.saatvikgroup.com/newsroom/press-release/saatvik-green-energy-launches-energy-storage-arm-saatvik-power-storage-solutions-limited-to-expand-beyond-solar-manufacturing.

## What Saatvik offers
Containerised lithium iron phosphate (LFP) storage for grid balancing, renewable firming and round-the-clock power, delivered as EPC: Saatvik designs, installs, commissions and runs operations and maintenance. For homes and small businesses, the UDAY Shakti wall battery is the product (topic `batteries`).

## Uses
- Grid services: frequency regulation, balancing supply and demand, shaving system peaks at substations; power conversion that injects or absorbs power in milliseconds.
- Renewable firming: pair with ground-mounted or rooftop solar to smooth intermittency, shift daytime generation into the evening peak, and meet FDRE (firm and dispatchable renewable energy) obligations.
- C&I backup and microgrids: replace diesel gensets, arbitrage time-of-day tariffs, power rural solar-plus-storage microgrids.

## FAQs, in Saatvik's words
- Peak shaving: discharging stored energy in the most expensive or most constrained hours, cutting peak-demand charges; the battery charges when power is cheap or solar is abundant and discharges in the evening peak.
- Why LFP: thermal stability, long cycle life and safety; with ~95% round-trip efficiency it gives the lowest levelised cost of storage over a 15+ year design life.
- With an existing solar plant: retrofitted AC-coupled to an operating plant, or DC- or AC-coupled in a new build, all under one EMS.
- Sizes: one 20-ft container stores up to 5 MWh; containers combine into utility plants of 100 MWh and beyond.
