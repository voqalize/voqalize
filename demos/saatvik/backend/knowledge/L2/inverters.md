## Uday Series, on-grid (grid-tie) string inverters
- From 1.1 kW single-phase units for homes to 50 kW three-phase units for commercial and industrial plants. Over 98.7% peak efficiency. IP65 with anti-PID protection. Standard 10-year warranty. Grid support: reactive-power compensation, low-voltage ride-through, anti-islanding. Certified to IEC 62109; also IEC 61000 (EMC), CE, BIS; made in India.
- Monitoring over Wi-Fi, RS485 or Ethernet, from a phone or computer via the Saatvik app.
- An on-grid inverter needs the grid: it shuts down in a power cut (anti-islanding). For backup in power cuts, the hybrid below is the one.

### Single phase (/inverters/single-phase)
- For homes, villas and small establishments. Over 98.7% efficiency, 80–500 V MPPT window with 1–2 MPPT channels, built-in Wi-Fi, quieter than 25 dB, slim wall mount, IP65 with surge, islanding and earth-fault protection.
- Flyer (p 1–2): 1.1 kW to 6 kW (1.1, 2.2, 2.5, 2.7, 3, 3.3, 3.68, 4, 5 and 6 kW models, SS-GTI-1P series), 150% PV input oversizing, MPPT efficiency 99.9%, max efficiency 97.5–97.9% and Euro efficiency 96.9–97.2% by model, 220/230/240 V, 50/60 Hz, power factor 0.8 leading to 0.8 lagging, THDi under 3%, transformerless, natural cooling, display LED + OLED, optional Wi-Fi, 4G or Ethernet, optional AFCI, built-in zero export (optional), Type II DC and AC surge protection, ambient −25 to +60 °C, night self-consumption under 1 W, wall-mounting bracket, MC4 connectors. Smaller models 290 × 220 × 119 mm, 5.5 kg; larger 353 × 320 × 150 mm, 10 kg.

### Three phase (/inverters/three-phase)
- For commercial and industrial solar, 6–50 kW, multi-MPPT, active/reactive power control, LVRT, built-in PID recovery, smart cooling, RS485/Ethernet and SCADA integration.
- The page text says a "200–1500 V operating window"; its table and the /inverters table say 200–1000 V MPPT. Quote the table and offer the user manual for exact figures.
- Flyer (p 3): 6, 8, 10, 12, 15, 20, 25, 30, 40 and 50 kW models; 150% PV input oversizing; Type II SPD on DC and AC; optional AFCI; built-in zero export (optional).

## UDAY Plus hybrid inverter (/inverters/hybrid)
- One unit that manages solar panels, the grid and a battery, keeping essential loads running in a power cut. Single phase. Works with lead-acid and lithium-ion batteries. Dual MPPT, up to 99% MPPT efficiency. IP65, installable outdoors. Monitoring via Wi-Fi / 4G. 5-year warranty. IEC, BIS, CE, ISO 9001 & 14001, made in India.
- For homes, shops, offices, small businesses, schools, clinics, and areas with frequent power cuts.
- The page says 3.6 kW to 8 kW. The datasheet (p 1) covers models from 3K to 8K and lists powers up to 10 kW; quote the page and point to the datasheet for a specific model.
- Datasheet figures: max efficiency 97.6%, Euro efficiency 97.0%; battery voltage 40–60 V; switchover under 10 ms; PV oversizing up to 200%; MPPT range 90–450 V; up to 6 units in parallel; 598 × 364 × 246.5 mm; 28 kg; IEC 61727, IEC 62116, IEC 62109.
- Pairs with the UDAY Shakti battery (topic `batteries`).
