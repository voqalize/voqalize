## Becoming a distributor (/apply-for-distributorship)
What Saatvik offers its partners:
- Competitive margins and marketing support.
- Priority supply from its 4.8 GW of manufacturing.
- Product and sales training.
- Leads shared from Saatvik's own enquiries.
- After-sales service handled centrally, so the partner is not left carrying warranty claims.

Who it looks for:
- A GST-registered business: proprietorship, partnership, LLP or private limited.
- Experience distributing solar, electrical, hardware or agricultural products preferred.
- Warehousing space, and working capital for the opening stock.
- A local sales network, and discipline on brand and pricing.

The application form asks for the business type and, optionally, turnover.

What happens after applying, in order:
- Day 0: the application is submitted.
- Days 1–7: the channel team reviews it.
- Weeks 2–3: a discussion and a visit.
- Week 4: agreement and onboarding.
- Weeks 5–6: launch in the territory.
