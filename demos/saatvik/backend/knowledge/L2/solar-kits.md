# SuryaConnect on-grid solar kits

Sources: https://www.saatvikgroup.com/solar-kits, https://www.saatvikgroup.com/; PDFs /docs/datasheets/Saatvik-Solar-Kit-On-Grid-N-TOPCon-Datasheet.pdf pp 1–4, /docs/datasheets/Saatvik-Solar-Kit-Brochure.pdf p 2, /docs/guides/Saatvik-On-Grid-Solar-Kit-Installation-Guide.pdf pp 1–2.

## What it is
A complete, ready-to-install on-grid rooftop system for homes and businesses, in one pre-engineered package: Saatvik DCR N-TOPCon bifacial modules, a pure-sine-wave grid-tie inverter, AC and DC distribution boxes, DC and AC cables, mounting structure and earthing. No battery needed, which keeps cost and maintenance down. Positioned for PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana and net metering (DCR modules are the domestic-content modules the scheme requires; see topic `subsidy-and-net-metering`).

## Features
- Advanced MPPT; real-time monitoring over Wi-Fi, SIM or app.
- Protection: anti-islanding, DC isolator, AC isolator, surge protection; a lightning arrester is in the box.
- Exports surplus to the grid for credit on the electricity bill; draws from the grid when high-power appliances need more.
- 5-year warranty on the kit; Munich RE as warranty partner; IEC, BIS, CE, PV CYCLE, made in India.
- It works only when the grid is available (anti-islanding safety): an on-grid kit gives no power during a power cut. For backup, the hybrid inverter with the UDAY Shakti battery is the route (topics `inverters`, `batteries`).

## Sizes
- The datasheets cover 3 kW and 5 kW kits. The brochure lists a product range of 3 kW, 5 kW and 6 kW; the home page describes kits from 1 to 10 kW. Quote 3 and 5 kW as the documented kits and send other sizes to the team.
- Prices are not published: "to know more about the price, specifications and availability … get in touch with our team" (sales@saatvikgroup.com, 1800-547-1151).

## Installation
The kit installation guide covers unboxing inspection, mounting, separate earthing for the lightning arrester, DC and AC sides, and the arrester height above the panels; details in topic `installation-and-service`. Saatvik describes the kit as simple enough to install with minimal effort, but PM Surya Ghar subsidy requires installation by a registered vendor; route subsidy buyers through Saatvik's team or a dealer (topic `dealers-and-distributorship`).
