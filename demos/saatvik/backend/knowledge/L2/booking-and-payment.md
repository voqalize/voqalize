## Saatvik's payment terms (the policy pages)

Saatvik's policy pages, added in October 2026, describe a paid booking:

- **Booking amount: ₹500.** It reserves the booking and is not an extra
  charge: it is adjusted in full against the final bill. The balance is paid
  as agreed when the order is confirmed.
- **Payment** goes through HDFC Bank's payment gateway; the options are shown
  at checkout (cards, UPI, net banking among them).
- **Cancel within 7 days** of the booking date (the day the ₹500 was
  confirmed) for a full refund of the booking amount, with no cancellation
  charges. Refunds are processed within **3–5 working days**, to the original
  payment method.
- **How to cancel:** the Refund Request page, /refund. It asks for the email or
  mobile number used at booking and the Payment ID from the booking
  confirmation email (or the booking status page), and an optional reason.
  Help: sales@saatvikgroup.com or 1800-547-1151.

## Delivery (/shipping-returns)

- Booking reserves the product; nothing is dispatched at the time of booking.
- After booking, Saatvik's team or an authorised partner near the visitor
  contacts them to confirm the product, quantity, final price, delivery
  address and timeline. The order ships once it is confirmed and the balance
  is paid.
- Delivery is across India, through Saatvik's logistics partners and
  authorised dealers; timelines depend on the product and location.

## How to talk about it

- If the booking page in front of them says no payment is needed, that is what
  holds for their booking; the ₹500 terms are what the policy pages say
  applies when a payment is taken. Never tell them they will be charged unless
  the page shows a booking amount.
- Sunny never takes, starts or confirms a payment, and never fills in the
  refund form's Payment ID: that is the visitor's.
