## What N-TOPCon is
- TOPCon (Tunnel Oxide Passivated Contact) adds an ultra-thin tunnel oxide layer and a polysilicon film to an N-type silicon wafer. The passivation cuts electron recombination, lifting average cell conversion efficiency to 25% and module efficiency to 23.69%, with better thermal stability and longevity (/modules/n-topcon FAQ).
- Hot and low light: the −0.30 %/°C power temperature coefficient limits hot-climate losses, and N-type cells keep strong output in early morning, overcast and low-irradiance conditions.
- Compatible with standard racking, trackers and string or central inverters; 1500 V DC system voltage; MC4-compatible connectors (/modules/n-topcon FAQ).
- The page's "Six Advantages of Going N-Type": Tunnel Oxide Passivated Contact; Stronger Low-Light Performance; Better Bifaciality; Exceptional Thermal Stability; Lower Degradation; Certified & Bankable ("IEC-qualified, CE-marked modules built in India with 100% EL inspection, backed by 12-year product and 30-year linear performance warranties").

## Figures that disagree across Saatvik's pages
- M10R: 560–600 Wp and 23.23% on its product page and datasheet; 560–585 Wp and 22.8% in the /modules/n-topcon card, table and FAQ; up to 600 Wp and 22.84% on /usa; 560–600 Wp throughout the installation manual the product pages link (/docs/manuals, pp 12, 13, 17, 19); 560–615 Wp on pp 12, 13 and 19 of the copy at /docs/product-documents, which still says 560–600 Wp on p 17.
- G12R: 600–640 Wp and 23.69% on its product page and datasheet; 600–625 Wp throughout the /docs/manuals installation manual (pp 12, 13, 17, 19) and on p 17 of the /docs/product-documents copy (600–640 Wp on its pp 12, 13, 19); "up to 625 Wp" and 23.12% on /usa and in the EPD.
- /modules' comparison table labels the N-TOPCon column 600–640 Wp, "N-Type TOPCon 182 mm", 132 half-cut — that is the G12R alone.
- Mechanical load: product pages and datasheets say 2400 Pa wind and 5400 Pa snow; the installation manual gives a design load of 3600 Pa down and 1600 Pa up with a safety factor of 1.5. The manual's mounting table calls 3600 Pa positive / 1600 Pa negative the "test load" for the four-outer-plus-four-inner-hole method, and 1600 / 1600 Pa for the other methods.
Quote the product page, and say which page a figure comes from if asked.
