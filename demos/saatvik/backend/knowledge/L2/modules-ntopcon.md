## What N-TOPCon is
- TOPCon (Tunnel Oxide Passivated Contact) adds an ultra-thin tunnel oxide layer and a polysilicon film to an N-type silicon wafer. The passivation cuts electron recombination, lifting average cell conversion efficiency to 25% and module efficiency to 23.69%, with better thermal stability and longevity (/modules/n-topcon).
- Saatvik's cells: N-type TOPCon, average 25% efficiency, average 9.55 Wp per cell, 182.3 × 210 mm wafer (/solar-cell-technology).
- Hot and low light: the −0.30 %/°C power temperature coefficient limits hot-climate losses, and N-type cells keep strong output in early morning, overcast and low-irradiance conditions.
- Compatible with standard racking, trackers and string or central inverters; 1500 V DC system voltage; MC4-compatible connectors.
