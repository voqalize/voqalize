# Mono PERC modules — VEGA G2WB monofacial

Sources: https://www.saatvikgroup.com/modules, https://www.saatvikgroup.com/modules/mono-perc, https://www.saatvikgroup.com/modules/mono-facial-g2wb, https://www.saatvikgroup.com/modules/n-topcon; PDFs /docs/product-documents/saatvik-green-energy-warranty-card.pdf pp 2–3, /docs/datasheets/Saatvik-Mono-PERC-Module-Datasheet.pdf p 2 (read by OCR).

## The line
- Mono PERC monofacial panels, 380–555 Wp, up to 21.48% efficiency, for standard installations (/modules).
- VEGA G2WB is the current monofacial model. Its bifacial sibling, VEGA Plus G2TB, is in topic `modules-bifacial`.
- Saatvik's commercial and ground-mounted EPC projects list 545 Wp, 570 Wp and 575 Wp Mono PERC modules (/epc-solutions/rooftop/commercial, /epc-solutions/ground-mounted).

The Mono PERC datasheet (p 2; its text is drawn as outlines, so it was read by OCR) adds: module efficiency 20.32% to 21.48% across the range; temperature coefficients Pmax −0.36 %/°C, Voc −0.28 %/°C, Isc 0.048 %/°C; max series fuse 25 A; power tolerance 0 to +3%; fire safety Class C, application Class A. Its example model is SGE 550-144 MHC.

## Warranty terms (warranty card)
- 12-year product warranty on half-cut modules.
- 25-year performance warranty for Mono PERC half-cut: at least 98% of nominal power in year 1, declining by no more than 0.55% a year for 24 years, at least 84.8% at the end of year 25.

## Against N-TOPCon
Saatvik's own comparison puts Mono PERC at up to 21.48% efficiency, −0.36 %/°C and 0.55 %/yr degradation against N-TOPCon's 23.69%, −0.30 %/°C and 0.40 %/yr, with a 25-year rather than 30-year performance warranty. See topic `modules-ntopcon`.
