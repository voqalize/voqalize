# The company — group, plants, board, leadership, recognition

Sources: https://www.saatvikgroup.com/about, https://www.saatvikgroup.com/company/our-presence, https://www.saatvikgroup.com/contact, https://www.saatvikgroup.com/company/leadership, the board and committee pages under /investors, https://www.saatvikgroup.com/newsroom/awards, https://www.saatvikgroup.com/careers, the press releases on /newsroom/press-release, /docs/esg/Saatvik-Solar-PV-Take-Back-Program.pdf.

## Who they are
- Saatvik Green Energy Limited (SGEL) makes solar PV modules and, increasingly, the parts upstream and the systems downstream of them. Making modules since 2015, from Ambala, Haryana. Listed on NSE (SAATVIKGL) and BSE (544526); investor detail is topic `investors`.
- 4.8 GW a year of module capacity in Haryana. The Odisha campus at Gopalpur adds cells and modules (below). A 2 GW in-house EPE film (encapsulant) facility at Ambala is commissioned, per a press release and /about; topic `epe-film`.
- Capacity figures differ by page: 4.8 GW across most of the site, 4.86 GW in the ALMM press release, and 4.2 GW for the subsidiary Saatvik Solar Industries alone. Quote 4.8 GW and name the page if pressed.

## Group companies (/about)
- Saatvik Green Energy Limited: the listed parent.
- Saatvik Solar Industries Private Limited: module manufacturing subsidiary (4.2 GW).
- Saatvik Cleantech EPC: the EPC arm, incorporated December 2022, ₹73.5 crore revenue in FY25. Topics `epc-rooftop-residential`, `epc-commercial-industrial`, `epc-utility-floating-carport`.
- Saatvik Green Energy USA Inc.: incorporated in Texas in January 2023, headquartered in Katy, TX; the group's international sales arm (/usa).
- Saatvik Power Storage Solutions: wholly owned, incorporated March 2026, for battery technology and energy storage, with plans for up to 20 GW of storage capacity over five years. Topic `bess`.
- Melcon Transformers: Saatvik acquired an 80% stake in Melcon Transformers and Electricals (April 23, 2026). The subsidiary now trades as Saatvik Transvolt Solutions Limited, converted to a public limited company with effect from September 28, 2026 (exchange filing). Topic `transformers`.
- Intelligent Hydel Solutions: a 49% stake announced February 13, 2026, Saatvik's entry into the IPP business with a 41 MW PPA-backed project in Maharashtra.
- Saatvik Social Foundation: CSR and community work.

## Odisha expansion
- Phase I at Gopalpur: 2.4 GW of cells and 4 GW of modules. Phase II: a 3.6 GW cell facility, MoU signed with the Odisha government (press release, August 17, 2026).
- August 31, 2026: Saatvik applied for ALMM List-II enlistment of the Odisha cell facility, for N-TOPCon G12R bifacial cells under the DCR category, model SS-NTP-210R-16BB-BF.
