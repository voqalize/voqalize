## Who makes them
Melcon Transformers, 80% owned by Saatvik Green Energy since April 2026; the acquisition marked Saatvik's entry into transmission-equipment manufacturing. Melcon has 25+ years of transformer engineering, is ISO 9001 certified (the Melcon site lists ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015 and ISO 45001:2018), and builds to IS, IEC, BIS and international standards. Factory: F-90, Matsya Industrial Area, Alwar, Rajasthan 301030.

- Energy-efficient units are BEE star-rated, low-loss distribution transformers.
- CT/PT sets in 11 kV and 33 kV class.
- Customer-specific designs: auxiliary-use, mining, dual-ratio, servo voltage stabilised, Scott-T, Zig-Zag, NGR-coupled, anti-theft, isolation / ultra-isolation, railway, furnace-duty, converter-duty, heat-tracing, hermetically sealed, low-harmonic.
- Solar: inverter-duty transformers step solar inverter output up to distribution or grid voltage while handling harmonic-rich PV waveforms; designed to each project's vector group, impedance, LV windings and tap-changing needs.
