## Who makes them
Melcon Transformers, 80% owned by Saatvik Green Energy since April 2026; the acquisition marked Saatvik's entry into transmission-equipment manufacturing. Melcon has 25+ years of transformer engineering and is ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015 and ISO 45001:2018 certified; it builds to IS, IEC, BIS and international standards (the catalogue card says "BIS certified"). Factory: F-90, Matsya Industrial Area, Alwar, Rajasthan 301030.

## The range (Melcon's four families)
- **Oil-immersed**: core and windings in dielectric oil for heat dissipation; 5 kVA to 25,000 kVA, up to 33 kV, ONAN / ONAF cooled (the /transformers page adds OFWF), IS 1180 / IS 2026 / IEC 76 compliant.
- **Dry type (VPI)**: air-cooled, epoxy / cast-resin insulation by vacuum pressure impregnation, up to 2,500 kVA, Class F/H, self-extinguishing — for indoor and fire-sensitive sites.
- **Energy-efficient distribution**: BEE star-rated, low-loss distribution transformers that cut no-load and load losses over their life.
- **Special-designed**: Scott-T, heat-tracing, converter-duty, hermetically sealed, furnace, ultra-isolation and low-harmonic units, plus solar-duty transformers and monitoring accessories and panels.

## Who it serves (/melcon-transformers)
- Power generation: solar (inverter-duty units for utility-scale PV), thermal (auxiliary and station-service), hydro (powerhouse and switchyard), wind (pad-mounted step-up for turbine strings).
- Industry, infrastructure and utilities: steel (furnace, converter and rectifier duty), paper, textiles (low-harmonic), oil and minerals (hermetically sealed), mining, real estate (dry-type VPI for occupied buildings), infrastructure (metro, highway, airport), PSUs and utilities. "MELCON designs to the application, not to a catalogue."
- Buyers: private industry, EPC partners, export utilities and industry (8+ countries), government utilities and PSUs.
