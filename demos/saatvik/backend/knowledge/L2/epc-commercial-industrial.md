## Commercial and industrial (C&I)
Factory sheds, warehouses, malls, office parks: metal sheet, RCC or membrane roofs, with tailored mounting and wind-load engineering. The /epc-solutions overview says 50 kW–5 MW for commercial rooftop; the commercial page says "modular designs from 50 kW to multi-MW", with net-metering and open-access pathways.

### The business case (Saatvik's figures)
- Headline tiles: power cost reduction up to 70%; 40% accelerated depreciation in year 1; typical payback (CAPEX) 4 years; 25-year revenue-generating asset life. The text and FAQs give the payback as 3–4 years ("as short as 3–4 years"), depending on tariff, roof type and state policy.
- Solar at ₹2.5–3.5 per unit over the asset life against commercial grid tariffs of ₹8–11 per unit.
- 40% accelerated depreciation in year one under the Income Tax Act for profit-making companies; the FAQ says this often improves IRR "by several percentage points".
- Also: Scope 2 emission cuts, ESG reporting, RE100 or customer sustainability mandates.

### Financing models
- CAPEX ("Own the asset"): the business invests in and owns the plant; full savings, accelerated depreciation, 3–4 year payback, then 20+ years of near-free power. Best for profit-making firms with capital.
- OPEX / RESCO ("Zero investment"): no upfront capital; an investor funds the plant on the roof and the business pays only for power generated, at a tariff below its grid rate; "savings from day one with zero performance risk"; O&M and asset management by the developer. Best for savings without balance-sheet impact.
Saatvik executes both and helps model which suits the balance sheet.

### What is included (the page's five steps)
- 01 Energy Audit & Load Profiling: 12 months of consumption, tariff structure, roof inventory.
- 02 Engineering & Financial Structuring: structural assessment, string design, a bankable financial model with CAPEX vs OPEX, IRR and depreciation schedule.

- 04 Zero-Disruption Installation: around the operating schedule under strict EHS; shutdown limited to a brief pre-agreed window for final grid synchronisation.
- 05 Commissioning, Net Metering & O&M: grid synchronisation, net metering or open-access approvals, remote monitoring dashboards, long-term O&M (module cleaning, preventive maintenance, inverter servicing, remote monitoring, performance reporting; generation guarantees available on long-term contracts).

## Institutional (schools, hospitals, government buildings, non-profits)
The overview also names hotels and multi-block campuses.
- Headline tiles: energy cost reduction up to 50%; "80% daytime load met by solar"; 140 t CO₂ offset per 100 kWp a year; 25-year system design life. The FAQ puts the 80% differently: "up to 80% of solar output is consumed on site", without storage. The two are different claims; quote the FAQ's, and say "up to".
- Who it serves: educational institutions (the plant doubles as a STEM and sustainability "living laboratory"), hospitals and healthcare (never compromising critical loads), government buildings (rooftop solar mandates), non-profits and trusts (OPEX options with no capital outlay). Generation costs locked for 25 years.
- The page's five steps: 01 Campus Energy Assessment (consumption across blocks, wards, hostels, offices; every usable roof; a phased programme); 02 Design for Daytime Loads; 03 Approvals & Scheme Assistance (DISCOM approvals, net metering, applicable central or state schemes); 04 Safe, Scheduled Installation (around academic calendars, hospital operations and public access, with safety cordons); 05 Handover, Training & O&M.
- Government institutions without budget can use OPEX/RESCO. Weekend and holiday surplus is exported through net metering and credited against the bill.
