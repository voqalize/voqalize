# Commercial, industrial and institutional rooftop solar

Sources: https://www.saatvikgroup.com/epc-solutions/rooftop/commercial, https://www.saatvikgroup.com/epc-solutions/rooftop/institutional, https://www.saatvikgroup.com/epc-solutions, https://www.saatvikgroup.com/epc-solutions/ground-mounted.

## Commercial and industrial (C&I)
Factory sheds, warehouses, malls, office parks: metal sheet, RCC or membrane roofs. Modular designs from 50 kW to multi-MW (the /epc-solutions overview says 50 kW–5 MW for commercial rooftop), with net-metering and open-access pathways.

### The business case (Saatvik's figures)
- Power cost reduction up to 70%; solar at ₹2.5–3.5 per unit over the asset life against commercial grid tariffs of ₹8–11 per unit.
- 40% accelerated depreciation in year one under the Income Tax Act for profit-making companies.
- Typical payback 3–4 years under CAPEX, depending on tariff, roof type and state policy; 25-year revenue-generating asset life.
- Also: Scope 2 emission cuts, ESG reporting, RE100 or customer sustainability mandates.

### Financing models
- CAPEX: the business invests in and owns the plant; full savings, accelerated depreciation, 3–4 year payback, then 20+ years of near-free power. Best for profit-making firms with capital.
- OPEX / RESCO: no upfront capital; an investor funds the plant on the roof and the business pays only for power generated, at a tariff below its grid rate; O&M by the developer. Best for savings without balance-sheet impact.
Saatvik executes both and models which suits the balance sheet.

### What is included
Energy audit and load profiling (12 months of consumption, tariff structure, roof inventory); structural assessment, string design and a bankable financial model with CAPEX vs OPEX, IRR and depreciation; modules from Saatvik's 4.8 GW lines (Mono PERC, bifacial, N-TOPCon) with tier-1 inverters and BoS; installation around the operating schedule under strict EHS, with shutdown limited to a pre-agreed window for grid synchronisation; commissioning, net metering or open-access approvals, monitoring dashboards and O&M (module cleaning, preventive maintenance, inverter servicing, remote monitoring, performance reporting; generation guarantees available on long-term contracts).

## Institutional (schools, hospitals, government buildings, non-profits)
- Institutions use most power in daylight, so up to 80% of solar output is used on site without storage; energy cost reduction up to 50%; every 100 kWp offsets around 140 tonnes of CO₂ a year; 25-year design life and performance warranty.
- Delivered as a phased campus programme: energy assessment, design for daytime loads, DISCOM approvals, net metering and help with applicable central or state schemes, installation sequenced around academic calendars and hospital operations with safety cordons, then handover, facilities-team training and O&M.
- Government institutions without budget can use OPEX/RESCO. Weekend and holiday surplus is exported through net metering.
- Reference: Dalmia Cement (Bharat) Ltd, Belagum, Karnataka.
