## SaurJal submersible (/solar-pumps/submersible-dc)
- Models 3, 5, 7.5 and 10 HP DC. The spec table's "Power Range" says 1–10 HP and the stat reads "10 HP maximum rating (from 1 HP)", but every listed model is 3 HP or more.
- Pump body and motor shell in stainless steel SS-304, resistant to dust, sand and abrasives. Water-filled DC motor sealed for life: no oil, no refilling, no servicing. High-efficiency BLDC / PMSM motors. Pump IP68.
- The page says it lifts from borewells up to 150 m deep with flow up to 25 m³/h "depending on the model and head". The datasheet's model table stops at 100 m dynamic head and gives no flow figures; if asked for a head above 100 m, say the page claims up to 150 m and the datasheet lists models to 100 m, and send them to sales.

## SuryaNeer surface (/solar-pumps/surface-dc)
- 3–10 HP mono block: the impeller sits directly on the motor shaft, so no couplings or alignment losses.
- BLDC motor with high-energy rare-earth permanent magnets.
- Build (datasheet p1): polyurethane paint for long life, impact-resistant water canopy, SS shaft, SS impeller, bronze neck ring for high suction lift, high-efficiency casting using no baking process.
- Head up to 30 m, flow up to 18 m³/h. Pump IP54 (the hub table; the product page gives no pump IP).
- In cloud or low light the MPPT keeps the pump running at reduced flow and returns it to full output when the sun recovers.

## The controller (both pumps)
- Fully enclosed IP65, integrated MPPT, designed to IS 16221 Part-2; environment tests IEC 60068-2-6:2007, IEC 60068-2-2:2007, IEC 60068-2-30:2005; efficiency test IEC 61683:1999.
- Protections: open circuit, accidental short circuit (2 minutes max), reverse polarity, under voltage, dry run. SaurJal's page and datasheet p1 add overheating protection; SuryaNeer's do not list it. Multifault diagnosis indications.
- Optional Tank Full and Source Empty sensors for automatic start and stop.
- Remote Monitoring System (RMS): switch the pumpset on or off and watch every parameter from a mobile app or web browser.
