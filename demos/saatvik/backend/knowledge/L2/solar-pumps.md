Which one: a borewell or deep well needs the submersible SaurJal; open wells, ponds, canals, rivers and tanks where the water is near ground level suit the surface SuryaNeer.

## SaurJal submersible (/solar-pumps/submersible-dc)
- Models: 3, 5, 7.5 and 10 HP DC (the spec table also gives a power range of 1–10 HP).
- Pump body and motor shell in stainless steel SS-304, resistant to sand, dust and abrasives. Water-filled DC motor sealed for life: no oil, no refilling, no servicing. High-efficiency BLDC / PMSM motors.
- Lifts from borewells up to 150 m deep; flow up to 25 m³/h depending on model and head.

## SuryaNeer surface (/solar-pumps/surface-dc)
- 3–10 HP mono block: the impeller sits directly on the motor shaft, so no couplings or alignment losses.
- BLDC motor with high-energy rare-earth permanent magnets.
- Head up to 30 m, flow up to 18 m³/h.
- In cloud or low light the MPPT keeps the pump running at reduced flow and returns it to full output when the sun recovers.

## The controller (both pumps)
- Fully enclosed IP65, integrated MPPT, designed to IS 16221 Part-2; environment tests IEC 60068-2-6 / -2-2 / -2-30; efficiency test IEC 61683:1999.
- Protections: open circuit, accidental short circuit (2 minutes max), reverse polarity, under voltage, dry run, and (SaurJal) overheating. Multifault diagnosis indications.
- Optional Tank Full and Source Empty sensors for automatic start and stop.
- Remote Monitoring System (RMS): switch the pumpset on or off and watch every parameter from a mobile app or web browser.
