## What bifacial means
- Monofacial panels take light from the front only; bifacial panels also take light on the rear surface. Saatvik says bifacial technology can deliver up to 30% more energy by harvesting reflected and diffuse light from the ground beneath, which makes it ideal for ground-mounted and tracker systems (/modules FAQ).
- Saatvik says its N-TOPCon and Mono PERC bifacial modules are designed for utility-scale deployment, with high bifacial gain of up to 30%.
- Every Saatvik N-TOPCon module is bifacial glass-to-glass; those are in topic `modules-ntopcon`.
