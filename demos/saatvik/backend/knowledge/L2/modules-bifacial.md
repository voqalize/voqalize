## What bifacial means
- Monofacial panels take light from the front only; bifacial panels also take light on the rear surface. "Bifacial technology can deliver up to 30% more energy by harvesting reflected and diffuse light from the ground beneath the panels, making them ideal for ground-mounted and tracker systems" (/modules FAQ).
- /modules says its N-TOPCon and Mono PERC bifacial modules are designed for utility-scale deployment, "optimised for ground-mounted tracker systems", with bifacial gain of up to 30%.
- Rear gain is not fixed: "Power gain from the rear side depends on the ground reflectance (Albedo) & Bifaciality factor" (every bifacial datasheet). Bifaciality factor is "The ratio of rear efficiency in relation to the front efficiency subject to the same irradiance".
- Every Saatvik N-TOPCon module is bifacial glass-to-glass; those are in topic `modules-ntopcon`.

## Where the pages disagree
- The /modules comparison table lists Mono PERC bifacial with a 30-year performance warranty; the G2TB page, its datasheet and /usa say 25 years. Quote the product page.
- The G2TB booking page (/book?product=bifacial-g2tb) headlines it "GLASS-TO-GLASS BIFACIAL … Bifacial Glass-to-Glass · High Energy Yield · Dual Glass". The product page and datasheet say glass and transparent backsheet. Quote the product page; the G2TB is not a dual-glass module.
- /usa adds: optimised for low-irradiance performance.
