# EPE encapsulant film — SS-EPE UVT-F and SS-EPE UVC-B

Sources: https://www.saatvikgroup.com/epe-film, the EPE plant press releases under https://www.saatvikgroup.com/newsroom/press-release; PDF /docs/datasheets/Saatvik-EPE-Encapsulant-Film-Datasheet.pdf pp 1–4.

## What it is
Encapsulant film for making solar modules, sold to module manufacturers (a B2B product, not for homeowners). An EVA · POE · EVA layered film, highly transparent, with strong adhesion to glass, metal and polymer backsheet, made for PID-free P-type and N-type bifacial modules, and allowing shorter lamination cycles. Saatvik makes it in its own 2 GW EPE film facility (press release).

## The grades
Both share the EVA · POE · EVA structure and roll specification; they differ in ultraviolet transmittance (380–290 nm) and in where they are used.
- SS-EPE UVT-F: ≥80% UV transmittance. For glass-to-glass modules; also used with UVC-B for glass-to-transparent-backsheet modules.
- SS-EPE UVC-B: ≤30% UV transmittance. Used with UVT-F for glass-to-transparent-backsheet modules.

Width tolerance 0/+3 mm; orange-peel or diamond embossed surface; customisable.

## Lamination and storage
- Double-chamber laminator, glass-glass: first step hot plate 110–135 °C, vacuum 360–390 s, press 1–8 min; second step 140–155 °C, press 8–14 min. Tune by measuring gel content (above 75%).
- Store indoors at 0–30 °C, below 60% RH, away from sun, heat and dust. Shelf life 5 months from production. The page says to use the film within 8 hours of opening in one place and within 6 hours in another; quote the shorter.
