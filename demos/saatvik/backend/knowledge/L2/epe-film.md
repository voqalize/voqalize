# EPE encapsulant film — SS-EPE UVT-F and SS-EPE UVC-B

Sources: https://www.saatvikgroup.com/epe-film, the EPE plant press releases under https://www.saatvikgroup.com/newsroom/press-release; PDF /docs/datasheets/Saatvik-EPE-Encapsulant-Film-Datasheet.pdf pp 1–4.

## What it is
Encapsulant film for making solar modules, sold to module manufacturers (a B2B product, not for homeowners; there is no BOOK NOW — enquiries via ENQUIRE NOW at /contact). An EVA · POE · EVA three-layer film, highly transparent, with strong adhesion to glass, metal and polymer backsheet, made for PID-free P-type and N-type bifacial modules, and allowing shorter lamination cycles. "A highly cost-effective encapsulant solution for PID-free requirements." It withstands a 288-hour PID test at 85 °C, 85% RH and 1500 VDC. Saatvik makes it in its own 2 GW EPE film facility (press release; not stated on the page).

Six characteristics the page lists: long-term PID resistance; durability in extreme weather, heat and humidity; adhesion to glass, metal and polymer back sheet; high light transmittance; good handling in lamination with no slipping; strong UV resistance. Product features: high transparency and low haze, high gel content, low WVTR, anti-PID, no bubbling or cell shifting, high volume resistance.

## The grades
Both share the EVA · POE · EVA structure and roll specification; they differ in ultraviolet transmittance (380–290 nm) and in where they are used.
- SS-EPE UVT-F: ≥80% UV transmittance. For glass-to-glass modules; also used with UVC-B for glass-to-transparent-backsheet modules.
- SS-EPE UVC-B: ≤30% UV transmittance. Used with UVT-F for glass-to-transparent-backsheet modules.

## Lamination and storage
- Double-chamber laminator, glass-glass: first step hot plate 110–135 °C, vacuum 360–390 s, press 1–8 min; second step 140–155 °C, vacuum 0–30 s, press 8–14 min. These are reference values: tune by measuring gel content (above 75%; the datasheet's marked area is above 80%).
- Store indoors at 0–30 °C, below 60% RH, away from sun, heat, dust and oxidising materials; do not handle with bare hands, water, oil or solvents. Shelf life 5 months from production. The page and datasheet say to use the film within 8 hours of opening in one place and within 6 hours in another; quote the shorter.
