## Ground-mounted (utility and captive)
- From 1 MW captive plants to utility parks of 100 MW and beyond. Tiles: 100 MW flagship, 545 Wp Saatvik modules deployed, 4 integrated EPC phases, 25-year plant design life.
- Four phases: Engineering (site survey, geotechnical analysis, fixed-tilt and tracker design); Procurement (annual rate contracts with major manufacturers globally, for modules, inverters and BoS); Construction (foundations, structures, cabling, transformers); Commissioning (testing, grid interconnection, monitoring, fine-tuning).
- Strengths the page names: skilled teams across desert, farmland and coastal sites; an in-house design cell (energy yield assessments, PVsyst simulations, grid studies); global ARC procurement; in-house R&D (trackers, bifacial gain, robotic cleaning); "record delivery times on MW-scale plants"; complete balance of system (foundations, cabling, transformers, switchyards, grid interconnection).
- Land: as a rule of thumb 4–5 acres per MW for fixed tilt with modern high-wattage modules.
- Trackers can lift generation 15–20% but add capex and maintenance; Saatvik compares both on yield and LCOE per site.
- O&M: cleaning, vegetation management, preventive and corrective maintenance, spares, 24×7 remote monitoring with performance-ratio guarantees.
- Flagship: 100 MW at the Raghanseda Solar Park, Gujarat (500+ acres, arid terrain, central inverter stations, high-voltage interconnection), on Saatvik-made modules.
- "Glimpses of projects accomplished": Dalmia Cement (Bharat) Ltd, Belagum, Karnataka (545 Wp Mono PERC); Dalmia Bharat Green Vision Ltd, Sattur, Tamil Nadu (545 Wp Mono PERC); Jindal Steel & Power Ltd, Chilhati, Chhattisgarh (545 Wp Mono PERC); Jindal Steel & Power Ltd, Shirpur Dhule, Maharashtra (570/575 Wp Mono PERC).

## Floating solar
- For reservoirs, dams, irrigation tanks, lakes and industrial ponds with fairly stable levels; "from sub-MW industrial ponds to multi-MW reservoir plants".
- Five steps: bathymetric and hydrological survey; platform and mooring design (UV-resistant HDPE floats, corrosion-resistant structures, anchoring for wind, waves and level swings, rated 25+ years); module and electrical engineering (N-TOPCon and Mono PERC from Saatvik's lines, IP-rated combiner boxes, floating walkways, water-crossing cabling); assembly on shore in blocks, then launched, towed and moored; commissioning and water-side O&M including mooring inspections.
- Yield: the tiles say 10% higher yield and the benefits say "up to 10%"; the FAQ says typically 5–10% over an equivalent ground plant. Quote 5–10%.
- Shading cuts evaporation by up to 70% under the covered area and curbs algae; no land consumed; also offered for rural electrification on irrigation tanks and village reservoirs.
- The 100 MW Ramagundam floating project in Telangana (about 500 acres of reservoir) is cited as "landmark reference" and "the benchmark Saatvik engineers to". The page does not say Saatvik built it; never claim it as a Saatvik project.

## Carport solar
- Each standard bay supports roughly 3–3.5 kWp (the tile says 3.5 kWp), so a 100-bay lot hosts about 300–350 kWp. Cantilever, single-slope or dual-pitch galvanised steel structures, engineered to IS wind-load codes, 25-year structural design life.
- Four steps: consultation and site assessment; customised design (with EV-charging and BESS provisions); installation with pre-fabricated steel so parking stays operational (the tile says "100% parking utility retained"; the FAQ says "most of your parking"); maintenance and support.
- EV charging and battery storage (carport + BESS) for commercial parks, airports and institutional campuses: peak shaving (tile: up to 30% peak demand shaving), demand response, backup; tile "24×7 clean power availability".
- Costs more per kWp than rooftop because of the steel, but uses paved land and qualifies for the same incentives, including accelerated depreciation. A cost-benefit model comes during consultation.
- Applications: corporate offices, commercial spaces (malls, business parks), residential projects (homes, housing societies, driveways, stilt parking), public parking.
