# Utility-scale ground-mounted, floating and carport solar

## Ground-mounted (utility and captive)
- From 1 MW captive plants to utility parks of 100 MW and beyond. Integrated engineering, procurement, construction and commissioning: site survey, geotechnical and topographical analysis, fixed-tilt and tracker design, PVsyst yield studies, foundations, structures, cabling, transformers, switchyards, grid interconnection and approvals.
- Procurement through annual rate contracts with major manufacturers, plus Saatvik's own modules; in-house design cell and R&D (trackers, bifacial gain, robotic cleaning).
- Land: as a rule of thumb 4–5 acres per MW for fixed tilt with modern high-wattage modules.
- Trackers can lift generation 15–20% but add capex and maintenance; Saatvik compares both on yield and LCOE per site.
- O&M: cleaning, vegetation management, preventive and corrective maintenance, spares, 24×7 remote monitoring with performance-ratio guarantees.
- Flagship: 100 MW at the Raghanseda Solar Park, Gujarat (500+ acres). Other projects named: Dalmia Cement (Bharat), Belagum, Karnataka (545 Wp Mono PERC); Dalmia Bharat Green Vision, Sattur, Tamil Nadu; Jindal Steel & Power at Chilhati, Chhattisgarh and Shirpur Dhule, Maharashtra (570/575 Wp Mono PERC); the Nabha Power Limited solar plant, 7.50 MWp ground-mounted; Jindal Chilhati mines.

## Floating solar
- For reservoirs, dams, irrigation tanks, lakes and industrial ponds with fairly stable levels. Process: bathymetric and hydrological survey, platform and mooring design (UV-resistant HDPE floats, corrosion-resistant structures, anchoring for wind, waves and level swings), N-TOPCon and Mono PERC modules from Saatvik's lines, assembly on shore in blocks then towed and moored, commissioning and water-side O&M.
- Water cooling typically lifts yield 5–10% over an equivalent ground plant; shading cuts evaporation by up to 70% under the covered area and curbs algae; no land consumed; platforms rated for 25+ years.
- The 100 MW Ramagundam floating project in Telangana (about 500 acres of reservoir) is cited on the page as a landmark reference and "the benchmark Saatvik engineers to". The page does not say Saatvik built it; never claim it as a Saatvik project.

## Carport solar
- Parking bays become generation: each standard bay supports roughly 3–3.5 kWp, so a 100-bay lot hosts about 300–350 kWp. Cantilever, single-slope or dual-pitch galvanised steel structures, engineered to IS wind-load codes, 25-year structural design life.
- EV charging and battery storage can be designed in (peak shaving, demand response, backup). Pre-fabricated structures keep most parking usable during construction.
- Costs more per kWp than rooftop because of the steel, but uses paved land and qualifies for the same incentives, including accelerated depreciation.
- For offices, malls and business parks, housing societies and public parking.
