# Explora — what Javier knows

From www.explora.com (150 English pages plus every lodge sub-page and 319 exploration pages, crawled 2026-10) and reservations.explora.com. Every fact is the site's own; where pages disagree, the topics say which page says what. Booking-site rates change by date: never quote a nightly rate without looking it up.

## What Explora is
"World's Leading Expedition Company" (World Travel Awards, 2019–2025). All-inclusive lodges and small-group expeditions in remote South America, plus Iceland — "the Luxury of the Essential". Founded by friends led by Pedro Ibáñez; first lodge Explora Patagonia (Torres del Paine), 1993. Purpose: "To explore remote territories as a journey to conservation." B Corp; two own conservation reserves (Torres del Paine, Puritama). Regions: Patagonia (Argentina + Chile), Sacred Valley of the Incas (Peru), Desert & Altiplano (Bolivia + Chile), Easter Island (Chile).

## Lodges (booking code · page)
- **Atacama**, near San Pedro de Atacama, Chile (EATA · /lodge/atacama-lodge/): 50 rooms, observatory, own stables, four pools, Puritama hot springs; "nearly 50" explorations.
- **Travesía Atacama–Uyuni**, Chile↔Bolivia (PATA Chile→Bolivia, PUYU Bolivia→Chile · /lodge/atacama-uyuni-crossing/): nine-day 4×4 crossing of the Altiplano to the Uyuni salt flat; Explora Atacama, then mountain lodges Ramaditas, Chituca, Jirira.
- **Rapa Nui**, Easter Island (ERAP · /lodge/easter-island-hotels/): 30 rooms outside Hanga Roa, 15 min from the airport; moai, Rano Raraku, kayak and bike.
- **Valle Sagrado**, Sacred Valley, Peru (EVAL · /lodge/travel-machupicchu-and-the-sacred-valley-of-the-incas-peru-explora/): 50 rooms near Urquillos at 2,900 m, Pumacahua Bath House; 40+ explorations; Machu Picchu extra (not on two-night stays). Pages disagree on whether alcohol is included.
- **Torres del Paine**, in the national park, Chile (EPAT · /lodge/torres-del-paine-lodge/): the original lodge, 50 rooms on Lake Pehoé facing the Paine Massif; 40+ explorations in six zones; open year-round.
- **Parque Nacional Patagonia**, Chacabuco Valley, Aysén, Chile (EPNP · /lodge/patagonia-national-park/): 13 rooms (2021), the only lodge inside the Tompkins' park; huemul, ice-field flights.
- **El Chaltén**, Argentina (ECHA · /el-chalten-lodge/; many links use the A/B copy /el-chalten-lodge-ab/): 20 rooms in the Los Huemules reserve, 17 km from town, facing the Marconi Glacier; Fitz Roy treks.
- **New — "Opening Dec 2026"** on the booking site: **Torres del Paine Conservation Reserve** (ERTP · /lodge/torres-del-paine-conservation-reserve/) — 10 rooms on Explora's private reserve at the park's northeast edge, gauchos and pumas, ~2 h from Puerto Natales; **El Calafate** (ECFT · /lodge/el-calafate/) — 20 rooms on the Chorrillo Malo estancia (1906), Perito Moreno Glacier, ~1 h from the airport.

## Included in every lodge stay
Accommodations, all explorations, all meals and beverages, spa facilities, park entrance fees, round-trip airport transfers at set times. Extra: flights, private transfers, spa treatments, private guiding, laundry, tips, third-party extras (Machu Picchu).

## Explorations
"More than 30" half- and full-day explorations per lodge, included (the crawl found 24–52 per lodge). Chosen each afternoon with your guide (two half-days or one full day) by interest, fitness and weather; groups of no more than eight; bilingual guides from Explora's School of Guides. Levels Easy / Moderate / Advanced / Expert; kinds Classic, Signature (Explora-only), Advanced. All optional.

## How booking works
- **Lodges and Travesía**: reservations.explora.com (every CHECK AVAILABILITY opens it in a new tab, no lodge chosen). Step 1: lodge → arrival date on the calendar (only available days clickable) → nights (each with a "from" nightly price) → Continue. Step 2: rooms (1 or 2 travelers, King/Twin) → Add this room. Step 3: contact details and payment, done by the visitor. `/ibe/paso1?hotel_code=CODE` preselects a lodge.
- **Expeditions**: choosing one opens an enquiry form (`/ibe/expeditions?expedition_request=CODE`): name, email, phone, nationality, message, an "I'm not a robot" tick the visitor does, SEND MESSAGE. Or reserveexpeditions@explora.com.
- **Terms**: lodges up to 30% deposit, balance 30 days out, free cancellation to 30 days; Travesía 60 days; expeditions 120 days plus a non-refundable USD 500/1,000 pp fee; holidays 50% and 60 days.
