# Explora Atacama (San Pedro de Atacama, Chile)

Sources: /lodge/atacama-lodge/; /atacama-desert-explora-lodge/; /atacama-explorations/; /explorations-lodge/atacama/; /atacama-getting-there/; /atacama-travel-tips/; /frequently-asked-questions-atacama/; /atacama-desert-hotels/; /atacama-desert-tours/; /atacama-desert/; /puritama-conservation-reserve-home-to-the-andean-cat/; /new-puritama/; /uyuni-lodge/; /2026/07/10/atacama-and-torres-del-paine-two-sides-of-chile/; /2026/09/17/how-to-sequence-explora-lodges-south-america-journey/; reservations.explora.com.

## The lodge
- **50 rooms** on "a pristine 42-acre (17-hectare) desert estate" on the Ayllu de Larache, near San Pedro de Atacama; destination group **Desert & Altiplano** (/atacama-desert/).
- Known for: four pools and a spa, an onsite **Observatory**, Explora's own **horse stables**, and Explora-only access to the **Puritama Conservation Reserve** hot springs.
- Architect **José Cruz Ovalle**. Spa in an adobe house (treatments, sauna, steam baths, outdoor hot tubs); four interconnected pools, one warmed to 77–82°F (25–28°C).
- Awards: Travelers' Choice 2025 Best of the Best (All Inclusive Hotels in South America); Condé Nast Traveler Readers' Choice 2022; World Travel Awards 2020 Chile's Leading Boutique Hotel. Badge "5.0 Excellent 1,097 reviews".
- Included: accommodations, all explorations, all meals and beverages, spa, national park fees, round-trip airport transfers.
- Booking code **EATA** ("Atacama, Chile"; calendar → nights → rooms). Also the start or end of the Travesía (PATA / PUYU; topic `uyuni-travesia`).

## Explorations
- **49** guided Explorations (count varies; see disagreements), half- and full-day, **groups of no more than eight**, bilingual (Spanish and English) guides from the School of Guides. Classic and Signature Explora-only routes; your guide plans the next day with you each evening. **Private Explorations cost extra**, arranged in advance.
- Activities: Hike, Overland, Bike, Horseback Ride, E-Bike, Sky, High Mountain Ascent. Difficulty: Easy, Moderate, Advanced, Expert.
- Zones: **Cuenca del salar** (salt flat, 2350 m.a.s.l.), **Quebradas Intermedias** (ravines, Puritama hot springs), **Altiplano** (3800–4300 m.a.s.l., geysers), **Montaña** (summits such as Licancabur, over 5000 m.a.s.l.; needs stamina and preparation), **Cielo** (night skies).
- Examples (/explorations-lodge/atacama/, 4 pages): Lari, Selenita (horseback, easy); Caspana (e-bike, advanced, full-day); Vilama (e-bike, expert); Cejar (lagoon float, easy; not on Tuesdays); Camino del Agua (through water; bring two pairs of shoes); Ronque (moderate, full-day, Puritama hot springs); Domeyko (not for vertigo); Ckausama (Tatio Geysers, full-day).
- Signature (2026 blog): El Tatio at dawn, Valle de la Luna at dusk, the observatory after dark; guides start low and climb day by day.
- Free equipment: trekking poles, helmets, riding gaiters, bike and horse gear, water bottle.

## Dining
An "authentic, handcrafted daily menu" of Atacama flavors; "local cuisine paired with bold Chilean wine" (/uyuni-lodge/). No reservations needed. Vegetarian/vegan options; tell reservations about dietary needs before arrival. /gastronomy-in-atacama/ was not crawled.

## Getting there (/atacama-getting-there/)
- Santiago (SCL), then 2 hours to **Calama (CJC)**; about 1 h by Explora van (Calama is 98 km / 61 mi away).
- Included transfers: **from Calama Airport 12:00, 14:00, 16:00, 18:00; to it 08:00, 10:00, 12:00** (±30 minutes). Hotel or bus-terminal pick-up in San Pedro or Calama is set the day before (about 10:00–11:00). Off-schedule service 9:00 am–7:00 pm costs extra per traveler, minimum 2.
- Explora's own vans, max 6 travelers. Sico or Jama border pick-up (Chilean side) costs extra (reserve@explora.com). San Pedro's strip takes small planes only (max 8,000 kg), just before sunset.
- **Check-in 13:00; check-out 10:00** (luggage held for a half-day exploration; no lunch on check-out day).

## Climate and best time
Year-round; "ninety percent of the days are clear and sunny". Winter -10°C (14°F) night to 20°C (68°F) day; summer 10°C (50°F) to 30°C (86°F). Spring and fall mild, fewer crowds. In the Altiplano Winter some explorations may change or be cancelled.

## Altitude
Lodge: **2,500 meters (8,202 feet)** (FAQ). See disagreements for other figures.

## Travel tips
- Pack four layers (thermal, fleece, warm jacket, waterproof jacket), sun hat, hiking shoes, gloves, sunglasses, sunscreen, buff, 25-liter backpack; optional swimsuit.
- Wi-Fi intermittent; 220V; good mobile coverage.
- No ATM or exchange at the lodge (both in San Pedro); extras by credit card only.
- Tips voluntary, at reception (amount: see disagreements). No hairdresser.
- Medical: clinic in San Pedro, Calama hospital one hour away; guides are WFR certified. Relatives' emergency email: recatacama@explora.com.
- Stay at least five nights; six or more for the high plateau or a volcano. Book 60 days ahead; 6 months for peak periods.
- Uyuni only via the Travesía, booked before arrival, never from the lodge.

## Puritama Conservation Reserve
60 km north of San Pedro along the Puritama River, with the hot springs; the endangered Andean cat is monitored there since 2010 with 13 cameras (see topic `conservation-reserves`).

## Where the site disagrees with itself
- **Number of explorations:** "nearly 50" (overview), "almost 50" (travel-tips FAQ), "49 guided Explorations" and "Explore 52 explorations" on the same /atacama-explorations/ page; home says "more than 30" per lodge.
- **Zones:** /atacama-explorations/ says "five unique zones" and lists 5, but the same page also says "four unique Andean zones".
- **Altitude:** lodge 2,500 m / 8,202 ft (FAQ, /atacama-desert/); San Pedro village 2,443 m / 8,015 ft (FAQ climate answer); Explora Atacama "8,000 feet (2,438 meters)" (/uyuni-lodge/).
- **Tip amount:** "USD 100 per day per room" (/atacama-travel-tips/) vs "$100 per stay per visitor" (FAQ).
- **Puritama size:** 6,000 hectares (Andean-cat page), 7,000 hectares (/new-puritama/), 8000 hectares (/atacama-desert/).
- **Winter months:** June–August (travel tips) vs June–September (FAQ). **Altiplano Winter:** mid-December to early March (FAQ), January–March (2026 blog); travel tips put the higher rain chance in December–February.
- **Minimum stay:** "at least five nights" (FAQ, travel tips) vs "4 nights minimum, 5 for more room" (2026 blog).
- **Beds:** booking FAQ says "king size bed or two double beds"; room pages say 1 King or 2 twin beds.
- **Yali Room views:** gardens (/atacama-desert-explora-lodge/) vs hotel's grounds (/atacama-desert-hotels/).
