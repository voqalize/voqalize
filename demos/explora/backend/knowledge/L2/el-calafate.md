## The lodge
- A remote lodge in the historic **Chorrillo Malo estancia** (founded **1906**, per the La Meseta exploration) on the Patagonian steppe, with views of the pampas, the Andes and "a distant glimpse of the Perito Moreno Glacier". "Unlike hotels in the town of El Calafate", it is a remote all-inclusive lodge.
- Designed by Chilean architect **José Cruz Ovalle**: natural materials, organic forms, expansive windows.
- **20 rooms**: Standard (pampa and mountain views) and Suites. No sizes or beds in the crawl.
- **Spa**: outdoor hot tubs.
- Overview badges: The Gold List 2025; World's Leading Expedition Company 2025 (company award); Conservation Sustainability with Soul.
- Included: accommodations, all explorations, all meals and drinks, spa, park entrance fees, round-trip airport transfers on preset schedules. **Not included**: flights, optional private transfers, personal expenses.
- Part of the **Patagonia Network** of six destinations (Patagonia National Park, Torres del Paine, Torres del Paine Reserve, El Calafate, El Chaltén, Tierra del Fuego expedition).

## Explorations
- **More than 30** on foot, horseback, boat or overland vehicle, for all levels; bilingual School of Guides guides; groups **limited to eight**.
- **Five zones**: **Hielos Milenarios** (Perito Moreno, "one of the few advancing glaciers on Earth", by boat and on foot); **Brazo Sur** (the most remote corner of Los Glaciares National Park); **Valles y Montañas** (forest hikes, viewpoints); **Pampa** (steppe and ranching history); **La Estancia** (horseback and hikes on the estancia).
- Activities: Hike, Overland, Horseback Ride, By Boat, Mountain Ascent. Difficulty Easy, Moderate, Advanced, Expert.
- Examples:
  - Cara Sur (boat on the Rico River to the glacier's south face, Easy, half-day).
  - Perito Moreno (boat, shore walk, walkways; listed "Hike", Easy, half-day).
  - Boardwalks ("the most accessible exploration", Easy, half-day).
  - Península (mountain ascent, Advanced, full-day).
  - Cerro Cristal (summit hike, Advanced, full-day); Mirador Cristal (Moderate, half-day).
  - Calafate (Laguna Nimez birds, Easy, half-day); El Chingue (Moderate, full-day).
  - La Tranquera (horseback, Easy, half-day); La Meseta, La Condorera (Moderate, half-day); El Fraile (Advanced, full-day).
- Explora provides trekking poles, horseback helmets and riding gaiters, and a bottle of water.

## Dining
Patagonian lamb, trout, native herbs, seasonal produce, "prepared with simplicity", with Patagonian wines; all included. Page /dining-el-calafate/ (not crawled).

## Getting here
- Fly to **El Calafate Airport (FTE)**, about **1 hour** from the lodge; regular flights from Buenos Aires.
- **Pick-up**: airport **12:00 p.m. and 4:30 pm**; El Calafate hotels **12:30 p.m. and 5:00 pm**. Confirmed by reservations or the lodge; the driver waits **at most 15 minutes**.
- **Drop-off**: leaves the lodge **10:00 am**; airport about 11:30 am, hotels about 12:00 pm.
- **From Explora El Chaltén**: van on select days **October–April**, about **3.5 hours**.
- **From Torres del Paine** (national park lodge or Conservation Reserve): preset-schedule transfers, about **six hours**, crossing the Chile–Argentina border.
- Private transfers at extra cost, booked in advance via your reservation executive or reserve@explora.com.

## Best time and climate
"El Calafate can be visited year-round."
- **Summer (December–February)**: long days, rarely above 20 °C (68 °F), strong wind; best for glacier and hiking.
- **Autumn (March–May)**: ochre and copper, quieter.
- **Winter (June–August)**: snow, silence. Bring a headlamp May–September.
- **Spring (September–November)**: wildflowers; guanacos, condors and flamingos return to Lago Argentino.
