## The lodge
- A remote lodge in the historic **Chorrillo Malo estancia** (founded **1906**, per the La Meseta exploration) on the Patagonian steppe, with views of the pampas, the Andes and "a distant glimpse of the Perito Moreno Glacier". "Unlike hotels in the town of El Calafate", it is a remote all-inclusive lodge.
- Designed by Chilean architect **José Cruz Ovalle**: natural materials, organic forms, expansive windows.
- About **19 miles (30 km) from the town of El Calafate**, near Lake Argentino (LODGE tab).
- **20 rooms** (LODGE tab, /where-to-stay-in-el-calafate/):
  - **Standard**: "Pampa and the Mountains" views; size shown only as "28" (no unit); sleeps 2; 1 king or 2 twin beds; a desk.
  - **Suite**: same views; 42 m² (450 ft²); sleeps 2; 1 king bed.
  - Both: heating, hair dryer, non-smoking; extra bed for children and crib (marked * — subject to availability). The split between Standard rooms and Suites is not stated.
- **Spa**: outdoor hot tubs.
- Overview badges: The Gold List 2025; World's Leading Expedition Company 2025 (company award); Conservation Sustainability with Soul.
- Included: accommodations, all explorations, all meals and drinks, spa, park entrance fees, round-trip airport transfers on preset schedules. **Not included**: flights, optional private transfers, personal expenses.
- Part of the **Patagonia Network** of six destinations (Patagonia National Park, Torres del Paine, Torres del Paine Reserve, El Calafate, El Chaltén, Tierra del Fuego expedition).

## Explorations
- "30+ explorations included" (EXPLORATIONS tab, FAQ), but the same tab says "Explore 24 explorations located in the 5 unique El Calafate zones" and "Stay tuned! Soon, you'll be able to discover the full range"; **24** detail pages. Bilingual School of Guides guides; each evening a guide tailors the next day; groups of no more than eight.
- **Five zones**: Hielos Milenarios (4; Perito Moreno, "one of the few advancing glaciers on Earth"; "Access is exclusively by vessel"); Valles y Montañas (2; Los Glaciares National Park and the Magallanes Peninsula; views to Fitz Roy and Torres del Paine on clear days); Pampa (5; steppe to the Río La Leona, ranching history); La Estancia (12; horseback and hikes on Chorrillo Malo); Brazo Sur (1; the most remote corner of Los Glaciares National Park).
- **By activity**: Hike 12, Horseback Ride 6, Overland 3, Mountain Ascent 2, By Boat 1. **By difficulty**: Easy 11, Moderate 8, Advanced 5 (no Expert). **Duration**: 16 half-day, 8 full-day.
- Examples:
  - Glacier: Cara Sur (boat on the Rico River to the south face, Easy); Perito Moreno (sail the Rico Arm, then walk the south shore; listed "Hike", Easy); Boardwalks ("the most accessible exploration", walkways and viewpoints, Easy); Península (ascent of the Magallanes Peninsula, 11.5 km, gain 1,111 m, Advanced).
  - Horseback: Chorrillo Malo (3.7 km), Pastizales, La Tranquera (Easy); Rio Rico, Las Faldas (Moderate); Veranadas (around Cerro El Fraile, 29 km, Advanced, full-day).
  - Hikes: Erraticas and Los Toros (Easy); La Meseta, La Condorera, Mirador Cristal (Moderate); Brazo Sur and La Frontera (toward the Chilean border, 16 km) (Moderate, full-day); Cerro Cristal (9.6 km, gain 1,043 m, Advanced); El Fraile (Advanced).
  - Overland: Calafate (Laguna Nimez bird sanctuary, Easy); Walichu (Punta Walichu rock paintings, Easy); Cerro Moyano (mountain ascent, Advanced).
- Explora provides trekking poles, horseback helmets and riding gaiters, and a bottle of water.

## Dining (/dining-el-calafate/)
Evenings "turn fireside": an open-fire kitchen rooted in Argentine cooking. A fireside parrilla anchors the dining room — plancha-seared trout, roasted vegetables, classic provoleta and, "for those who wish", asado argentino. Breakfast, lunch and dinner, trail meals on long days and beverages through the day are included; Patagonian wines (tips FAQ). "Vegetarian, vegan, and other preferences are welcomed."

## Getting here
- Fly to **El Calafate Airport (FTE)**, about **1 hour** from the lodge; regular flights from Buenos Aires.
- **Pick-up**: airport **12:00 p.m. and 4:30 pm**; El Calafate hotels **12:30 p.m. and 5:00 pm**. Confirmed by reservations or the lodge; the driver waits **at most 15 minutes**.
- **Drop-off**: leaves the lodge **10:00 am**; airport about 11:30 am, hotels about 12:00 pm.
- **From Explora El Chaltén**: van on select days **October–April**, about **3.5 hours**.
- **From Torres del Paine** (national park lodge or Conservation Reserve): preset-schedule transfers, about **six hours**, crossing the Chile–Argentina border.
- Private transfers at extra cost, booked in advance via your reservation executive or reserve@explora.com.

## Best time and climate
"El Calafate can be visited year-round."
- **Summer (December–February)**: long days, rarely above 20 °C (68 °F), strong wind; best for glacier and hiking.
- **Autumn (March–May)**: ochre and copper, quieter.
- **Winter (June–August)**: snow, silence. Bring a headlamp May–September.
- **Spring (September–November)**: wildflowers; guanacos, condors and flamingos return to Lago Argentino.
