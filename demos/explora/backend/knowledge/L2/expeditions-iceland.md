# Explora Expeditions in Iceland: Highlands (summer) and Winter

Sources: /explora-expeditions/; /iceland-expedition-overview/, /iceland-expedition-itinerary/, /iceland-expedition-departure-dates/, /what-to-bring-iceland/, /iceland-expedition-frequently-asked-questions/; /explora-expeditions/iceland-winter-expedition/, /iceland-winter-expedition-itinerary/, /iceland-winter-departure-dates/, /what-to-bring-iceland-winter-expedition/, /iceland-winter-questions/; /expeditions-call-out-homepage/; /2026/06/03/from-the-arctic-to-the-andes-…/ (2027-season post); /payment-and-cancellation-policies/; reservations.explora.com.

**All expeditions:**
- **Group size:** small groups of no more than 8 travelers. Private departures are available.
- **How to book:** through an **enquiry form**, not the calendar. Use reservations.explora.com/ibe/expeditions?expedition_request=CODE (the visitor ticks the reCAPTCHA themselves), or write to reserveexpeditions@explora.com, or use "Contact our experts" (/contact-expedition-expert).
- **Payment** (/payment-and-cancellation-policies/): a deposit of up to 30%, which includes a non-refundable Reservation Fee of **USD 1,000 per person for Iceland** (USD 500 for Tierra del Fuego and Sacred Mountains). The balance is due 120 days before check-in.
- **Changes and cancellation:** free 120+ days before, a 50% fine at 119–60 days and 100% at 59–0 days. The Reservation Fee is never refunded.

The Peru and Chile expeditions are in topic `expeditions-south-america`.

Each expedition has sub-pages OVERVIEW · ITINERARY · DEPARTURE DATES · WHAT TO BRING · FAQ · ALL EXPEDITIONS. Explorer Echo Society members get special savings; the Winter FAQ says 10%.

## Iceland Expedition: Venturing Across the Untouched Highlands (XICE)
- **The trip:** 8 days / 7 nights, from USD $22,050 per person on double occupancy, limited to 8 travelers. It starts and ends in Reykjavik. The overview says "Suitable for all travelers"; the itinerary text says it is "designed for those with a moderate level of physical fitness".
- **Route:** the Southern Highlands, with nights in boutique hotels, guest houses and Reykjavik hotels. Travel is by 4×4 Super Jeeps.

**Itinerary** (/iceland-expedition-itinerary/):
- D1 Check in at Hotel Borg, Reykjavik (or equivalent), aperitif and briefing.
- D2 Thorsmork Valley (2.5 h drive). Hotel Skálakot.
- D3 Southern Highlands (Tindfjall, Maelifell, Myrdalsjokull), with an optional challenging 10 km (6.2 mi) hike to the Strútslaug Hot Springs. Hotel Arctic Exclusive Ranch.
- D4 Vatnajökull: canyon hikes or walking on the ice. Hotel Arctic Exclusive Ranch.
- D5 Lakagígar, Langisjór and Fjallabak. Highlands Center Hotel.
- D6 Landmannalaugar, the hardest hikes: four options from 2.5 km to 20 km (1.5 to 12.4 mi). Highlands Center Hotel.
- D7 Craters, Thjorsardalur waterfalls, Sky Lagoon, final dinner. Hotel Borg.
- D8 Check-out 10:00 am and transfer to the airport.

**Dates** (/iceland-expedition-departure-dates/):
- 2026: July 8–15, July 20–27, Aug 21–28 and Sept 1–8 are all Sold Out.
- 2027: July 9–16, July 19–26, Aug 7–14, Aug 17–24, Sept 4–11 and Sept 14–21 are all Available.

**What to bring:** four layers (base, fleece, down or synthetic jacket, waterproof shell), hats, thermal gloves, trekking clothes and shoes, a 25 L pack with rain cover, a headlamp, sunscreen, a 1 L bottle, a swimsuit and shower sandals. Optional: a Type C or F adapter, a battery pack and cash. Explora provides poles and a bottle.

**FAQ:**
- **Included:** the Explora team (team leader and expert guides), all transfers, all accommodation and meals, guided explorations and entrance fees.
- **Not included:** gratuities, massages and extra services, and private services. This is a group experience.
- **Arrival:** arrive at Reykjavík Airport on day 1. The team picks you up, and check-in begins at 3:00 PM.
- **Connectivity:** at the accommodations, but not on some remote days.
- **Luggage:** at most 1 suitcase, up to 20 kg.
- **Fitness:** it depends on the activities you choose; "there are options for all fitness levels".
- **Food:** vegan and other diets if you ask in advance.
- **Safety:** first-aid-certified guides, and team members in the Icelandic Association for Search and Rescue. Bring your own medications.

## Iceland Winter Expedition (XICW)
- **The trip:** 7 days / 6 nights, from $17,900 USD per person in a double room, limited to 8 travelers, "Suitable for all travelers". It starts and ends in Reykjavík. The 2027-season post calls it Explora's first winter itinerary in Iceland and "the newest of the four" expeditions.
- **Route:** four territories (Golden Circle, Eyjafjallajökull, Dyrhólaey, Vatnajökull).

**Itinerary** (/iceland-winter-expedition-itinerary/):
- D1 Reykjavik, Hotel Borg (or equivalent), group aperitif.
- D2 Golden Circle (Þingvellir rift, steam vents, waterfalls). Hotel Skálakot.
- D3 Eyjafjallajökull by snowmobile, with snowshoes toward the summit if conditions allow. Hotel Skálakot.
- D4 Dyrhólaey, Reynisfjara and Jökulsárlón Glacier Lagoon. Hotel Jökulsárlón.
- D5 Vatnajökull ice walk with crampons and ice axe, then the hotel's outdoor hot tubs and sauna. Hotel Jökulsárlón.
- D6 South coast and Skógafoss, then Sky Lagoon (38°C water). Hotel Borg (or equivalent).
- D7 Check-out before noon; the transfer to Keflavík Airport at noon is included.
- The 2027 post also mentions a morning at the interactive Lava Center.

**Included** (overview): selected accommodations, meals and beverages, all explorations, all entrances and Sky Lagoon. The FAQ adds equipment (crampons, ice axes, snowshoes), all transport during the expedition and airport transfers.

**Dates** (/iceland-winter-departure-dates/): Dec 5–11, 2026; Feb 22–28, 2027; Mar 3–9, 2027. All are Available.

**Climate** (/what-to-bring-iceland-winter-expedition/):

| | December | February | March |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daylight | ~4–5 h | ~7–8 h | ~9–10 h |
| Average temperature | –1 to 3°C (30 to 37°F) | –2 to 2°C (28 to 36°F) | –1 to 4°C (30 to 39°F) |

Minimum temperatures go down to about –5°C. In extreme cases it can reach –20°C (–4°F) between December and March.

**What to bring:** four layers plus waterproof over-pants, waterproof trekking boots with grip for snow and ice, a warm hat, waterproof gloves plus a spare pair, a 25 L pack, a headlamp, SPF 50, an insulated 1 L bottle and a swimsuit. Recommended: an adapter, a battery pack (cold drains batteries), hand warmers and SPF lip balm. Explora provides poles, a bottle and safety equipment when needed.

**FAQ** (/iceland-winter-questions/):
- **Why winter:** crampon glacier hikes, snowmobiling and the Northern Lights, none of which summer offers.
- **Fitness:** suitable for all kinds of travelers. The group can split into two subgroups, and no mountaineering experience is needed.
- **Age:** 13 and older is recommended; exceptions are considered case by case.
- **Daylight and weather:** explorations fit the 4–5 hours of daylight, and the plan adapts to cold and wind. A packing list is sent before departure.
- **Guides:** Explora's team, plus local drivers and specialist guides, for example for the glacier hike.
- **Transfers and start:** pickup from Keflavík (KEF) or Reykjavík Domestic (RKV) to suit your arrival. Check-in is from 3:00 PM, and the expedition begins at 5:30 PM at the hotel.
- **Luggage:** one suitcase (up to 20 kg / 44 lbs) and a daypack.
- **Solo travelers:** welcome, with a single supplement on request.
- **Private groups:** dates must be coordinated in advance through reserveexpeditions@explora.com.

## Where the site disagrees with itself
- **2027 summer dates:** the departure page lists six (July 9–16 through Sept 14–21). The 2027-season post lists seven: June 18–25; June 28–July 5; July 9–16; July 19–26; Aug 7–14; Aug 17–24; Sept 4–11. It has no Sept 14–21 date and adds the two June dates. Quote the departure page and suggest confirming by enquiry.
- **Summer fitness:** "Suitable for all travelers" and "options for all fitness levels" against "designed for those with a moderate level of physical fitness" (overview, itinerary summary), and the hub's "moderate or advanced physical challenge".
- **Names:** "Iceland Expedition: Venturing Across the Untouched Highlands" (overview), "The Iceland's Highlands Expedition" (home page) and "Iceland Summer Expedition" (Winter FAQ, 2027 post) are the same trip.
- **Stale page:** /expeditions-call-out-homepage/ still shows 2025 dates.
- **Wrong-language link:** the Winter overview's "View all departures" goes to the Spanish /es/iceland-winter-expedition-departure-dates/.
