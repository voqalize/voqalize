# Explorations: how they work

Sources: https://www.explora.com/ (section "Our Explorations: Discover More with the Best Experts"); /2025/12/02/how-do-we-explore/ (fetched live 2026-10-21; linked from the home page "HOW WE EXPLORE"); /explora-hotels/ (tabs); /torres-del-paine-tours/; /2026/08/27/solo-travel-set-your-own-pace/; the exploration archives /explorations-lodge/atacama/, /explorations-lodge/atacama-uyuni/, /explorations-lodge/easter-island/, /explorations-lodge/machu-picchu-sacred-valley/, /explorations-lodge/torres-del-paine/, /explorations-lodge/reserva-torres-del-paine/, /explorations-lodge/patagonia-national-park/, /explorations-lodge/el-chalten/, /explorations-lodge/explorations-el-calafate/; lodge overviews and FAQ pages; /benefits-categories/.

## The basics
- **Included**: "we offer more than 30 curated half- and full-day explorations at each of our lodges, all included with your stay" (home). Privately guided explorations cost extra.
- **Chosen day by day with your guide**: "Each afternoon, you meet with them to review options for the next day according to your interests and activity levels" (/2025/12/02/how-do-we-explore/). Torres del Paine says the guide meets you "every evening". The solo post: "it could be two half-day Explorations or one full day out, depending on your physical condition, the weather, and how you're feeling."
- **Small groups**: "no more than eight guests" per exploration; group size varies day to day.
- **Optional**: "every Exploration is optional, and the pools and spa stay open on your own schedule" (solo post FAQ).
- **Guides**: trained at Explora's own **School of Guides**; bilingual (also "multilingual" on El Chaltén's page); grounded in geology, ecology, wildlife and local culture, "alongside extensive safety and first-aid training".
- **Weather decides**: in Patagonia "a day of low cloud or strong wind may shift the plan, and your guide adjusts the Explorations accordingly"; in Atacama explorations are sequenced to follow the rise in altitude, low first and higher later.
- **Echo Society** Discoverer tier and above may plan all explorations for the whole stay on arrival day (normally it is day by day).

## Kinds of exploration (/2025/12/02/how-do-we-explore/)
- **Classic Explorations** — iconic places "seen with new eyes": avoiding crowds, alternate timings, less-travelled approaches.
- **Signature Explorations** — "designed and operated exclusively by Explora", on little-travelled routes "that no one else offers".
- **Advanced Explorations** — for experienced hikers: longer distances, significant elevation gain, high-altitude hikes, volcanic slopes or glacier viewpoints, "carefully assessed according to weather and guest experience".

## Difficulty and duration (archive filters)
- **Difficulty**: Easy, Moderate, Advanced, Expert (the filter also shows Spanish and French duplicates: Fácil, Moderado, Avanzado, Facile, Moyen).
- **Duration**: Half-day or Full-day on every card.
- **Each archive filters by** Areas (zones), Activity and Difficulty; each card shows name, zone, activity, difficulty, duration, one line and "VIEW DETAILS" (/explorations/<name>/). Archives run to 2–4 pages.

## Gear
Walking sticks and bike equipment are provided without extra cost (Patagonia National Park FAQ; corporate program list includes horse and bicycle equipment and trekking sticks).

## Departure day
At most lodges a half-day exploration is possible on check-out day by checking out at 07:00 (Patagonia National Park, Sacred Valley, Easter Island) or before the exploration (Atacama, Torres del Paine); El Chaltén's 08:00 shared transfer leaves no time for one unless a private transfer is booked.
