# Special offers, loyalty and programs

Sources: https://www.explora.com/offer/ (archive "SPECIAL OFFERS"); /offer/10-for-2026-bookings/; /offer/childrens-benefit-at-explora/; /offer/stay-longer-and-save-30/; /offer/solo-travel-benefit/; /offer/honeymoon-benefit/; /explora-passport/; /offer/explorer-echo-society/; /what-is-explorer-echo-society/, /benefits-categories/, /join-the-explorer-echo-society/ (completed from the live pages 2026-10-21); /program/ ("UNIQUE PROGRAMS"); /program/explora-connects-patagonia/; /program/photography-program-with-katsuyoshi-tanaka/; /corporate-travel-program/.

Every offer is "subject to availability", applies to the all-inclusive rate shown on the booking site, and is booked online ("BOOK NOW" → reservations.explora.com) or via reserve@explora.com. Offers are generally not combinable with each other; Echo Society members often get an extra 5%. None of the offers below applies to the expeditions, and most exclude the Travesía Atacama–Uyuni.

## Offers (/offer/)
**10% For 2026 Bookings** (/offer/10-for-2026-bookings/) — "We rarely reduce our rates." Special rates at **Explora Torres del Paine and Explora Atacama** for travel **November 8–14, November 18–24 or December 1–6, 2026**. Book by **October 31, 2026**, new bookings only, minimum 3 nights, limited stock. Echo Society travelers get an additional 5%. Not valid for the Travesía; third-party and additional services excluded.

**For Families** (/offer/childrens-benefit-at-explora/) — under 2 free in the parents' room; ages 2–12 extra bed from USD 310 per child per night (max 1 per room); ages 13–17 family rate from USD 360 per person per night in an additional room (double occupancy, min 2 young guests or 2 charged). Rapa Nui: extra bed only in Suites. Not for Travesías or Expeditions. Rates vary by season.

**Stay Longer and Save 30%** (/offer/stay-longer-and-save-30/) — 30% off every additional night **from your fifth night**; stays of five nights or more. Not valid for the Travesía or Expeditions, special programs, local rates (Chile, Peru, Argentina), child or youth rates, or third-party services; only combinable with the Echo Society 5%. Dates not applicable: Torres del Paine Nov 1, 2026 – Mar 15, 2027; Patagonia National Park Dec 24, 2026 – Jan 1, 2027; El Chaltén Nov 1, 2026 – Mar 31, 2027; Atacama Dec 22, 2026 – Jan 1, 2027 and Feb 5–13, 2027 (Carnival); Easter Island Dec 22, 2026 – Jan 1, 2027; Sacred Valley Dec 23, 2026 – Jan 1, 2027.

**Solo Travelers Save 50%** (/offer/solo-travel-benefit/) — "Solo travelers save 50% on the double occupancy rate": a single room at half price on select dates, i.e. the same rate as a double-occupancy guest. Dates: Sacred Valley and Patagonia National Park for stays through December 20, 2026; Atacama through December 20, 2026 except January 1–31, February 28 – March 3, April 17–20 and July 1–31, 2026; Torres del Paine and El Chaltén for stays beginning April 15 to October 15, 2026. Not combinable; not valid for the Travesía.

**Save 50% on Your Honeymoon** (/offer/honeymoon-benefit/) — 50% off the companion's fare (one person) on an all-inclusive stay, travel up to 6 months after the wedding; a marriage or wedding certificate is required at booking. Excludes third-party or private extras (e.g. Machu Picchu, helicopter flight, diving), local rates; not combinable. Travel windows — see disagreements.

**Combine Destinations and Save — Explora Passport** (/explora-passport/) — two or more destinations on one trip:
- **25%** on additional **Andean** destinations: Sacred Valley, Atacama, Rapa Nui, Travesía Atacama–Uyuni.
- **10%** on additional **Patagonia** destinations: Torres del Paine (National Park & Conservation Reserve), Patagonia National Park, El Calafate, El Chaltén.
- Plus a complimentary room upgrade at each destination (subject to availability at check-in); Echo Society members +5%.
- Adult rates only; individual bookings with no more than 30 days between first and last check-in. The discount goes to the lowest-priced destination(s) (with three destinations, the two lowest). The Travesía counts as one destination. Not valid at holiday periods (Christmas and New Year everywhere, Carnival in Atacama, Tapati on Easter Island). Example trips are in `journeys`.

**Explorer ECHO Society** (/offer/explorer-echo-society/) — "Former Explora travelers have special benefits and discounts of up to 50% for new stays." See below.

## Explorer ECHO Society (loyalty)
A loyalty program "to create a global community deeply committed to exploring and conserving remote territories". Registration is automatic after a stay at any lodge; a welcome email gives your category. Nights counted: from 2022 to 2025 all nights since 2007; 2025–2030 the previous 10 years; from 2030 the previous 5 years. Minimum age 18. Discounts extend to partners and direct relatives travelling with you; not for groups; not for local rates or special programs (e.g. the air portion of Explora Connects); benefits do not apply 21 December – 5 January. For online bookings, request the discount after the initial deposit and before paying 100%.

The program says "Up to 15% of the cost of your stay will go to support projects" in the destinations (water monitoring in Torres del Paine and Atacama, the Toki Forest on Rapa Nui, ECOAN in the Sacred Valley and others; see `conservation-reserves`). ## Unique programs (/program/)
- **Explora Connects Patagonia** (/program/explora-connects-patagonia/) — charter flights or vans between Patagonian lodges: Torres del Paine ↔ Patagonia National Park (1.5-hour flight), Balmaceda Airport ↔ Patagonia National Park (1-hour flight), Torres del Paine ↔ El Chaltén (8-hour van). "All-inclusive programs from 8 nights." Schedules and rates: reserve@explora.com; terms in a linked PDF. Details in `getting-there-and-transfers`.
- **Photography Program with Katsuyoshi Tanaka** (/program/photography-program-with-katsuyoshi-tanaka/) — with the photographer Katsuyoshi Tanaka and Nikon, for photographers of all levels; language English; sub-pages for Torres del Paine and Easter Island, and for Sacred Valley & Machu Picchu. No dates on the crawled page; contact reserve@explora.com.
- **Corporate Travel Program** (/corporate-travel-program/) — MICE (Meetings, Incentives, Conferences, Exhibitions) programs in Patagonia, Atacama, Easter Island and the Sacred Valley: incentive trips, board meetings, top-client invitations, team building; meeting infrastructure; can run privately. Inclusions in `whats-included`. Contact reserve@explora.com.

## Where the site disagrees with itself
- **Honeymoon windows** (same page): the intro says April–October for Torres del Paine, El Chaltén, Patagonia National Park *and the Travesía Atacama–Uyuni*, and year-round except **December 20 – January 20** for Atacama, Easter Island and Sacred Valley; the conditions say year-round except **Holidays (Christmas, New Year, Carnival, Easter)** for those three and April–October for Torres del Paine, El Chaltén and PNP (no Travesía).
- **Passport discount:** lodge pages say "up to 25% off on each additional destination" (the Atacama page adds "when combining stays in different regions"); /explora-passport/ gives only 10% for Patagonia combinations.
- **Echo "up to 50%":** /offer/explorer-echo-society/ says "discounts of up to 50%"; the categories page gives that only to Guardian/Custodian, whose participation is "coming soon".
- **Echo seasons are dated 2022–2023** on /join-the-explorer-echo-society/ (e.g. Patagonia high season 1 Nov 2022 – 31 Mar 2023); no current season table.
- **Echo deposit 20%** vs the policy page's "up to 30%" (see `booking-and-policies`).
- **Explora Connects van time:** "8-hour" (/program/explora-connects-patagonia/) vs "approximately 8–9 hours" (getting-here pages).
- **Solo offer vs solo FAQ:** the offer gives the double-occupancy rate on select dates; the solo blog's FAQ says "a single rate applies to private solo accommodation, standard across the collection".
