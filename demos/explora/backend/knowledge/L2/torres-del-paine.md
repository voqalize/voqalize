# Explora Torres del Paine (the lodge on Lake Pehoé)

Sources: /lodge/torres-del-paine-lodge/; /torres-del-paine-explora-lodge/; /torres-del-paine-tours/; /explorations-lodge/torres-del-paine/; /torres-del-paine-cuisine/; /torres-del-paine-getting-there/; /torres-del-paine-travel-tips/; /frequently-asked-questions-torres-del-paine/; /2026/08/25/spring-in-torres-del-paine/; /2026/07/10/atacama-and-torres-del-paine-two-sides-of-chile/; /2026/09/17/how-to-sequence-explora-lodges-south-america-journey/; reservations.explora.com.

## The lodge
- Inside Torres del Paine National Park, Chilean Patagonia, on the shore of **Lake Pehoé**, facing the Cuernos del Paine and the Paine Massif.
- Explora's original lodge; it "marked the beginning of our journey to conservation over two decades ago".
- **50 rooms**. Designed by Chilean architect **José Cruz Ovalle**; cypress, lenga and almendrillo woods. Guests get complimentary transfers across the lake on Explora's own ferry.
- Spa near the lodge: heated indoor pool, steam sauna and **four open-air hot tubs** with views to the Paine River. Facilities included; treatments extra.
- Awards (overview): Travelers' Choice 2025 Best of the Best (All Inclusive, South America); Condé Nast Traveler Readers' Choice 2025: Best Resorts in South America; Travel + Leisure Readers' 2022 Top 500. TripAdvisor 4.5 (1,609 reviews).
- Booking code on reservations.explora.com: **EPAT**. Deep link: `https://reservations.explora.com/ibe/paso1?language=EN&hotel_code=EPAT`.
- Open year-round.

## Explorations
- **More than 40** guided Explorations, full- and half-day, small groups of **no more than eight guests**, chosen each evening with your guide for the next day; classic routes plus Explora-only "Signature Explorations"; privately guided Explorations at extra cost. Guides trained at Explora's School of Guides, bilingual, Wilderness First Response certified.
- **Six zones** ("34 destinations"): 1 **Encuentro** (around the lodge; self-guided walks, Ona Bath House), 2 **Estepa** (guanacos, rhea, foxes, puma; 4,000-year-old Aonikenk cave paintings), 3 **Conservation Reserve** (Estancia 2 de Enero; the gaucho **Quincho** lamb roast, October–April), 4 **Bosque** (Río Pingo Valley, Ferrier hill), 5 **Glaciar** (catamaran to Grey Glacier; boat across Lake Pehoé to the French Valley), 6 **Montañas** (British camp, base of the Torres, Tenerife and Castillo hills; more demanding).
- Activities: hike, overland, horseback ride, high mountain ascent. Difficulty filters: Easy, Moderate, Advanced, Expert.
- Examples: **Puma Encounters** (overland, easy, full-day; 18+ only; third-party service subject to availability; **additional cost USD 2,700 for a single traveler and USD 1,550 per person for groups of 2 to 6**; book ahead via reserve@explora.com); also reserve hikes (Horizonte, Exploradores, El Pasado), easy rides (Cañadón de las Torres, Fronteras, Puntilla del Toro), advanced rides (Tough Life, Florence, Serrano, Donoso, Témpanos), Aonikenk (hike) and Pioneros (overland).
- Also kayaking and birdwatching; non-hikers can fill a stay with riding, kayaking, wildlife and Grey Glacier boat trips (FAQ).
- Free: trekking poles, helmets, riding gaiters, a bottle of water.

## Dining (/torres-del-paine-cuisine/)
All meals, all beverages and an open bar, plus meals and snacks on full-day explorations. Locally sourced dishes highlighting the gaucho legacy, a dining room over Lake Pehoé, Chilean wines chosen by the culinary team. Lunch is not included on check-out day.

## Getting there (/torres-del-paine-getting-there/)
- **Puerto Natales** (recommended): direct flights from Santiago with LATAM or Sky. Explora van from Puerto Natales Airport at **12:00 and 15:00**, free, arranged in advance; drive about 1.45 hours. Pick-ups from Puerto Natales hotels and port between 11:00 and 12:00 (driver waits max 15 minutes). Puerto Natales Airport has maintenance **January–March 2027** with a reduced schedule; write to reserve@explora.com if affected.
- **Punta Arenas**: free airport pick-ups at **10:00 and 15:00**; about 4.5 hours' drive with a lunch stop.
- Private transfers extra; vans seat 14.
- **From Patagonia National Park**: 1.5-hour lodge-to-lodge charter flight (Explora Connects), Mondays and Thursdays, October 3rd to mid-April, at additional cost.
- **To/from El Chaltén or El Calafate**: van daily at 9:00 a.m., extra cost, October–April, minimum two passengers, private up to six; El Chaltén 402 km (250 mi), about 8–9 hours; El Calafate 374 km (232 mi), about 5–6 hours.
- Check-in from 13:00; check-out by 10:00 (luggage held if you join a half-day exploration).
- Distances (FAQ page): Punta Arenas 390 km, Puerto Natales 140 km, El Calafate 376 km, El Chaltén 400 km.

## Best time and climate
- Overview FAQ: main season **October through April**; November–February has the longest days and most stable weather; wind year-round; shoulder seasons (October–November, March–April) have fewer visitors and lower rates.
- /2026/07/10/…: high season October–April; low season May–September (quieter trails, better wildlife sightings, snow on the massif).
- Spring at Explora runs **October 1 through December 20** (/2026/08/25/…): steppe turns green, guanaco calves (chulengos), calafate blooms.
- Climate: the "Wet Andes", "all four seasons into a single day".

## Travel tips
- Packing: sun and warm hats; four layers (thermal, fleece, down, waterproof shell and trousers); hiking shoes; thin and windproof gloves; sunglasses, sunscreen, buff; 25-litre backpack; headlamp. Optional: swimsuit, sandals.
- Voltage **220V**. Wi-Fi limited in common areas; no cell signal at the hotel or around it.
- Medical: paramedic 15 minutes away year-round; hospital in Puerto Natales 1.5 hours; Clínica Magallanes, Punta Arenas, 5 hours.
- Emergency contact email for relatives: recpatagonia@explora.com (don't quote the phone numbers).
- Stay length: minimum five nights recommended; six for a more complete picture.
