## The lodge
- **Explora Rapa Nui**, Easter Island, Chile; booking code **ERAP**. "The World's most remote inhabited island", some 2,300 miles (3,700 km) west of South America; countryside location outside Hanga Roa ("5 miles" away per /destinations/), with Pacific views.
- Known for the moai and Rapa Nui culture: hikes to the Rano Raraku quarry, bikes to ancient settlements, coastal kayaking.
- Awards: World's Leading Expedition Company 2025; Condé Nast Traveler Readers' Choice 2025: Best Resorts in South America. TripAdvisor: "5.0 Excellent 887 reviews".
- **Included (all-inclusive):** accommodations, all Explorations, all meals and beverages, spa facilities, national park entrance fees, and round-trip transfers from Mataveri International Airport (IPC). **Not included:** personal purchases, gratuities, spa treatments, phone calls, laundry. Drinks include Chilean wines, beer and spirits; "premium or specialty selections" may cost extra.

## The lodge building (/explora-lodge-easter-island-new/)
Architect **José Cruz Ovalle**: "interconnected volumes, reminiscent of the arrangement of the moai statues", natural woods, light and shadow. Elevated grassland just outside Hanga Roa, "central to trailheads and archeological sites". Full-service spa **Hare Vaka** (massage and body treatments inspired by Polynesian and Rapa Nui traditions; treatments extra), sauna, outdoor pool and hot tub (included).

## Explorations (EXPLORATIONS tab, /explorations-lodge/easter-island/, 35 detail pages)
- "30 guided Explorations" included, customized daily (your guide meets you **every evening**), groups of no more than eight, guides from the School of Guides "fluent in Spanish and English"; privately guided Explorations cost extra. Classic routes plus Explora-only Signature Explorations.
- The crawl holds **35** exploration pages: by activity Hike 12, At the sea 8, Bike 6, Overland 6, Combination 2, Self Guided 1; by difficulty Easy 14, Moderate 10, Advanced 10, Expert 1; Half-day 29, Full-day 6. Zones: Ora (volcanic landscapes, 12), Tao a Tupuna (archaeology, 9), Vaikava (sea, 8), Maohi (living culture, 4).
- Examples: **Ara Moai** (hike from the Rano Raraku moai quarry, easy, 5.3 km, 3 h); **Bird Cult** (Vinapu and the Rano Kau crater, easy overland, 3 h 30); **Tongariki** (Rapa Nui's largest ahu, easy, 3 h); **Rapa Nui Sanctuaries** (Ahu Akivi's 7 moai, Ana Te Pahu lava tunnel); **Tangata Manu** (kayak on the Birdman coast, advanced, 12 km, 4 h); **Ariki** (kayak from Anakena, moderate); **Motu Nui** boat and two snorkeling outings; **Discover Scuba Diving** (easy) and **Rapa Nui Diving** (certified divers); **The Hills** (expert full-day bike, 38.7 km, 772 m gain); **Rano Kau** and **Lava flows** (advanced full days, 6–7 h); **Poike** (moderate full-day hike); **Hanga Roa** (self-guided town visit, 2 h).
- **Diving costs extra** (third party; book via reservations or on site). **No horseback riding** (third parties can arrange it, extra cost).
- Unguided: taxi (extra) or bike to Anakena/Ovahe beaches or Hanga Roa. A certified guide is **mandatory in all National Park areas**.
- Gear provided free: trekking poles, and all equipment for cycling and snorkeling.

## Dining (DINING tab, /easter-island-cuisine/)
Polynesian and Chilean cuisine in an ocean-view dining room: sweet potatoes, taro, Pacific seafood, tropical fruit "such as the famous Rapa Nui pineapple". Included: breakfast, lunch and dinner, "all beverages, wine and an open bar", a curated Chilean wine selection at the bar and dining room, and meals and snacks on full-day Explorations. FAQ: alcohol (Chilean wines, beer, spirits) **is included**, with "no separate bar tab"; premium or specialty selections may cost extra.

## Getting there (/getting-there-how-to-get-to-easter-island/)
- Fly from Santiago, **about 5 hours** (LATAM; seasonal flights from Lima) to Mataveri International Airport (IPC), Hanga Roa.
- Van transfer of **15 minutes**, included in programs with transportation, timed to each traveler's flights; vans seat up to 9.
- Private transfer: extra cost (reserve@explora.com); a flight change under 24 hours before check-in may add costs.
- **Check-in after 13:00** (arrive at noon for an afternoon Exploration); **check-out 10:00**. A departure-day half-day Exploration is possible if flights allow (check out 07:00, luggage stored; lunch that day not included). Late check-out: ask for availability and rates.

## Climate and best time (/easter-island-travel-tips-new/)
Mild, subtropical, pleasant year-round. Average about 70°F (21°C); February warmest, up to 81°F (27°C). Brief scattered rain, about 45 inches (1,140 mm) a year, no distinct rainy season; humidity about 76%. No altitude stated.

## Travel tips
- **Pack:** sun hat, three layers (base, fleece, waterproof jacket), hiking trousers and shorts, hiking shoes, windproof gloves, sunglasses, sunscreen; optional swimsuit, sandals. Explora provides trekking poles and a water bottle.
- **Voltage 220V.** Wi-Fi may be unreliable in rooms and common areas. There is **no cell phone coverage** at the lodge; a landline is available "at an acceptable cost".
- **How long:** "We recommend a minimum of four nights"; five nights "are a good alternative to explore all its corners".
- **Medical:** Hanga Roa has a hospital. Explorations staff hold Wilderness Advanced First Aid (WAFA) certification.
- Emergency contact for relatives: recrapanui@explora.com. **Tips** are voluntary, at reception (amount differs by page, below).
