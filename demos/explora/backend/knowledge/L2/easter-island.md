# Easter Island: Explora Rapa Nui

Sources: /lodge/easter-island-hotels/ (overview); /easter-island-reserve-hotel/ (rooms); /explorations-lodge/easter-island/ (explorations archive, page 1 of 3); /easter-island-cuisine/; /getting-there-how-to-get-to-easter-island/; /easter-island-travel-tips-new/; /frequently-asked-questions-easter-island/; /destinations/ and /lodge/ (lodge cards); /2026/09/17/how-to-sequence-explora-lodges-south-america-journey/; /offer/childrens-benefit-at-explora/; /what-is-explorer-echo-society/; L3/reservations.json.

## The lodge
- **Explora Rapa Nui**, Easter Island, Chile; booking code **ERAP**. "The World's most remote inhabited island", some 2,300 miles (3,700 km) west of South America; countryside location outside Hanga Roa ("5 miles" away per /destinations/), with Pacific views.
- Known for the moai and Rapa Nui culture: hikes to the Rano Raraku quarry, bikes to ancient settlements, coastal kayaking; award-winning architecture.
- Full-service spa, outdoor pool, hot tub, sauna.
- Awards: World's Leading Expedition Company 2025; Condé Nast Traveler Readers' Choice 2025: Best Resorts in South America. TripAdvisor: "5.0 Excellent 887 reviews".
- **Included (all-inclusive):** accommodations, all Explorations, all meals and beverages, spa facilities, national park entrance fees, and round-trip transfers from Mataveri International Airport (IPC). **Not included:** personal purchases, gratuities, spa treatments, phone calls, laundry. Drinks include Chilean wines, beer and spirits; "premium or specialty selections" may cost extra.

## Rooms (/easter-island-reserve-hotel/, rooms tabs)
30 rooms in total: **4 suites and 26 Varúa rooms**.
- **Varúa Room** (26): 32 m² (345 ft²), sea view; 1 king or 2 twin beds, crib*, heating, jetted bathtub, separate WC, non-smoking.
- **Raa Suite** (4): 44 m² (474 ft²), sea view; same, plus **extra bed for children***.
- *Subject to availability. The children's offer: extra bed **only in the Suite category**. All rooms have air conditioning; bed type is chosen at booking (changes on site subject to availability).

## Explorations
- 30 / "more than 30" guided Explorations, included, customized daily to interest and fitness: hiking, biking, snorkeling, diving, SUP, kayaking, culture.
- Archive filters: **area** (Maohi, Ora, Tao a Tupuna, Vaikava), **activity** (Hike, Overland, Bike, Self Guided, At the sea, Combination), **difficulty** (Easy, Moderate, Advanced, Expert).
- Examples (archive page 1): at sea — Tangata Manu (kayak, Birdman coast, advanced), Maratiri (kayak, Tongariki, moderate), Motu Nui (boat to bird islets, easy), two snorkeling outings, Discover Scuba Diving (easy), Rapa Nui Diving (certified divers); bike — La Peninsula (Poike, advanced), The Hills (Terevaka, expert, full day); overland, all easy half days — Bird Cult (Rano Kau crater, Orongo), Rapa Nui Sanctuaries (Akivi's 7 moai, Ana Te Pahu lava tunnel, Tahai), Hanga (Papa Vaka petroglyphs), Pukao (Puna Pao quarry).
- **Diving costs extra** (third party; book via reservations or on site). **No horseback riding** (third parties can arrange it, extra cost).
- Unguided: taxi (extra) or bike to Anakena/Ovahe beaches or Hanga Roa (about 20 min round trip by car, 1:30 h by bike). A certified guide is **mandatory in all National Park areas**.
- Gear provided free: trekking poles, and all equipment for cycling and snorkeling.

## Dining (/easter-island-cuisine/)
Polynesian and Chilean cuisine in an ocean-view dining room: sweet potatoes, taro, Pacific seafood, tropical fruit "such as the famous Rapa Nui pineapple". Included: all meals, beverages, wine and an open bar, plus meals and snacks on full-day Explorations.

## Getting there (/getting-there-how-to-get-to-easter-island/)
- Fly from Santiago, **about 5 hours** (LATAM; seasonal flights from Lima) to Mataveri International Airport (IPC), Hanga Roa.
- Van transfer of **15 minutes**, included in programs with transportation, timed to each traveler's flights; vans seat up to 9.
- Private transfer: extra cost (reserve@explora.com); a flight change under 24 hours before check-in may add costs.
- **Check-in after 13:00** (arrive at noon for an afternoon Exploration); **check-out 10:00**. A departure-day half-day Exploration is possible if flights allow (check out 07:00, luggage stored; lunch that day not included). Late check-out: ask for availability and rates.
- Coordinates: 27º8'30.89"S 109º21'26.91"W.

## Climate and best time (/easter-island-travel-tips-new/)
Mild, subtropical, pleasant year-round. Average about 70°F (21°C); February warmest, up to 81°F (27°C). Brief scattered rain, about 45 inches (1,140 mm) a year, no distinct rainy season; humidity about 76%. No altitude stated.

## Travel tips
- **Pack:** sun hat, three layers (base, fleece, waterproof jacket), t-shirts, hiking trousers and shorts, hiking shoes and long socks, windproof gloves, sunglasses, buff, sunscreen; optional swimsuit, sandals, camel back. Explora provides trekking poles and a water bottle.
- **Voltage 220V.** Wi-Fi may be unreliable in rooms and common areas. There is **no cell phone coverage** at the lodge; a landline is available "at an acceptable cost".
- **How long:** a minimum of **four nights**; five is "a good alternative".
- **Medical:** Hanga Roa has a hospital. Explorations staff hold Wilderness Advanced First Aid (WAFA) certification.
- Emergency contact for relatives: recrapanui@explora.com. **Tips** are voluntary, at reception (amount differs by page, below).

## Where the site disagrees with itself
- **Tip amount:** /easter-island-travel-tips-new/ suggests "USD 100 per day per room". /frequently-asked-questions-easter-island/ suggests "$100 per stay per visitor".
- **Number of Explorations:** "30 guided Explorations" (overview) vs "more than 30" (travel-tips FAQ, and the home page for every lodge).
- **Diving:** listed as Explorations in the archive (overview: all Explorations included), but both FAQ pages say it costs extra and is run by third parties.
- **Bed options:** the rooms page says "1 King size bed or 2 twin beds". The FAQs say "a king size bed or two double beds".
- **Wi-Fi:** "may vary in reliability" in rooms and common areas (travel tips) vs "limited" in common areas (FAQ).
- **Raa Suite views:** "a view of the sea" (rooms page) vs "ocean and garden views" (travel-tips FAQ).
