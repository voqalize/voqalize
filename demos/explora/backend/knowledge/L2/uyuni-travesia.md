# Travesía Atacama–Uyuni (Chile to Bolivia, or Bolivia to Chile)

Sources: `L3/lodges.json` (the six sub-menu pages below and 30 /explorations/ detail pages); /explorations-lodge/atacama-uyuni/; /uyuni-salt-flat-unique-lodge-jirira-lodge/ (older Jirira page); /uyuni-hotel/; /atacama-desert/; /2026/09/17/how-to-sequence-explora-lodges-south-america-journey/; /2026/08/27/solo-travel-set-your-own-pace/; /payment-and-cancellation-policies/; /explora-passport/; offer pages; reservations.explora.com.

## What it is
- A **nine-day** overland crossing ("travesía", Spanish for long crossing) from the Atacama Desert to Bolivia's Uyuni Salt Flat "or vice versa", "over 300 miles" past **Laguna Colorada** (flamingos), **Irruputuncu** volcano and the **Salar de Uyuni**.
- Lodges: **Explora Atacama** first, to acclimatize (four nights per /atacama-uyuni-itineraries/, three per /uyuni-lodge/), then **Ramaditas**, **Chituca** and **Jirira** in Bolivia; the 2026 blog calls it "privately guided".
- From Atacama you cross 338 miles (544 km) in "a comfortable, satellite-equipped 4×4 vehicle with a private driver and expert Explora guide, who join you for the remainder of your itinerary" (itineraries page); GPS, VHF radio and oxygen on board (older Jirira page).
- Included: accommodations, all explorations, all meals and beverages, guided 4x4 drive, park fees, round-trip airport transfers.
- Booking codes: **PATA** "Atacama & Uyuni (Chile to Bolivia)", **PUYU** "Uyuni & Atacama (Bolivia to Chile)".

## Itineraries (/atacama-uyuni-itineraries/)
- The standard journey is **nine days**. The page offers an **8 Night Itinerary** and a **10 Night Itinerary** (links only; their day-by-day content is not in the crawl), and can tailor "anything from a six-night adventure to an immersive 11-night itinerary".
- "We always recommend additional days at the start" in Atacama to acclimatize before Ramaditas at "13,123 feet (4,000 meters)" and Chituca and Jirira at "11,482-plus feet (3,500 meters)".
- Order from the zone and exploration pages: Atacama → overland to **Ramaditas** (Trayecto Atacama–Ramaditas, full day across the Altiplano) → overland to **Chituca** (via Laguna Pastos Grandes and the yardangs, or the Altiplanic lagoons) → **Jirira** on the Uyuni salt flat (Trayecto Overland Salar). The reverse runs Bolivia → Chile (PUYU).

## Explorations (30 detail pages in lodges.json; /uyuni-tours/)
- Planned each evening with your guide; Atacama's own explorations are in topic `atacama`.
- **30** explorations: Hike 12, Overland 10, Bike 4, High Mountain Ascent 3, Sky 1; Easy 17, Moderate 8, Advanced 5 (no Expert); 16 full-day, 14 half-day. By base: Lodge Uyuni (Jirira) 16, Ramaditas 6, Chituca 5, transfer days 3.
- **Ramaditas:** Laguna Ramaditas (hike, easy); Laguna Honda (hike, moderate, 10 km); Lagunas Andinas (overland, easy, lagoons and vicuñas); Yardangs (hike, advanced, 18.8 km); Volcán Ascotán (ascent, advanced).
- **Chituca:** Las Apachetas and Pueblos Andinos (villages, easy, full-day); Jawincha (hike, advanced, 14.3 km); Volcán Irruputunku (ascent, advanced); Astronomía Andina (sky).
- **Jirira / Uyuni:** Jirira (hike, easy, 3.3 km); Inti (sunrise) and Killa (sunset and moonrise) on the salt flat (overland, easy); Salar and Tara bike rides across the salt (easy / moderate); Isla Pescado (hike, moderate); Colores del Tunupa (ascent, advanced, 11.8 km, with a local guide from Coquesa). Tunupa summit: 17,822 f.a.s.l.
- Transfer days are explorations too: Trayecto Overland & Yardangs (Ramaditas–Chituca, moderate, 163 km, max 4,744 m).

## Dining
No DINING tab. "Dining and meals crafted from locally sourced ingredients" at every lodge (/uyuni-lodge/); the solo-travel post says the Travesía lodges seat you at a long communal table. The Jirira menu is by Bolivian chefs Sebastián Giménez and Mauricio López of restaurant Ancestral in La Paz.

## Getting there (/how-to-get-to-uyuni/)
- **From Chile:** Santiago (SCL) to **Calama (CJC)**, 2 hours (early flight advised); pick-up at the airport or Calama/San Pedro hotels. Ends at **Joya Andina Airport (UYU)** for a flight to **La Paz (LPB)**.
- **From Bolivia:** fly to Uyuni (UYU); airport or hotel pick-up (09:00 recommended, lodge around 11:00). Spend a day in **La Paz first** to acclimatize. Ends at Calama Airport for Santiago.
- Departures: San Pedro check-out 10:00, Calama transfers until 12:00, no lunch, leave three hours before the flight; Uyuni, two hours before (10:00 from Uyuni hotels). Return to Atacama: private leg to the **Ollagüe border** (max 2, lunch, extra cost).
- Uyuni town to Jirira: about **1 h 45 min**, up to **4 h 30 min** in the rainy season. Check-in 13:00, check-out 10:00.
- **Bolivia visa** (tab "VISA to Bolivia"): many nationalities need only ID or passport; **US citizens require a visa** (older Jirira page). Get it at a consulate beforehand rather than at this remote border; the tab lists the documents (passport with six months' validity, photos, yellow fever certificate, Explora booking copy and more).

## Altitude, health and acclimatization
- Route altitude "between 3,600 and 4,700 m.a.s.l. (11,811 and 15,419 f.a.s.l)"; first day only gentle walks. Be healthy enough for heights and temperature swings.

## Climate
San Pedro: over 90% sunny days; 8–23°C by day in winter (June–September), 12–28°C in summer (December–March). Uyuni: as dry but colder, 2–18°C in winter, 5–20°C in "summer (September to December)", nights down to -20°C. Wet-season rains "deeply damage the tracks" (dates: see disagreements).

## Travel tips
- Pack sun and cold hats, four layers, hiking shoes, gloves, sunglasses, sunscreen, 25-liter backpack; trekking poles, helmets and water provided.
- Wi-Fi limited; 220V. Tips voluntary; "USD 100 per day per room".
- Must be booked before arrival; it cannot be added from Explora Atacama.

## Booking, payment and offers
- Up to 30% deposit; balance and free changes until **60 days before check-in**; 59–30 days 50%, 29–0 days 100% (/payment-and-cancellation-policies/).

## Where the site disagrees with itself
- **Uyuni altitude:** "Uyuni is located at 4,700 m.a.s.l (15,400 f.a.s.l.)" (/atacama-to-uyuni-travel-tips/) vs "3,681 m.a.s.l. (12,076 f.a.s.l.)" (older Jirira page), which matches Jirira lodge's 12,077 ft (3,681 m) on /uyuni-lodge/.
- **Atacama altitude:** 2,438 m / 8,000 ft (/uyuni-lodge/) vs 2,500 m / 8,200 ft (travel tips).
- **Length:** "nine-day" travesía (overview, itineraries) vs an "ITINERARY 8 NIGHTS" link (travel tips); the itineraries page offers both 8- and 10-night versions.
- **Nights at Atacama before crossing:** four (/atacama-uyuni-itineraries/) vs three (/uyuni-lodge/).
- **Distance:** "over 300 miles" (overview) vs "338 miles (544 km)" (itineraries) — compatible, but different figures.
- **Ramaditas elevation:** 13,370 ft (4,075 m) (/uyuni-lodge/) vs 13,123 ft (4,000 m) (/atacama-uyuni-itineraries/); Chituca 12,244 ft and Jirira 12,077 ft vs "11,482-plus feet (3,500 meters)".
- **Guides:** "certified bilingual guides" and "multilingual experts" on the same /uyuni-tours/ page.
- **Zones:** "three unique zones" and "5 unique ... zones" on the same /uyuni-tours/ page.
- **Rainy season:** "Between December and March ... 'The Altiplano Summer'" (travel tips) vs "Between January and March ... 'the Altiplano Winter'" (older Jirira page). Uyuni "summer" is given as September–December while San Pedro's is December–March.
- **Experience on the Altiplano:** "over 15 years" (older Jirira page) vs "for over 20 years" (/atacama-desert/).
