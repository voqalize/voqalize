# Travesía Atacama–Uyuni (Chile to Bolivia, or Bolivia to Chile)

Sources: /lodge/atacama-uyuni-crossing/; /uyuni-lodge/; /uyuni-tours/; /explorations-lodge/atacama-uyuni/; /how-to-get-to-uyuni/; /atacama-to-uyuni-travel-tips/; /uyuni-salt-flat-unique-lodge-jirira-lodge/ (older Jirira page); /uyuni-hotel/; /atacama-desert/; /2026/09/17/how-to-sequence-explora-lodges-south-america-journey/; /payment-and-cancellation-policies/; /explora-passport/; offer pages; reservations.explora.com.

## What it is
- A **nine-day** overland crossing ("travesía", Spanish for long crossing) from the Atacama Desert to Bolivia's Uyuni Salt Flat "or vice versa", "over 300 miles" past **Laguna Colorada** (flamingos), **Irruputuncu** volcano and the **Salar de Uyuni**, entirely with Explora guides.
- Lodges: **Explora Atacama** (three nights first, to acclimatize), then **Ramaditas**, **Chituca** and **Jirira** in Bolivia; the sequence blog calls Jirira "the anchor lodge" and the Travesía "privately guided".
- Fully equipped **4×4** with an Explora guide and a local driver; satellite communication, GPS, VHF radio and oxygen on board.
- Included: accommodations, all explorations, all meals and beverages, guided 4x4 drive, national park fees, round-trip airport transfers ("with a private service", older Jirira page).
- Booking codes (group "Travesias", booking flow): **PATA** "Atacama & Uyuni (Chile to Bolivia)", **PUYU** "Uyuni & Atacama (Bolivia to Chile)". /atacama-uyuni-itineraries/ and /uyuni-itinerary-8-nights/ were not crawled, so the day-by-day plan is unknown.

## Explorations
- Customized daily; your guide (School of Guides, bilingual) plans the next day with you each evening.
- Types: Hike, Overland, Bike, Sky, High Mountain Ascent: wetlands, ancient settlements, bike rides over salt and quinoa fields, volcano ascents.
- "30 destinations" in 5 zones: Chituca, Ramaditas, Lodge Uyuni, Ramaditas–Chituca, Chituca–Lodge Uyuni. Tunupa summit: 17,822 f.a.s.l.
- Examples (/explorations-lodge/atacama-uyuni/): Jirira (hike, easy, half-day); Isla K'ajchi (bike, moderate, at sunset); Trayecto Overland & Yardangs (Ramaditas–Chituca, moderate, full-day, 4-hour hike, cave paintings); Killa (sunset and moonrise) and Inti (sunrise) on the salt flat, overland, easy; Colores del Tunupa (high mountain ascent, advanced, full-day).
- Provided: trekking poles, bike helmets, water bottle.

## Dining
Locally sourced meals. The Jirira menu is by Bolivian chefs Sebastián Giménez and Mauricio López of restaurant Ancestral in La Paz.

## Getting there (/how-to-get-to-uyuni/)
- **From Chile:** Santiago (SCL) to **Calama (CJC)**, 2 hours; take an early flight. Pick-up at Calama airport or Calama/San Pedro hotels. Ends at **Joya Andina Airport (UYU)** for a flight to **La Paz (LPB)**.
- **From Bolivia:** fly to Uyuni (UYU); airport or hotel pick-up (09:00 recommended, lodge around 11:00). Spend a day in **La Paz first** to acclimatize. Ends at Calama Airport for Santiago.
- Departures: San Pedro check-out 10:00, Calama transfers until 12:00, no lunch, leave three hours before the flight; Uyuni, two hours before (10:00 from Uyuni hotels). Private leg to the **Ollagüe border** (max 2, lunch, extra cost); the crawl cut the rest.
- Uyuni town to Jirira: about **1 hour 45 minutes**, up to **4 hours 30 minutes** in the rainy season. Check Uyuni–La Paz flight frequency. Check-in after 13:00, check-out 10:00.
- **Bolivia visa:** many countries need only ID or passport (e.g. Great Britain, Canada, Australia, Germany, France, Spain, Japan). **US citizens require a visa** (older Jirira page). Get it at a consulate beforehand rather than at this remote border. Documents: sworn application, passport copy (six months' validity), photos, yellow fever certificate, Explora booking copy, round-trip tickets, credit card copy, "USD 160 in cash" (older page). Check requirements months ahead.

## Altitude, health and acclimatization
- Route altitude "between 3,600 and 4,700 m.a.s.l. (11,811 and 15,419 f.a.s.l)"; first day only gentle walks. Be healthy enough for heights and temperature swings; bring a sleep aid only if altitude keeps you awake.

## Climate
- San Pedro: over 90% sunny days; 8°C–23°C by day in winter (June–September), 12°C–28°C in summer (December–March).
- Uyuni: as dry but colder; 2°C–18°C in winter (June–September), 5°C–20°C in "summer (September to December)"; nights as low as -20°C. Heavy rains in the Altiplano wet season "deeply damage the tracks" (dates: see disagreements).

## Travel tips
- Pack sun and cold hats, four layers, hiking boots, warm gloves, sunglasses, sunscreen, 25-liter backpack.
- Wi-Fi limited; 220V. Tips voluntary; "USD 100 per day per room".
- Cannot be added once at Explora Atacama; book and confirm before arrival.

## Booking, payment and offers
- Down payment of up to 30% to confirm; the balance is due **60 days before check-in**. Changes are free up to 60 days before check-in. Cancellation: 60+ days no fine; 59–30 days 50%; 29–0 days 100% (/payment-and-cancellation-policies/).
- Price on the older Jirira page: "from 3 nights for USD 1,390 per person/ night". Live rates are on reservations.explora.com and vary by date.
- Explora Passport: "counts as one destination"; 25% off as an Andean destination. Not valid: Stay Longer and Save 30%, Solo Travel, Children's benefit. Help: reserve@explora.com.

## Where the site disagrees with itself
- **Uyuni altitude:** "Uyuni is located at 4,700 m.a.s.l (15,400 f.a.s.l.)" (/atacama-to-uyuni-travel-tips/) vs "3,681 m.a.s.l. (12,076 f.a.s.l.)" (older Jirira page), which matches Jirira lodge's 12,077 ft (3,681 m) on /uyuni-lodge/.
- **Atacama altitude:** 2,438 m / 8,000 ft (/uyuni-lodge/) vs 2,500 m / 8,200 ft (travel tips).
- **Length:** "nine-day" travesía (overview) vs an "ITINERARY 8 NIGHTS" link (travel tips); these may describe the same trip.
- **Zones:** "three unique zones" and "5 unique ... zones" on the same /uyuni-tours/ page.
- **Rainy season:** "Between December and March ... 'The Altiplano Summer'" (travel tips) vs "Between January and March ... 'the Altiplano Winter'" (older Jirira page). Uyuni "summer" is given as September–December while San Pedro's is December–March.
- **Experience on the Altiplano:** "over 15 years" (older Jirira page) vs "for over 20 years" (/atacama-desert/).
