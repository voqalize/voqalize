# Explora Expeditions in South America: Sacred Mountains (Peru) and Tierra del Fuego (Chile)

Sources: /explora-expeditions/; `L3/lodges.json` (expedition tabs, crawled 2026-10-22); both expeditions' tab pages (listed below); copies /sacred-mountain-expedition/sacred-mountains-expedition-departure-dates/, /sacred-mountains-expedition-itinerary/, /tierra-del-fuego-itinenary/, /echo-society-travel-tierra-del-fuego/…; /expeditions-call-out-homepage/; /2026/06/03/from-the-arctic-to-the-andes-…/; /payment-and-cancellation-policies/.

**All expeditions:**
- **Group:** max 8 travelers; private departures available. **Booking:** an **enquiry form**, not the calendar (reservations.explora.com/ibe/expeditions?expedition_request=XSCM or XTFG; the visitor ticks the reCAPTCHA), or reserveexpeditions@explora.com.
- **Payment** (/payment-and-cancellation-policies/): deposit up to 30%, including a non-refundable Reservation Fee of **USD 500 pp** (USD 1,000 for Iceland); balance 120 days before check-in.
- **Changes and cancellation:** policy page: free 120+ days before, 50% at 119–60, 100% at 59–0; the Reservation Fee is never refunded. Both expeditions' own FAQs state a 60/30-day scale (see disagreements). A flight disclaimer and exploration waiver must be signed; travel insurance is recommended for evacuation.

The Iceland expeditions are in topic `expeditions-iceland`.

## Sacred Mountains Expedition: The Unknown Inca Trail to Machu Picchu (XSCM)
- **The trip:** 9 days / 8 nights, from USD $8,640 per person, double occupancy, max 8. "Physically demanding"; "requires excellent physical fitness" and comfort at high altitude. Starts and ends at Explora Valle Sagrado; finishes at Machu Picchu through the Sun Gate. Nights: Explora Valle Sagrado (D1–3, D8), private tented camps (D4–6), Las Qolqas Hotel (D7).

**Itinerary** (/peru-sacred-mountains-expedition-itinerary/):
- D1 Cusco Airport to Valle Sagrado; Chinchero and Machu Kolka, 4.6 km, max 3,933 m.
- D2 Qhapaq Ñan to Wata, 11.5 km, max 3,820 m.
- D3 Shepherds' route Lamay–Pisac (acclimatization), 10.3 km, max 4,396 m.
- D4 Misquiyaco Private Conservation Area (exclusive Explora access), 7.7 km, max 4,297 m. Tented camp.
- D5 Toward Apu Salkantay, 13 km, max 4,671 m (15,324 ft). Camp at Pampacahuana, 3,900 m.
- D6 Descent toward the Vilcanota River, 15.2 km, max 3,883 m. Camp at Chamana, 2,575 m.
- D7 About 2.5 h walk, then Ollantaytambo and the Temple of the Sun, pachamanca lunch; 6.4 km, max 2,703 m. Las Qolqas Hotel.
- D8 Vistadome train to Chachabamba, then Wiñay Wayna, the Sun Gate and Machu Picchu, 10.5 km, max 2,721 m. Farewell dinner at Valle Sagrado.
- D9 Depart by 10:00 AM.

**Dates** (/peru-sacred-mountains-expeditions-departure-dates/, the page the overview links):
- 2026: Aug 8–16, Aug 15–23 and Sept 5–13 are Sold out.
- 2027: May 4–12, May 15–23, June 9–17, June 26–July 4, July 6–14, July 17–25, Aug 7–15 and Aug 18–26 are Available; May 29–June 6 is Sold out.
- **Permit:** passport copies due **at least four months before departure** for the Machu Picchu permit; it can't be reissued, so carry both passports if you renew.

**What to bring:** four layers, hats, gloves, trekking kit, sleeping socks, camp shoes, 25 L pack, headlamp, sunscreen, 1 L bottle, mosquito repellent. Explora provides poles, a bottle and a microfiber towel.

**FAQ:**
- **Included:** guides, all transport (incl. trains), accommodation, meals, entrance fees, camp gear, a daily hot shower, two days of leg massages, a 15–20-person staff. **Not included:** gratuities, hotel extras, private services.
- **Arrival:** morning flight to Cusco for pickup, or arrive a day early (pickup from your Cusco hotel). Check-in from 3:00 pm.
- **Fitness:** you hike 80 km (50 miles), up to 4,683 m (15,350 ft). Strong physical condition is needed.
- **Connectivity:** at the lodges, none in the mountains. **Food:** vegan on request.
- **Luggage:** a 20 L daypack; mules carry the 40 L duffel provided, and extra bags stay at the hotel.
- **Emergencies:** satellite communication, oxygen, evacuation points; care in Ollantaytambo, Urubamba or Cusco.

## Tierra del Fuego Expedition: A Journey to the End of the World (XTFG)
- **The trip:** 7 days / 6 nights, from USD $10,950 per person, double occupancy, max 8, "Suitable for all travelers". It starts and ends in Punta Arenas, Chile.
- Nights are "hosted by local families", and travel is by 4×4 trucks.

**Itinerary** (/tierra-del-fuego-expedition-itinerary/):
- D1 Meet at the Yegua Loca Hotel, Punta Arenas; welcome dinner.
- D2 Boat to Porvenir, the pampa and the island's only king penguin colony. Local estancia (single rooms only).
- D3 Karukinka Park, a 7 km hike. Estancia Fagnano, the Genskowsky family's cabins (some have outdoor bathrooms), D3–D6.
- D4 Boat to Parry Fjord and the Almirantazgo Sound.
- D5 Full-day hike of "advanced intensity" in Yendegaia National Park (closed to visitors; Explora has special access).
- D6 Andean treeline walk (snowshoes possible), farewell dinner.
- D7 Flight from Pampa Guanaco to Punta Arenas on Aerolíneas DAP (max 15 people).

**Dates** (/tierra-del-fuego-departure-dates/):
- 2026: Nov 7–13 and Nov 14–20 are Sold out. Dec 5–11 is Available.
- 2026 holiday season: Dec 22–28 and Dec 29, 2026–Jan 4, 2027 are Available.
- 2027: Jan 18–24, Feb 8–14, Mar 1–7, Apr 3–9, Oct 26–Nov 1, Nov 4–10, Nov 13–19, Dec 20–26 and Dec 28, 2027–Jan 3, 2028 are Available.
- Holiday departures: peak-season rate **USD $12,075** pp, double occupancy. Suggested add-ons: Torres del Paine, Patagonia National Park or El Chaltén lodges.

**What to bring:** three layers, cap and cold hat, thermal and windproof gloves, waterproof gaiters, trekking kit, 25 L pack with rain cover, sunscreen, headlamp, sneakers. Explora provides poles and a canteen.

**FAQ** (/tierra-del-fuego-expedition-questions/):
- **Included:** the accommodation and transfers in the itinerary, meals, beverages (Chilean wines included) and guided explorations.
- **Getting there:** fly Santiago to Punta Arenas (about 4 h). Same-day arrivals are picked up from 10 am.
- **Connectivity:** intermittent signal until day 3, and no free Wi-Fi (emergency satellite Wi-Fi and phone only).
- **Luggage:** at most 1 suitcase, up to 20 kg, because the return plane is small.
- **Return flight:** included; if weather grounds it, return by land (6–10 h extra), so leave a margin.
- **Changes:** itinerary fixed except for season or weather.
- **Who it suits:** active travelers able to hike up to 10 km on mud, rock, snow and peat. The overview: "A moderate level of physical fitness is recommended"; the dates page: "Suitable for all fitness levels".
- **Lodging:** options are "very limited"; night 1 is a small colonial-house hotel in Punta Arenas (Yegua Loca). Satellite phone carried in one vehicle for emergencies.
- **Comfort:** "It won't be the same as going to an Explora" (basic). **Food:** local menu (spit-roast lamb); flag intolerances in advance.
- **Emergencies:** care in Porvenir or Punta Arenas. Bring all your medications.

## Where the site disagrees with itself
- **Expedition cancellation:** the TdF FAQ and the Sacred Mountains FAQ ("BOOKING AND CANCELATION POLICIES") both say free 60+ days, 50% at 59–30 and 100% at 29–0. /payment-and-cancellation-policies/ says free 120+ days, 50% at 119–60 and 100% at 59–0, plus the non-refundable fee. Quote the policy page and suggest confirming by enquiry.
- **Tierra del Fuego price:** USD $10,950 (overview, hub) vs "From USD $10,500" (/expeditions-call-out-homepage/, an old block).
- **Tierra del Fuego return flight:** "approximate duration of 1 hour" (itinerary, FAQ) vs "approximately 30 minutes" (/tierra-del-fuego-itinenary/, the Echo Society copy).
- **Tierra del Fuego fitness:** "Suitable for all travelers" vs "moderate level of physical fitness" (overview), "active individuals" (FAQ) and day 5 "advanced intensity".
- **Sacred Mountains dates:** /sacred-mountain-expedition/sacred-mountains-expedition-departure-dates/ lists nine 2026 departures (May 17–25 to Sept 5–13, all sold out) and shows May 29–June 6, 2027 as **Available**. The linked page lists three 2026 departures and shows that date as **Sold out**.
- **Sacred Mountains maximum altitude:** 4,683 m / 15,350 ft (FAQ) vs 4,671 m / 15,324 ft (itinerary, 2027 post).
- **Stale copies:** the Echo Society TdF pages say the trip "will be taken only once" for members. The hub links /sacred-mountain-expedition-overview/, but the live overview is /sacred-mountain-expedition/.
