# Explora Parque Nacional Patagonia (Patagonia National Park, Chile)

Sources: L3/lodges.json (lodge entry with all six sub-page tabs, rooms, 30 exploration detail pages); /lodge/patagonia-national-park/; /patagonia-national-park-lodge/; /explorations/chile-patagonia-national-park/; /explorations-lodge/patagonia-national-park/; /cuisine-patagonia-national-park/; /how-to-get-to-patagonia-national-park/ (tabs "How to Get Here", "Pick-up & Drop-Off", "Check-in & Check-out"); /patagonia-national-park-travel-tips/; /frequently-asked-questions-patagonia-national-park/; /program/explora-connects-patagonia/; /destinations/; /2026/09/17/how-to-sequence-explora-lodges-south-america-journey/; reservations.json.

## The lodge
- **Chacabuco Valley**, just off the **Carretera Austral**, inside Patagonia National Park, **Aysén Region**, Chile. Coordinates 47° 7'1.88"S – 72°29'6.57"O.
- The park was created in **2018** by the conservation effort of **Douglas and Kristine Tompkins**; Explora continues "the work began by the Tompkins Conservation Foundation". The sequencing post: "the only lodge within the park's boundaries".
- Opened **2021**. **13 rooms**; locally sourced stone, reclaimed wood, recycled copper roofs.
- Awards: World's Leading Lodge 2025, South America's Leading Lodge 2025, Condé Nast Readers' Choice 2025 (overview); World's Leading Lodge 2024 and the Condé Nast award "consecutively for the last four years" (lodge page).
- **Spa** with massages and **three outdoor hot tubs**. **Exploration Center** onsite (maps, flora and fauna, guided activities, also for independent park visitors).
- Wildlife: guanacos, endangered huemul deer, ñandú, condors, armadillos, foxes, flamingos, sometimes pumas.
- Included: accommodations, all meals and beverages, all explorations, spa, park entrance fees, round-trip airport transfers.
- **Booking code EPNP** ("Parque Nacional Patagonia, Chile").

## Rooms
- **5 Suites**: 35–42 m² (376–452 ft²). **8 Standard Rooms**: 25–32 m² (269–344 ft²). All have park views, sleep 2, 1 king or 2 twin beds.
- Heating (no air conditioning), hair dryer, non-smoking. Extra bed for children, crib and interconnected family rooms are "not available in all rooms", subject to availability. **Laundry not included.**

## Explorations
- "30-plus guided Explorations included with your stay" (EXPLORATIONS tab); the archive and detail pages list **30**, "30 options to explore across 4 distinct zones". Groups of no more than eight, "often fewer"; planned each afternoon with your guide. Signature Explorations (Explora-only) and Classic routes.
- **Zones**: Waters (rivers and lakes; huemul habitat), Veranadas (old high summer pastures, restored by Rewilding Chile), The Uplands (above the forest line), The Historic Valley.
- **By activity**: Hike 11, Overland 5, E-Bike 5, High Mountain Ascent 3, By air 2, By water 2, Combination 2. **By difficulty**: Easy 12, Moderate 11, Advanced 4, Expert 3. **Duration**: 17 half-day, 13 full-day.
- Examples (difficulty, duration, distance/time where given):
  - Easy: La Vega (introductory hike from the lodge, 5.2 km, 2 h 30 m); Laguna Cisnes (birds, 6.1 km); El Furioso (4.6 km, 3 h); Confluencia (Baker and Chacabuco rivers, 1 h 40 m); Aguas Claras (boat on the Cochrane River); La Valchac (overland, full-day, 144 km, 7 h); Viaje Glaciar to the Calluqueo Glacier.
  - Moderate: Lago Gutierrez (Aonikenk paths, 6.6 km, 4 h 30 m); Los Pumas (steppe "most populated by pumas", 10.9 km); Tres Puentes, Gran Pasarela, Laguna La Pepa (e-bike, 19–24 km); Borde Río and Las Correntadas (boat or kayak plus hike, full-day); Cañadón Chacabuco (16.5 km).
  - Advanced: Caminantes (to Lake Cochrane, 19.8 km, 9 h); Lagunas Altas; La Tejuela (e-bike, 41.8 km); El Encuentro (ascent, 25.7 km, 11 h).
  - Expert: Sierra Guadal (e-bike, 43.9 km, gain 1,292 m); Oportus (ascent to 2,073 m, 10 h); La Leona (ascent to 2,215 m, gain 1,967 m, 10 h 30 m).
  - By air / by water: Valles Glaciares and Northern Patagonian Ice Field (helicopter over Laguna San Rafael National Park; Easy, half-day); Fly Fishing in the Baker River (cataraft from Puerto Bertrand, 5 h). The EXPLORATIONS tab calls fly fishing and ice-field flights "optional experiences at an additional cost".
- Explora provides trekking poles, bike helmets and a bottle of water (tips); walking sticks and bike equipment at no extra cost (FAQ).

## Dining (/cuisine-patagonia-national-park/)
Dishes "inspired by the gaucho legacy": organic produce from sustainable local farms, meats and vegetables on the plancha. All included: breakfast, lunch and dinner, all beverages, wine and an open bar, plus meals and snacks on full-day Explorations. Buffet-style lunches and daily dishes; in the evening a special menu centred on grilled meats and vegetables; a children's menu. Chilean wines in the dining room and tastings in the bar and lounge.

## Getting here
- Fly to **Balmaceda (BBA)**; Sky and LATAM fly daily from Santiago. 288 km, about **5 hours** by road.
- **Scheduled van**: once a day at **9:45 am** from Balmaceda, no extra cost on all-inclusive programs, mostly gravel, with scenic stops. **Road works** close the Carretera Austral several hours each afternoon until further notice, so land **by 9:15 am**. Otherwise spend a night in **Coyhaique** (pick-up 08:00–09:00 am, coordinated the day before).
- **Departure**: to Balmaceda for flights from **3:00 pm** onwards.
- **Charter from Balmaceda** plus a shorter drive: from **USD 1,969 pp one way (4-night stay) / USD 2,592 (3-night stay)**.
- **Lodge-to-lodge flight with Torres del Paine**: 1.5 hours over the Andes and southern ice field, Mondays and Thursdays, October 3rd to mid-April; from **USD 3,384 pp one way (4-night stay) / USD 4,925 (3-night stay)**. Both flights: soft suitcase up to 20 kg; reserve@explora.com.
- **Extension**: 2 days/1 night on the Carretera Austral (Cerro Castillo, Marble Caves) before arrival, /extended-castillo/.
- Vehicles up to 12 passengers. Distances: Balmaceda 288 km (5 h), Coyhaique 324 km (7 h), Puerto Río Tranquilo 109 km (2:30 h).

## Check-in and check-out
Check-in after 13:00 (arrive at noon for an afternoon exploration); check-out 10:00. For a departure-day half-day exploration, check out at 07:00 and leave luggage. Departure-day lunch costs extra. Late check-out on request.

## Best time and climate
- Winds year-round; sun, rain and wind in one day.
- Spring and summer (October–March): 10–20 °C (50–68 °F), long days. Fall (from end of March): red and gold foliage. Winter: calmer winds, short days, -5 to 5 °C (23–41 °F).
- Stay **at least five nights**; six is "a great alternative".

## Travel tips
- **Pack**: four layers (thermal, fleece, down, waterproof jacket and trousers), sun and warm hats, hiking shoes, gloves, sunglasses, sunscreen, buff, 25-liter backpack; optional swimsuit.
- **220V**. Limited Wi-Fi. **No cell coverage**; IP landline for guests.
- Tips voluntary, at reception (amount: see disagreements).
- Guides are WFR certified; nearest hospital **Cochrane**, 40 minutes; Coyhaique (6 hours) for complex cases. Relatives can email recpnp@explora.com (phone not quoted).
