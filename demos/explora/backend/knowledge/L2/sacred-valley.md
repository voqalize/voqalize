# Sacred Valley and Machu Picchu: Explora Valle Sagrado

Sources: /lodge/travel-machupicchu-and-the-sacred-valley-of-the-incas-peru-explora/ (overview + FAQs); /machu-picchu-lodge-peru-new/ (lodge + FAQs); /machu-picchu-hotels/ (rooms); /machu-picchu-hiking-tours/ (explorations); /explorations-lodge/machu-picchu-sacred-valley/ (archive, page 1 of 4); /getting-there-how-to-get-to-sacred-valley-from-cusco/; /machu-picchu-travel-tips/; /frequently-asked-questions-sacred-valley/; /2026/09/18/explora-valle-sagrado-family-stay/; /2026/09/17/how-to-sequence-explora-lodges-south-america-journey/; /awards-page/; /press-page/; /corporate-travel-program/; L3/reservations.json.

## The lodge
- **Explora Valle Sagrado**, Sacred Valley of the Incas, Peru; booking code **EVAL**. A historic 32-hectare estate near Urquillos on "a remote ancient Incan corn plantation", between Pisac and Ollantaytambo. Address: Salabella-Silvayoc Farm s/n, Urubamba; coordinates 13º20'31.43"S – 72º2'52.59"W.
- Known for Inca sites (Ollantaytambo, Pisac, Moray, Maras, Chinchero), Machu Picchu and Cusco full days, and hacienda-inspired architecture by **José Cruz Ovalle** (Chile's National Architecture Award 2012).
- **Pumacahua Bath House** (17th-century manor restored with the Archaeology Institute of Peru): spa, saunas, steam bath, outdoor pool, two hot tubs. Events space seats up to 70.
- Awards: Peru's Leading Lodge 2025; Condé Nast Traveler Readers' Choice 2025 (overview: "Best Resorts in the World"; /awards-page/: "South America's #1 Resort"); "five-time" Readers' Choice winner; Travelers' Choice 2025 Best of the Best; Condé Nast Gold List 2022 (/press-page/). TripAdvisor: "5.0 Excellent 696 reviews".
- **Included** (overview box): accommodations, all Explorations, spa facilities, meals and beverages, park fees, round-trip airport transfers. Per the FAQs, **alcohol is not included**; spa treatments, private guiding, Machu Picchu permits/train, phone calls and laundry cost extra.

## Rooms (/machu-picchu-hotels/, /machu-picchu-lodge-peru-new/)
50 rooms: **44 Standard Rooms and 6 suites**, all with corn-field and mountain views.
- **Standard Room:** 430 ft²; 1 king or 2 twin beds, extra child bed*, crib*, heating, jetted bathtub, iPhone dock, local infusions, non-smoking.
- **Suite:** 570 ft², 1 king, same amenities. Interconnecting rooms for families. *Subject to availability.
- **No air conditioning** ("not necessary": 9°C–22°C, natural ventilation). Bed type chosen at booking.

## Explorations
- **More than 40** included Explorations (also "49", see below); half and full days, groups of **no more than eight**; School of Guides guides, Spanish and English, plan the next day with you each evening. **Private guiding costs extra.**
- **Four zones**, by altitude: **Quechua** (the lodge's zone, along the Urubamba; Pisac to Machu Picchu; Pumamarca fortress; the recommended starting point); **Suni** (transition zone; Moray, the Salineras de Maras, Tipón's canals); **Puna** (shepherd trails, glaciers); **Janca** (the highest; needs special acclimatization, steep and technical).
- Activities: Hike, Overland, Bike, E-Bike, Climbing, Combination, High Mountain Ascent, Self Guided; difficulty Easy to Expert.
- Archive examples: 9 Apus (e-bike, advanced, Moray to Maras); Wiraqocha (e-bike, easy, Zurite terraces); Full Day Inca Trail (hike, moderate, Qhapaq Ñan to Machu Picchu); Wilkamayu (rafting, easy); Pachar (climbing, moderate); Yanahuara (easy hike, hummingbird reserve); Qenqo (bike, expert); Machu-Picchu, Cusco, Valle Sur (overland, easy, full day); Pisac (half day).
- Family post: easy Huertos de Yucay and Arin; moderate Salineras and Moray. The **Pachar via ferrata and zipline is not included** (third party, extra cost; book ahead).
- Gear provided free: trekking poles and cycling equipment.

## Machu Picchu
- A full-day overland Exploration with a ~three-hour guided tour: train Ollantaytambo → Aguas Calientes (~1.5–2 h), then bus (~30 min). Explora coordinates train, timed permit (daily caps) and guiding.
- Cost "may" be additional depending on program (Explora plus third-party services); see disagreements.
- The 9-day Sacred Mountains Expedition (departs from Explora Valle Sagrado, ends at Machu Picchu) is in topic `expeditions`.

## Dining
A menu that "celebrates Andean culinary traditions" with Sacred Valley and Peruvian ingredients. "MORE DETAILS" links to /cuisine-by-virgilio-martinez-chef/ (not crawled). Wine, beer and spirits are charged separately.

## Getting there (/getting-there-how-to-get-to-sacred-valley-from-cusco/ + FAQs)
- Fly Lima → **Cusco** (CUZ, Alejandro Velasco Astete), about 1 h 20 min. The lodge is about 90 km away, **1.5 to 2 hours** by van (seats 10), included in programs with transfer and timed to your flights.
- Not from the airport: pick-up at **11:00** from certain Cusco/Sacred Valley hotels (max 15 min wait); check-out drop-offs to Cusco hotels at **10:00, 12:30, 15:00** (no lunch). Private transfer extra (reserve@explora.com); flight changes under 24 h may add costs.
- **Check-in after 13:00; check-out 10:00**; departure-day half-day Exploration possible (check out 07:00, bags stored, no lunch).
- "600 meters below Cusco", so starting here eases acclimatization.

## Altitude
Lodge **9,514 ft (2,900 m)**; Machu Picchu 7,970 ft (2,430 m); Cusco about 11,200 ft (3,400 m). Most acclimatize in one to two days; hydrate, skip alcohol on arrival, rest the first afternoon; altitude-sensitive guests should see a physician.

## Climate and best time
- Travel tips: **dry season April–October** (sun, night frosts; July coldest, avg 49.5°F/9.7°C); **rainy season November–March** (avg 55.9°F/13.3°C, moderate showers); year range 32.4°F–69.6°F (0.2°C–20.9°C). Overview FAQ: June–August is peak demand; November–April has fewer crowds.
- Book four to six months ahead, especially May–August and December–January (lodge FAQ).

## Travel tips
- **How long:** a minimum of **five nights**, or six for a deeper stay.
- **Pack:** sun hat, wool cap, four layers incl. waterproof trousers and a warm jacket, hiking gear, gloves, sunscreen, swimsuit, and a **20-litre backpack max** (larger not allowed in Machu Picchu); optional insect repellent.
- **220V.** Wi-Fi limited. At the lodge only "Claro" has good mobile coverage.
- **Medical:** none in Urquillos; Urubamba (a few km) has a hospital and two clinics; severe cases go to Lima.
- Families are welcome (not adults-only). Emergency contact email: recvallesagrado@explora.com.
