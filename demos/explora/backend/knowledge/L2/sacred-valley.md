## The lodge
- **Explora Valle Sagrado**, Sacred Valley of the Incas, Peru; booking code **EVAL**. A historic 32-hectare estate near Urquillos on "a remote ancient Incan corn plantation", between Pisac and Ollantaytambo.
- Known for Inca sites (Ollantaytambo, Pisac, Moray, Maras, Chinchero), Machu Picchu and Cusco full days, and hacienda-inspired architecture by **José Cruz Ovalle** (Chile's National Architecture Award 2012).
- **Pumacahua Bath House** (17th-century manor restored with the Archaeology Institute of Peru): spa, saunas, steam bath, outdoor pool, two hot tubs.
- Awards: Peru's Leading Lodge 2025; Condé Nast Traveler Readers' Choice 2025 (overview: "Best Resorts in the World"; /awards-page/: "South America's #1 Resort"); "five-time" Readers' Choice winner; Condé Nast Gold List 2022 (/press-page/).
- **Included** (overview box): accommodations, all Explorations, spa facilities, meals and beverages, park fees, round-trip airport transfers. Alcohol: the DINING tab says drinks are included, the FAQs say not (see disagreements). Spa treatments, private guiding, Machu Picchu permits/train, phone calls and laundry cost extra.

## Explorations (EXPLORATIONS tab, /explorations-lodge/machu-picchu-sacred-valley/, 47 detail pages)
- "Over 40" / "49" included Explorations, half and full days, groups of **no more than eight**; guides from the School of Guides, "fluent in Spanish and English", tailor the next day with you **every evening**. **Private guiding costs extra.**
- The crawl holds **47** exploration pages: Hike 23, Overland 9, Bike 5, Self Guided 4, E-Bike 2, Combination 2, Climbing 1, High Mountain Ascent 1; Easy 23, Moderate 15, Advanced 6, Expert 3; Full-day 25, Half-day 22.
- **Four zones** by altitude: **Quechua** (19; the lodge's zone along the Urubamba, Pisac to Machu Picchu, Pumamarca; "the starting point"); **Suni** (16; Moray, Salineras de Maras, Chinchero); **Puna** (11; high trails and lagoons above 4,000 m); **Janca** (1; the highest, special acclimatization).
- Examples: **Machu-Picchu** (overland, easy, full day, 18 h); **Cusco** and **Valle Sur** (overland full days); **Pisac**, **Chinchero**, **Moray y Salineras** (easy half days by van); **Ollantaytambo**, **Huertos de Yucay**, **Arin** (easy hikes); **Salineras** and **Kaytucha** (moderate); **Full Day Inca Trail** (moderate, 12.9 km on the Qhapaq Ñan to Machu Picchu); **5 Lagunas** and **La Verónica** (moderate Puna hikes, up to ~4,400 m); **Huchuy Qosqo** (advanced, 17.3 km); **Pitusiray** (expert high-mountain ascent to 5,012 m, 13 h 40); **9 Apus** (Signature e-bike, advanced) and **Qenqo** (expert bike, 54.5 km); **Pachar** (climbing, moderate); self-guided walks from the lodge.
- Family post: the **Pachar via ferrata and zipline is not included** (third party, extra cost; book ahead).
- Gear provided free: trekking poles and cycling equipment.

## Machu Picchu
- A full-day overland Exploration with a ~three-hour guided tour: train Ollantaytambo → Aguas Calientes (~1.5–2 h), then bus (~30 min). Explora coordinates train, timed permit (daily caps) and guiding.
- Cost "may" be additional depending on program (Explora plus third-party services); see disagreements.
- The Sacred Mountains Expedition: topic `expeditions-south-america`.

## Dining (DINING tab, /gastronomy-by-explora-valle-sagrado/)
Products from the valley and local producers plus ingredients from the coast, highlands and Amazon; menus rotate through the stay; lunch and dinner offer a choice of starters, mains and desserts. Examples: pachamanca a la olla, alpaca carpaccio, scallop tiradito, lomo saltado, roasted paiche, suspiro a la limeña. Tell the lodge about dietary restrictions before arrival. The tab says every meal is included (plus meals and snacks on full-day explorations), "all beverages, a selection of wines and an open bar" — pisco sour, chicha morada, wine.

## Getting there (/getting-there-how-to-get-to-sacred-valley-from-cusco/ + FAQs)
- Fly Lima → **Cusco** (CUZ, Alejandro Velasco Astete), about 1 h 20 min. The lodge is about 90 km away, **1.5 to 2 hours** by van (seats 10), included in programs with transfer and timed to your flights.
- Not from the airport: pick-up at **11:00** from certain Cusco/Sacred Valley hotels (max 15 min wait); check-out drop-offs to Cusco hotels at **10:00, 12:30, 15:00** (no lunch). Private transfer extra (reserve@explora.com); flight changes under 24 h may add costs.
- **Check-in after 13:00; check-out 10:00**; departure-day half-day Exploration possible (check out 07:00, bags stored, no lunch).
- "600 meters below Cusco", so starting here eases acclimatization.

## Altitude
Lodge **9,514 ft (2,900 m)**; Machu Picchu 7,970 ft (2,430 m); Cusco about 11,200 ft (3,400 m). Most acclimatize in one to two days; hydrate and rest the first afternoon.

## Climate and best time
- Travel tips: **dry season April–October** (sun, night frosts; July coldest, avg 49.5°F/9.7°C); **rainy season November–March** (avg 55.9°F/13.3°C, moderate showers).
- Book four to six months ahead, especially May–August and December–January (lodge FAQ).

## Travel tips
- **How long:** "We recommend a minimum of five nights to truly experience the Sacred Valley"; six for a deeper stay.
- **Pack:** sun hat, wool cap, layers incl. waterproof trousers and a warm jacket, hiking gear, sunscreen, swimsuit, and a **20-litre backpack max** (larger not allowed in Machu Picchu).
- **220V.** Wi-Fi limited. At the lodge only "Claro" has good mobile coverage.
- **Medical:** none in Urquillos; Urubamba (a few km) has a hospital and two clinics; severe cases go to Lima.
- Families are welcome. Emergency contact: recvallesagrado@explora.com.
