# What an all-inclusive stay includes

Sources: https://www.explora.com/ (section "What's Included in Your Stay"); the "Included with Your Stay" block on each lodge overview (/el-chalten-lodge/, /lodge/torres-del-paine-lodge/, /lodge/easter-island-hotels/, /lodge/atacama-lodge/ and others); /explora-hotels/ (tab "Our Lodges"); /lodge/travel-machupicchu-and-the-sacred-valley-of-the-incas-peru-explora/ and /machu-picchu-lodge-peru-new/ (FAQ); /easter-island-travel-tips-new/ (FAQ); /torres-del-paine-lodge-conservation-reserve/; /travel-tips-el-calafate-argentina/; /lodge/torres-del-paine-lodge/; /patagonia-national-park-lodge/; /corporate-travel-program/; /2026/08/27/solo-travel-set-your-own-pace/; /payment-and-cancellation-policies/; the getting-here pages.

## The standard list
Home page: "Explora's lodges are all-inclusive, so you don't have to worry about anything but exploring and connecting deeply with the territory." The same six items appear on the home page and on every lodge overview's "Included with Your Stay" block:
1. **Accommodations**
2. **All Explorations** (guided excursions — see `explorations`)
3. **All Meals and Beverages**
4. **Spa Facilities**
5. **National Park Entrance Fees**
6. **Round-trip Airport Transfers** (at pre-established times; see `getting-there-and-transfers`)

Solo-travel post: "Your stay includes accommodations, daily Explorations, and dining, along with Signature Explorations, designed and operated exclusively by Explora." /explora-hotels/: "Everything is included throughout the entire experience"; amenities "like massages, jacuzzis, and pools".

Lodge-specific wording:
- **Torres del Paine Conservation Reserve** (/torres-del-paine-lodge-conservation-reserve/): all meals and beverages, "Patagonian-inspired cuisine including traditional asados prepared by reserve gauchos, paired with Chilean wines"; spa facilities (outdoor hot tubs and massage room); National Park entrance fees; round-trip transfers from Puerto Natales according to preset schedules.
- **El Calafate** (/travel-tips-el-calafate-argentina/): all meals and drinks with Patagonian wines; outdoor hot tubs; park fees; airport transfers on preset schedules. "The only things not included are flights, optional private transfers, and personal expenses."
- **Easter Island** (/easter-island-travel-tips-new/ FAQ "What is not included"): the all-inclusive rate covers accommodations, all guided Explorations, all meals and beverages, spa facility access and more; not included are personal purchases, gratuities and optional spa treatments beyond general facility access. Premium or specialty drink selections beyond the standard offering may cost extra.
- **Offers' wording** (e.g. /offer/10-for-2026-bookings/): "Accommodation at our Lodge. Exclusive Explorations, led by expert guides. All meals, wine and bar. Airport transfers at pre-established times."

## What costs extra (as stated on the site)
- **Individual spa treatments / massages**: "available to book separately and carry an additional charge" (/lodge/torres-del-paine-lodge/); same at Sacred Valley (Pumacahua Spa). Spa *facilities* (pools, hot tubs, saunas) are included.
- **Privately guided Explorations**: "available at an additional cost" (/torres-del-paine-tours/; Atacama FAQ; Sacred Valley).
- **Private transfers** outside the scheduled times, at every lodge.
- **Lunch on check-out day** is not included.
- **Machu Picchu** (Sacred Valley): a third-party exploration with an extra cost, not available on two-night stays (see `sacred-valley`, `booking-and-policies`).
- **Explora Connects** lodge-to-lodge flights and the cross-border vans (see `getting-there-and-transfers`).
- **Laundry** (/patagonia-national-park-lodge/: "Laundry service is not included"; corporate program list).
- **Third-party activities** such as diving or a helicopter flight (named in the Honeymoon offer's exclusions), taxis to Hanga Roa or beaches on Easter Island, horseback riding on Easter Island via third parties.
- **Flights** to the destinations are never included.
- **Gratuities** are not included; the site suggests tips (amounts differ by page — see the lodge topics).

## Corporate programs (/corporate-travel-program/)
Include: transfers at pre-established times; accommodation; 3 meals a day plus snacks and open bar; two half-day explorations or a full-day exploration with Explora guides; horse and bicycle equipment; trekking sticks; tickets to reserves, parks and archaeological sites except Machu Picchu; thematic talks; pools, hot tubs and social areas; Wi-Fi in common areas. Do not include: laundry, massages, telephone calls, wines from the special selection.

## Wi-Fi
Because the lodges are remote, Wi-Fi is limited or intermittent (FAQ and travel-tips pages for Atacama, Torres del Paine, Patagonia National Park, Sacred Valley, Uyuni).

## Where the site disagrees with itself
- **Alcohol at Explora Valle Sagrado**: /machu-picchu-lodge-peru-new/ FAQ "Does Explora Valle Sagrado include alcohol?" says "Alcoholic beverages are not included in the standard all-inclusive rate … Wine, beer, and spirits are available at the bar and restaurant and charged separately." Every lodge overview, the Sacred Valley overview included, lists "All Meals and Beverages", and offer pages say "All meals, wine and bar". Do not promise drinks are included at Sacred Valley; suggest confirming with reserve@explora.com.
- **Special wine selection**: the corporate program excludes "Wines from the special selection"; the Echo Society Guardian/Custodian tiers give "a bottle of wine from our special selection" as a perk — implying it is normally extra. Lodge pages do not say.
- **Transfers**: "Round-trip Airport Transfers" are listed as included everywhere, but the getting-here pages qualify them as "Exclusive for programs with transfer included" / "Depending on the program", and at scheduled times only.
