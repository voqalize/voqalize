## What a "journey" is on the site
The lodge pages show suggested combinations of two to four lodges as cards: a name, a line, the length ("Starting at 8 nights", "Starting at 12 days"), the lodges, and a "Starting at $X USD / total per person" price, with a "Check Availability" or "VIEW DETAILS" button. There is no separate journey booking: a combined trip is booked lodge by lodge on reservations.explora.com (the room pop-up offers "Add another Explora destination") or through reserve@explora.com, and the Explora Passport discount applies (see `offers-and-programs`). Prices are "starting at" figures as printed on the pages; actual rates vary by date.

## Explora Passport ideas (/explora-passport/)
- South American must-sees: Torres del Paine + Easter Island + Sacred Valley / Machu Picchu.
- Ancestral lands: Torres del Paine + Atacama + Sacred Valley / Machu Picchu.
- Full immersion: Torres del Paine + Easter Island + Atacama + Sacred Valley / Machu Picchu.
- Full Patagonia (10%): Torres del Paine + Patagonia National Park + El Calafate.

## Atacama + Torres del Paine in 12–14 days (blog, 2026-07-10)
Begin at Explora Atacama for 4 or 5 nights, travel south through Santiago toward Punta Arenas, continue to Explora Torres del Paine for 5 or 6 nights; the order is yours. Recommended stays: Atacama "4 nights minimum, 5 for more room"; Torres del Paine "5 nights to start, 6 or 7". Atacama can be visited year-round (clearest stargazing nights June–August; January–March altiplano winter storms may affect higher explorations). Torres del Paine is open year-round: October–April high season, May–September low season.

## Sequencing the nine lodges (blog, 2026-09-17)
"9 lodges across 4 countries, Chile, Peru, Argentina and Bolivia." The common instinct is to "start dry and finish frozen": Valle Sagrado (gentlest entry, and acclimatization because the valley floor is well below Cusco) → Atacama → the Travesía to Uyuni (Explora Jirira Lodge on the salt flat's edge) → Rapa Nui as "a deliberate detour, taken before the desert or after the ice" → Torres del Paine, the new Torres del Paine Conservation Reserve lodge (10 rooms, opens December 2026), Patagonia National Park → El Chaltén → El Calafate (20 rooms, close to the Perito Moreno Glacier). "There's no required order here."

## Moving between Patagonian lodges (/program/explora-connects-patagonia/)
Explora Connects Patagonia: Torres del Paine ↔ Patagonia National Park by 1.5-hour charter flight; Balmaceda Airport ↔ Patagonia National Park by 1-hour charter flight; Torres del Paine ↔ El Chaltén by 8-hour van. "All-inclusive programs from 8 nights" (/program/). Schedules and rates from reserve@explora.com. Details per route in `getting-there-and-transfers`.

## Where the site disagrees with itself
- **Same route, two prices:** Torres del Paine + Patagonia National Park is "Chilean Patagonia … $7,000" on the Torres del Paine and PNP pages but "Pristine Patagonia … $9,680" on the El Chaltén page.
- **Similar names, different trips:** "Lands Of Contrasts" (12 nights, 3 lodges, $9,290, Easter Island page) vs "South America: Land of Contrasts" (8 nights, 2 lodges, $6,440, Sacred Valley page).
- **Nights vs days:** the same Patagonia cards say "Starting at 8 nights" on the Torres del Paine page and "From 8 days" on the El Chaltén page.
- **Atacama minimum stay:** 4 nights (2026 blog) vs five nights (Atacama FAQ and travel tips).
- **How many lodges:** the blog says 9 lodges in 4 countries (counting Jirira in Bolivia); the home page says "nine lodges across Chile, Easter Island, Argentina, and Peru; one overland Travesía into Bolivia"; /explora-jobs/ says "seven destinations across four countries".
- **Passport discount on lodge pages:** "up to 25% off on each additional destination" (lodge pages) vs 25% for Andean destinations and only 10% for Patagonia combinations (/explora-passport/).
