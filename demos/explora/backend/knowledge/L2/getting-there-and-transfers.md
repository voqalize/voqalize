# Getting there and transfers (all lodges)

Sources: https://www.explora.com/transfers-information/ (and the links behind it, fetched live 2026-10-21); /atacama-getting-there/; /how-to-get-to-uyuni/; /torres-del-paine-getting-there/; /getting-here-torres-del-paine-conservation-reserve/; /how-to-get-to-patagonia-national-park/; /how-to-get-el-chalten/; /how-to-get-el-calafate/; /getting-there-how-to-get-to-sacred-valley-from-cusco/; /getting-there-how-to-get-to-easter-island/; /program/explora-connects-patagonia/; /frequently-asked-questions-atacama/.

## The rule
Round-trip airport transfers are included, "at pre-established times" and "exclusive for programs with transfer included". Outside the schedule, a **private transfer** costs extra (ask reserve@explora.com before arrival). A flight change within 24 hours of check-in that needs a special transfer may cost extra. Flights are never included. /transfers-information/ lists one GETTING HERE page per lodge: Lodge Atacama (/atacama-getting-there/), Travesía Atacama Uyuni (/salar-de-uyuni-information/), Lodge Torres del Paine (/torres-del-paine-getting-there/), Lodge Parque Nacional Patagonia (/how-to-get-to-patagonia-national-park/), Lodge El Chaltén (/how-to-get-el-chalten/), Lodge Torres del Paine Conservation Reserve (/getting-here-torres-del-paine-conservation-reserve/), Lodge El Calafate (/how-to-get-el-calafate/), Lodge Valle Sagrado & Machu Picchu (/getting-there-how-to-get-to-sacred-valley-from-cusco/), Lodge Isla de Pascua (/getting-there-how-to-get-to-easter-island/).

## Check-in and check-out
Check-in 13:00 (arrive at noon for an afternoon exploration), check-out 10:00; lunch not included on check-out day. A half-day exploration on departure day is possible at several lodges with a 07:00 check-out.

## Between lodges
- **Explora Connects Patagonia** (/program/explora-connects-patagonia/): Torres del Paine ↔ Patagonia National Park, 1.5-hour charter flight "over the Andes Mountain Range and the southern ice field", every Monday and Thursday from October 3rd to mid-April, soft suitcase up to 20 kg; Balmaceda ↔ Patagonia National Park 1-hour charter flight; Torres del Paine ↔ El Chaltén by 8-hour van. Contact reserve@explora.com for schedules and rates.
- Prices printed on getting-here pages (per person, one way): TdP Conservation Reserve page "start at USD 2,352 … for a 4-night stay and USD 3,420 for a 3-night stay"; Patagonia National Park page for the Torres del Paine flight "USD 3,384 … 4-night stay and USD 4,925 … 3-night stay"; the Balmaceda charter alternative "USD 1,969 … 4-night stay and USD 2,592 … 3-night stay".
- **Vans Chile ↔ Argentina** (extra cost, book ahead, October–April): Torres del Paine ↔ El Chaltén 402 km (250 miles), about 8–9 hours; Torres del Paine ↔ El Calafate 374 km (232 miles), about 5–6 hours; both depart 9:00 a.m. for the border crossing; private (up to six) or shared (minimum two). El Chaltén ↔ El Calafate by van on select days October–April, about 3.5 hours.
- **Patagonia National Park extension**: an optional two-day, one-night private Cerro Castillo & Marble Caves extension along the Carretera Austral (/extended-castillo/).
- **Travesía return to Atacama**: a private leg from the Uyuni lodge to the Ollagüe border (max 2 people, lunch included, extra cost).

## Notices on the pages (as of the crawl)
- Puerto Natales Airport "will undergo maintenance work from January through March 2027 and will operate on a reduced schedule".
- Road works on the Carretera Austral close the route "for several hours each afternoon, until further notice" — hence the 9:45 am Balmaceda departure.
- Flights to Puerto Natales operate only on certain days of the week.
- Bolivia visa rules for the Travesía are on /how-to-get-to-uyuni/ (tab "VISA to Bolivia"); travelers needing a visa are advised to get it at a Bolivian consulate before travelling.

## Where the site disagrees with itself
- **Puerto Natales → Torres del Paine**: "approximately 1.45 hours" (Chile tab) vs "two and a half hours" (Argentina tab) on /torres-del-paine-getting-there/; the TdP FAQ gives 112 km / 1.5–2 h and the FAQ page 140 km.
- **TdP ↔ El Chaltén van**: "8-hour" (Explora Connects) vs "approximately 8–9 hours"; "available daily at 9:00 a.m." (TdP and El Chaltén pages) vs "on select days of the week" (Conservation Reserve page).
- **TdP ↔ El Calafate**: "approximately six hours" (/how-to-get-el-calafate/) vs "5–6 hours".
- **Lodge-to-lodge flight price**: different figures on the Conservation Reserve page (from USD 2,352) and the Patagonia National Park page (from USD 3,384) for what both call the 1.5-hour lodge-to-lodge flight; the FAQ pages say only "with an additional cost".
- **Transfers included**: listed as included on every overview; the getting-here pages say "Exclusive for programs with transfer included" / "Depending on the program".
