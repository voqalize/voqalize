# Explora Torres del Paine Conservation Reserve (new 10-room lodge)

Sources: /lodge/torres-del-paine-conservation-reserve/ (overview, FAQs); /torres-del-paine-lodge-conservation-reserve/ (lodge, rooms, FAQs); /torres-del-paine-hiking-tours/ (explorations, four zones; its H1 is "Torres del Paine Conservation Reserve"); /explorations-lodge/reserva-torres-del-paine/ (exploration list); /getting-here-torres-del-paine-conservation-reserve/ (tabs); /destinations/; / (home, Latest News); /patagonia-travel-argentina-chile/; /2026/09/17/how-to-sequence-explora-lodges-south-america-journey/; reservations.explora.com (hotel list).

## The lodge
- A **new lodge** at the heart of Explora's private Torres del Paine Conservation Reserve, **on the edge of Torres del Paine National Park, not inside it** (lodge page and FAQ). "Off the map, away from the crowds"; "a more private and personal refuge than our other lodges".
- **Just ten rooms**, overlooking the Patagonian steppe and the Torres peaks. Outdoor hot tubs and a massage room. Stables with horses bred for the terrain and cared for by the reserve's gauchos; a quincho for traditional asados.
- The reserve is "nearly 15,000 acres (6,000 hectares)", part of the Torres del Paine Biosphere Reserve (see topic `conservation-reserves`).
- **Opening**: the booking site lists it under "New hotels (Opening Dec 2026)"; /2026/09/17/… says "a separate 10-room lodge, Explora Torres del Paine Conservation Reserve, opens in December 2026", a short distance northeast of the Lake Pehoé lodge "on restored ranch land". The home page says "Two New Lodges Opening 2026" (with Explora El Calafate).
- Awards shown on the overview: The Best Hotels & Resorts in the World: The Gold List 2025; World's Leading Expedition Company 2025; Conservation Sustainability with Soul.
- Booking code: **ERTP** ("Torres del Paine - Conservation Reserve, Chile", booking flow). Deep link: `https://reservations.explora.com/ibe/paso1?language=EN&hotel_code=ERTP`.
- Part of Explora's "Patagonia Network": "five lodges and one expedition", interconnected across Chile and Argentina.
- The lodge FAQ says it is open year-round.

## Included with your stay
Accommodations; all Explorations (guided excursions); all meals and beverages (Patagonian cuisine, asados by the reserve gauchos, Chilean wines); spa facilities (outdoor hot tubs and massage room); national park entrance fees; round-trip transfers from Puerto Natales on preset schedules. FAQ: "The only additional costs are flights, optional private transfers, and personal expenses."

## Rooms (/torres-del-paine-lodge-conservation-reserve/)
One room type card: "Rooms", views to the mountains or to the forest, **25 m²**, sleeps 2, 1 king or 2 twin beds, heating, hair dryer, wake-up call, non-smoking. No prices on the page; rates are on the booking site and vary by date.

## Explorations
- **30+** guided Explorations across **four zones**; the explorations page also says "Explore 36 explorations located in the 4 unique Torres del Paine C. Reserve zones". Groups limited to 8; bilingual guides trained at the School of Guides; planned each evening with your guide; also self-guided trails.
- Ways to explore: hike, overland, horseback ride, mountain ascent, self-guided.
- Zones: 1 **Macizo Paine** (Base Torres, Valle Francés, Glaciar Grey, overland and by boat across Lago Pehoé); 2 **Cuenca Paine** (southeastern steppe and lakes: Laguna Azul, Lago Sarmiento, Laguna Amarga, Vega Castillo wetland); 3 **La Reserva** (the private reserve: lagoons, valleys, former estancias); 4 **Sierra Baguales** (northeast of the reserve near the Chile–Argentina border; fossils, remote valleys; "one of the best regions in the park for observing pumas").
- Examples (/explorations-lodge/reserva-torres-del-paine/): Baguales (overland, easy, half-day, to Estancia Las Cumbres); Vuelta a Baguales and Cerro Redondo (mountain ascent, advanced, full-day); El Centinela (hike, advanced, full-day); Bandurria (hike, easy, half-day); Sierra Contreras (mountain ascent, advanced, full-day); Florence (horseback at trot and gallop, advanced, full-day, through "the three former estancias"); Tough Life (horseback, advanced, half-day); Cañadón Las Torres (horseback, easy, half-day); Into the Wild (hike, moderate, full-day, camera-trap research); Huellas de Las Chinas (hike, moderate, half-day, to a waterfall); Mirador del Obelisco (hike, moderate, half-day, back on foot to the lodge); Encuentros con el Puma (overland, easy, full-day, with external specialists, subject to availability); Estepa Patagónica (overland, easy, half-day).

## Dining
Seasonal menus rooted in Patagonian flavors and traditions, traditional asados at the quincho prepared by the reserve gauchos, ingredients locally sourced whenever possible, Chilean wines. The overview links a DINING page at /dining-torres-del-paine-reserve/ (not in the crawl).

## Getting there (/getting-here-torres-del-paine-conservation-reserve/)
- Fly Santiago → **Puerto Natales** (LATAM or Sky). Then about **two hours** by road, mostly paved, only 20 km (12.4 mi) of gravel.
- Free pick-ups: Puerto Natales Airport **12:00 and 15:00**; Puerto Natales hotels and port between 11:00 and 12:00 (driver waits max 15 minutes). Extra shuttles and private transfers cost more; arrange with your reservation executive or reserve@explora.com. Puerto Natales Airport has maintenance January–March 2027 with a reduced schedule.
- **From Patagonia National Park**: 1.5-hour lodge-to-lodge flight over the Andes and the southern ice field, Mondays and Thursdays from October 3rd to mid-April, "exclusive for programs with transfer included"; travel light with a soft suitcase up to 20 kg. The page states "Prices start at USD 2,352 per person, one way, for a 4-night stay and USD 3,420 for a 3-night stay."
- **Via Argentina**: Buenos Aires → El Calafate (about 3.5 hours' flight), then bus or transfer about four hours to the Chilean border at Cerro Castillo, where Explora picks you up. Transfers are scheduled around noon; other times need a private transfer.
- **El Calafate ↔ reserve**: van on select days October–April, private or shared (minimum 2), extra fee, booked in advance; 374 km (232 mi), about 5–6 hours. **El Chaltén ↔ reserve**: one-way for an extra fee for guests combining stays; 402 km (250 mi), about 8–9 hours. Explora is not responsible for border or road delays.

## Best time and climate (lodge FAQ)
"Wet Andes", volatile weather, "all four seasons in a single day". Summer (November–February): long days, best for Base Torres and Valle Francés. Autumn (March–May): golden and red foliage, calmer winds, fewer visitors. Winter (June–August): solitary, snow-dusted peaks. Spring (September–October): wildlife activity peaks.
