## The lodge
- A **new lodge** at the heart of Explora's private Torres del Paine Conservation Reserve, **on the edge of Torres del Paine National Park, not inside it** (lodge page and FAQ). "Off the map, away from the crowds"; "a more private and personal refuge than our other lodges".
- **Just ten rooms**, overlooking the Patagonian steppe and the Torres peaks. Outdoor hot tubs and a massage room. Stables with horses bred for the terrain and cared for by the reserve's gauchos; a quincho for traditional asados.
- The reserve is "nearly 15,000 acres (6,000 hectares)", part of the Torres del Paine Biosphere Reserve (see topic `conservation-reserves`).
- **Opening**: the booking site lists it under "New hotels (Opening Dec 2026)"; /2026/09/17/… says "a separate 10-room lodge, Explora Torres del Paine Conservation Reserve, opens in December 2026", a short distance northeast of the Lake Pehoé lodge "on restored ranch land". The home page says "Two New Lodges Opening 2026" (with Explora El Calafate).
- Overview awards: The Gold List 2025; World's Leading Expedition Company 2025; Conservation Sustainability with Soul.
- Booking code: **ERTP** ("Torres del Paine - Conservation Reserve, Chile", booking flow). Deep link: `https://reservations.explora.com/ibe/paso1?language=EN&hotel_code=ERTP`.
- The lodge FAQ says it is open year-round.

## Included with your stay
Accommodations; all Explorations (guided excursions); all meals and beverages (Patagonian cuisine, asados by the reserve gauchos, Chilean wines); spa facilities (outdoor hot tubs and massage room); national park entrance fees; round-trip transfers from Puerto Natales on preset schedules. FAQ: "The only additional costs are flights, optional private transfers, and personal expenses."

## Rooms (/torres-del-paine-lodge-conservation-reserve/)
One room type, "Rooms" (10 in all): views to the mountains or to the forest, **25 m²**, sleeps 2, 1 king or 2 twin beds; heating, hair dryer, wake-up call, non-smoking. Unlike the Lake Pehoé lodge's room cards, no jetted bathtub, extra bed or crib is listed. Rates are on the booking site and vary by date.

## Explorations
- "30+" guided Explorations in **four zones**; the list shows **36 cards**: **Hike 23, Horseback Ride 5, Overland 4, Mountain Ascent 4**; **Easy 17, Moderate 11, Advanced 8** (no Expert); 21 half-day, 15 full-day. Groups of max 8, bilingual guides from the School of Guides plus Explora park rangers and local gauchos; planned each evening; self-guided trail network.
- Zones: **La Reserva** (13; the private reserve: lagoons, valleys, three former estancias), **Cuenca Paine** (11; the park's quieter southeast: Laguna Azul, Lago Sarmiento, wetlands), **Sierra Baguales** (7; fossils, remote valleys near the border, "one of the best regions in the park for observing pumas"), **Macizo Paine** (5; Base Torres, Valle Francés, Glaciar Grey, crossing Lago Pehoé).
- Examples: **Mirador Masle** (hike, easy, 3.5 km, 1 h); **Laguna Larga** (easy, 1 h 30 m); **Fronteras** ride to Laguna Jara (easy, 1 h 45 m); **Huellas de las Chinas** (moderate, to a waterfall); **Into the Wild** (moderate, 17.2 km, camera-trap research); **Fosiles** (moderate, 13 km, Sierra Baguales); **Base Torres** (hike, advanced, 22 km, 8 h); **Valle del Silencio** (mountain ascent, advanced, 29.4 km, 9 h); **Cerro Redondo** (advanced, 18.5 km); **Florence** (advanced ride through the former estancias; advanced riders only); **Encuentros con el Puma** (overland, easy, full-day, 7 h, with external specialists, subject to availability).

## Dining (/dining-torres-del-paine-reserve/)
"An old estancia becomes your dining room." Breakfast, lunch and dinner, trail meals on full-day explorations and beverages all day are included. A short walk away, the **quincho** on an old sheep ranch: fires lit for a traditional **asado al palo**, long tables, slow-roasted lamb and beef, seasonal salads. Chilean wines, at the quincho or as a tasting back at the lodge. Ingredients locally sourced whenever possible.

## Getting there (/getting-here-torres-del-paine-conservation-reserve/)
- Fly Santiago → **Puerto Natales** (LATAM or Sky). Then about **two hours** by road, mostly paved, only 20 km (12.4 mi) of gravel.
- Free pick-ups: Puerto Natales Airport **12:00 and 15:00**; Puerto Natales hotels and port between 11:00 and 12:00 (driver waits max 15 minutes). Private transfers cost extra. Puerto Natales Airport has maintenance January–March 2027 with a reduced schedule.
- **From Patagonia National Park**: 1.5-hour lodge-to-lodge flight over the Andes and the southern ice field, Mondays and Thursdays from October 3rd to mid-April, "exclusive for programs with transfer included". The page states "Prices start at USD 2,352 per person, one way, for a 4-night stay and USD 3,420 for a 3-night stay."
- **Via Argentina**: Buenos Aires → El Calafate (about 3.5 hours' flight), then bus or transfer about four hours to the Chilean border at Cerro Castillo, where Explora picks you up. Transfers around noon.
- **El Calafate ↔ reserve**: van on select days October–April, private or shared (minimum 2), extra fee, booked in advance; 374 km (232 mi), about 5–6 hours. **El Chaltén ↔ reserve**: one-way for an extra fee for guests combining stays; 402 km (250 mi), about 8–9 hours.

## Best time and climate (lodge FAQ)
"Wet Andes", volatile weather, "all four seasons in a single day". Summer (November–February): long days, best for Base Torres and Valle Francés. Autumn (March–May): golden and red foliage, calmer winds, fewer visitors. Winter (June–August): solitary, snow-dusted peaks. Spring (September–October): wildlife activity peaks.

## Travel tips (/torres-del-paine-travel-guied-tips/)
- Explora provides trekking poles, helmets and riding gaiters, and a bottle of water.
- Bring: sun hat and warm hat; four layers (thermal, fleece, down, waterproof jacket and trousers); hiking trousers, shorts, long socks, hiking shoes; thin and windproof gloves; waterproof leggings; sunglasses, sunscreen, buff; 25-litre backpack; **a headlamp if travelling May–September**. Optional: swimsuit, sandals, camel back.
- Climate: the "Wet Andes", all four seasons in a day. Wi-Fi in rooms and common areas "may vary in reliability". Voltage **220V**.
- Tips voluntary, at reception at the end of the stay; suggested **USD 100 per day per room**.
- No recommended minimum stay is stated for this lodge (the Lake Pehoé lodge recommends five nights).
