Sources: https://www.explora.com/explora-travels/ (About Us, fetched live 2026-10-21 for the tab text); /founders-vision/; /explora-hotels/ ("WHY EXPLORA"); /explora-south-america/ ("OUR COMMITMENT"); /awards-page/; /press-page/; /explora-experts/; /explorers-manifesto/; /explora-jobs/; /reporting-channel/; /explora-contact/; /contact-explora-lodges/; /what-is-explorer-echo-society/; /blog/; /payment-and-cancellation-policies/ (legal name); home page.

## Who Explora is
- "World's Leading Expedition Company" (home title). "Set in remote territories of South America, our intimate lodges and expeditions deliver essential luxury and a deep connection." Its idea of luxury is "the Luxury of the Essential" / "essential luxury": "exploring remote places in-depth without ostentation" (/explora-hotels/).
- **Purpose** (/explora-travels/): "To explore remote territories as a journey to conservation." **Values**: Care for the Journey; Close attention for travelers; Pursuit of excellence; Conservation for the future; Passion for exploration.
- **History**: "A group of friends who were enthusiastic about unconventional travel and adventure gave life to Explora." **1993**: launch of Explora Patagonia, "the only hotel situated in the heart of the Torres del Paine National Park". "In over 30 years, we made our way to becoming world-class pioneers in exploration." (The crawled timeline stops at 1993.)
- **Founder** (/founders-vision/): a letter from **Pedro Ibáñez, President and Founder**. In the 1970s and 80s few travelers came to Chile, "That was when we decided to create Explora, to open up Chile and make it known to the world" — combining "interaction with nature and local cultures with relatively demanding physical activity", then at day's end "those comforts and pleasures which reward the effort made". "At that time, no one offered a service … So we had no choice but to invent it."
- **Legal name** on the policy page: Explora Chile S.A.
- **B Corp**: "This 2025, Explora has been recertified as a B Corp"; goal of reducing its carbon footprint 46% by 2030 (/explora-south-america/). Three pillars: environmental, social (incl. an "Explora Minimum Ethical Income" for staff), economic (local hiring and suppliers; supplier development in Rapa Nui, Atacama, PNP and Torres del Paine). The page links an Impact Report 2023.
- **Explorer's Manifesto** (/explorers-manifesto/): Leave home; Enjoy the journey; Pack light; Go far; Forget the time; Walk; Deviate from the route; Go outside when it rains; Ask questions; Try new flavors; Do not stop; Stop; Talk with people; Listen to stories about the place; Protect nature.

## Where the site disagrees with itself
- **How many lodges/destinations**: home "nine lodges in Chile, Peru, Argentina, and Bolivia" and, in another section, "nine lodges across Chile, Easter Island, Argentina, and Peru; one overland Travesía into Bolivia"; the nine-lodge blog counts Jirira in Bolivia among 9 lodges in 4 countries; /explora-jobs/ says "seven destinations across four countries".
- **Age**: "over 30 years" (/explora-travels/), "almost three decades" (/explora-south-america/), "More than 20 years since then" (founder's letter, older text), "With more than 20 years of experience" (/corporate-travel-program/).
- **Leading Expedition Company years**: awards page lists World's Leading 2019–2025 (seven); /2025/12/02/how-do-we-explore/ badges show 2023 and 2024; /explora-travels/ and /explora-south-america/ still show a 2022 badge.
- **PNP World's Leading Lodge**: 2025 on the overview, 2024 on the lodge page (see `patagonia-national-park`).
