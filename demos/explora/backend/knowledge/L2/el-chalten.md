# Explora El Chaltén (Patagonia, Argentina)

Sources: /el-chalten-lodge/; /el-chalten-hotel/; /el-chalten-hotel-ab/ and /el-chalten-hotels/ (A/B pages); /el-chalten-tours/; /el-chalten-tours-ab/ (FAQ); /explorations-lodge/el-chalten/; /how-to-get-el-chalten/ (tabs "How to Get Here", "Pick-up & Drop-off", "Transfer to/from Explora Torres del Paine", "Check-In & Check-Out"); /el-chalten-travel-tips/; /program/explora-connects-patagonia/; /destinations/; reservations.json.

## The lodge
- Inside the **14,000-acre Los Huemules Conservation Reserve**, bordering **Los Glaciares National Park**, on the north bank of the **Eléctrico River** under the **Marconi Glacier**, in lenga and ñirre forest. Address: Provincial Route 23 km 16 (see disagreements).
- **17 km from El Chaltén village**, "the hiking capital of Argentina". Close to the Southern Patagonian Ice Field ("the world's third largest fresh water reserve").
- Known for Marconi Glacier and Eléctrico Valley views, Fitz Roy treks and high-mountain ascents.
- **20 rooms**; minimalist, sustainable architecture by **Pepe José Cruz**.
- **Spa**: **five open-air hot tubs, two indoor saunas**, two treatment rooms, relaxation lounge, outdoor seating, a shower with valley views.
- Awards: Argentina's Leading Lodge 2025; Condé Nast Readers' Choice 2025; Andrew Harper Editors' Choice 2024.
- Included: accommodations, all explorations, all meals and beverages, spa, park entrance fees, round-trip airport transfers.
- **Booking code ECHA** (/el-chalten-hotels/ deep-links to …/ibe/paso1?session_id=&hotel_code=ECHA).

## Rooms
- **3 Suites**: 60 m² (646 ft²), 1 king or 2 twin beds. **17 Standard Rooms**: 36 m² (388 ft²); bed type differs by page. All have forest views and sleep 2.
- Heating, hair dryer, wake-up call, non-smoking; extra bed for children and crib subject to availability. The A/B page adds safe box, bathrobes, slippers. **Not included: phone calls, laundry.**
- Bed type is chosen at booking and can be changed at the lodge, subject to availability.

## Explorations
- **More than 30** full- and half-day explorations, included; bilingual guides from the School of Guides; groups of **no more than eight**. Each evening your guide plans the next day with you. **Private guiding costs extra**; self-guided options exist.
- Areas: **Los Huemules Conservation Reserve** (routes from the lodge's door); **Parque Nacional Los Glaciares** (over 100 bird species); **Lago del Desierto** (about 10 km by 1 km, fed by the Diablo River).
- Activities: Hike, Overland (van plus short hikes), Climbing (beginners to experts), Self Guided, By packraft, High Mountain Ascent (Cerro Madsen, Paso del Cuadrado). Difficulty Easy, Moderate, Advanced, Expert.
- Examples:
  - Packraft Río de las Vueltas (Moderate, half-day).
  - Glaciar Huemul (hike, Moderate, half-day).
  - Laguna Pollone (hike, Advanced, full-day).
  - Rio Arriba and Río Las Vueltas (overland, Easy, half-day); Reserva Vespignani (overland, Easy, full-day).
  - Las Lolas, Las Lengas (self-guided, Easy).
  - Laguna de los Huemules, Aves Patagónicas, Laguna Cóndor (Easy, half-day).
  - La Roca (climbing, Moderate); Conexión Vertical (climbing briefing, Easy).
  - Paso del Cuadrado (Advanced, full-day).
- Explora provides trekking poles and a bottle of water.

## Dining
Argentine Patagonian cuisine with gaucho roots, craft cocktails and regional wines, included. The A/B page: seasonal produce from small agroecological farmers and wild-harvested products. Page /our-cuisine-is-an-interpetation-of-the-patagonian-environment/ (not crawled).

## Getting here
- Nearest airport **Comandante Armando Tola International, El Calafate**: 220 km (137 miles), about **3 hours**. Flights mainly from Buenos Aires Ezeiza (about 3.5 h) on Aerolíneas Argentinas, Jetsmart, Flybondy.
- From Chile: SKY flies Santiago–El Calafate twice weekly (afternoon arrivals overnight in El Calafate, pick-up next day), or fly to Punta Arenas or Puerto Natales and cross at Río Don Guillermo to meet Explora's van.
- **Free arrival shuttle** (programs with transfer): El Calafate Airport **12:00 PM and 4:30 PM**; El Calafate hotels about **11:00 AM and 3:30 PM**. Flight changes within 24 hours may cost extra.
- **Departure**: leaves the lodge **8:00 AM**; airport by 11:30 AM, hotels by 12:00 PM. Private transfers at extra cost via reserve@explora.com.
- **Torres del Paine van** (Explora Connects): **9:00 a.m.**, extra cost, shared (minimum two) or private (up to six), booked in advance; 402 km (250 miles), meal stop. Conditions (in Spanish): limited places, **October–April** only, no responsibility for border or road delays.
- By car: route PDF on the page; 17 km (10.5 miles) from the village.

## Check-in and check-out
Check-in after 1:00 p.m. (arrive at noon for an afternoon exploration); check-out by 10:00 a.m. The shared transfer leaves at 8:00 a.m., so no departure-day exploration unless you book a private transfer. Check-out-day lunch not included.

## Best time and climate
- "All four seasons can unfold within a single day"; dress in layers.
- Patagonia hub: summer (December–February) most popular; spring and fall quieter.
- Stay **at least 5 nights**; six for more depth.

## Travel tips
- **Pack**: four layers (thermal, fleece, down, waterproof jacket and trousers), sun and warm hats, hiking shoes and long socks, thin and windproof gloves, waterproof leggings, sunglasses, buff, sunscreen, 25-liter backpack; optional swimsuit, sandals, camel back.
- **220V**; Wi-Fi may be unreliable. Tips voluntary, suggested USD 100 per day per room. Guides are WFR certified.
