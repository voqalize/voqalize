Who Are We?

We are a team of experienced business operators and security practitioners passionate about technology. As lifelong learners, we continually seek ways to simplify complexity using automation, striving to deliver exceptional value to those we serve.
 
In recent years, we witnessed a global cybersecurity perfect storm, leaving thousands of organizations, without the internal resources, skills, or capabilities, to protect their businesses effectively. We recognized an opportunity to help companies use cybersecurity as a business enabler and to democratize the strategic practices of experienced CISOs into a SaaS platform that can guide IT and Security teams at every step with a strategic approach to cybersecurity.
 
From Enveedo’s early days, we had the privilege to work with world-class security experts from Fortune 500 companies who shared our vision. They have been instrumental in guiding us through the product development process of digitizing what CISOs have been doing for decades: creating and evolving cyber resiliency to better protect organizations.
That’s how Enveedo was born—a Cyber Risk Management Platform that is constantly innovating. Our mission is to help companies achieve and continuously evolve cyber resiliency in their business.

Our mission is to enable your organization to build continuous cyber resiliency.

Enveedo is the world’s first cybersecurity strategy execution platform that helps you manage the ongoing problem of cyber threats.

We simplify the complex process of planning, building, and running a Cybersecurity Program. The Enveedo platform is easy to use and guides you every step of the way.

Building cyber resiliency is no longer an option, it is a necessity

Our Principles

We Build to Win

We build to win in the markets we choose. Not from arrogance, but from conviction — that what we do matters and can be done better. Winning is not declared; it is built through product, judgment, discipline, and resilience.

Think From the Roots

We don’t copy what already exists. We deconstruct it to understand it and rebuild it better. We think from first principles, design robust systems, and innovate where others accept the status quo. Our advantage is seeing deeper.

Decide and Move

We think deeply, decide without paralysis, and execute with judgment. Perfect information never arrives — waiting has a cost. Deciding is moving forward, and adjusting along the way is part of the process.

Customer Obsession

We are focused on creating real value for the customer. We don’t measure success by what we deliver, but by the impact we create. The customer is not the end — it is the starting point of everything we build.

Extreme Ownership

If it’s in front of you, it’s yours. Responsibility is owned, coordinated with the team, and carried through to completion. We don’t wait for others to solve problems — we act and take full accountability for results.

Radical Honesty

We say what is, not what we want to hear. With the team, customers, and stakeholders — always with respect and empathy. Honesty guides decisions and outcomes, grounded in facts rather than perception.
