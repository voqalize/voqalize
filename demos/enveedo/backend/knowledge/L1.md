## Security Performance OS (/security-performance-os: the enterprise pitch)
"Turn your security program into an integrated, governable, and measurable system." Why enterprise programs stall: operational fragmentation, multi-framework duplication, hard-to-defend risk, disruptive audits. Pillars:
- **Control Performance**: formal ownership and testing, centralized recurring evidence, multi-framework reuse, controls linked to risks.
- **Risk Performance**: risk catalog by framework, configurable methods (qualitative, semi-quantitative, quantitative), control maturity linked to residual risk, "financial risk quantification when required".
- **Audit Performance**: audit projects with scope, controlled access for internal and external auditors, evidence in context, findings traced to risks.
- **Document Management**: version control, approval workflow with audit trail, employee digital signature and acknowledgment.
- **Unified Multi-Workspace Console**: isolated programs per subsidiary or region, one console to compare posture, role-based access.
- **AI-powered risk suggestions** from industry, assets and processes; "only anonymized data is shared".
- Versus manual operation and traditional GRC platforms: faster time-to-value, security specialization, structured reusability, performance governed by your team.
