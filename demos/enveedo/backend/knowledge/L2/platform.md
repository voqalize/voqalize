# The platform: modules, automation, dashboards

Sources: https://enveedo.com/enveedo_platform, https://enveedo.com/ (How Enveedo Works, Impact Timeline, Without/With), https://enveedo.com/book-a-demo, https://enveedo.com/cyber-insurance.

## Six modules ("Fully Integrated Security Management", place `modules` on /enveedo_platform)
- **Crown Jewel Analysis (CJA)**: "Map critical business functions to supporting assets and processes to focus security efforts where they matter most." /book-a-demo: "Platform-led workshops to identify and define your company's most important critical assets (the crown jewels of your business)." /cyber-insurance: "Unlike traditional compliance-first platforms, Enveedo's Crown Jewel Analysis (CJA) seamlessly prioritizes your most critical assets and business functions across all modules." FAQ: it "considers factors such as asset value, vulnerability, and potential impact on your business".
- **Risk Register**: "Consolidate, track, and manage risks in one place with automated updates from integrated security tools." /book-a-demo: "Real-time Risk Heatmap to visualize and understand your Risk Posture with detailed recommendations."
- **Compliance Management**: "Map controls once and apply to multiple frameworks (NIST, ISO, SOC2, etc.) with automated evidence collection." On /frameworks this is called the Assessment module.
- **Vendor Risk Management**: "Streamline vendor assessments with automated questionnaires, risk scoring, and continuous monitoring." The comparison table adds "due diligence questionnaires & approval workflows".
- **Incident Response Manager**: "Build, test, and execute incident response plans tied directly to your critical assets and business functions." FAQ: predefined communication templates, incident tracking, expert guidance.
- **Security Roadmap**: "Plan and track your security program over time with prioritized initiatives and automated progress tracking." /book-a-demo: "Risk-based Cybersecurity Roadmap to prioritize your risk reduction activities."

## Automation (place `automation`: "Let Enveedo Do the Heavy Lifting")
Task management (auto-assign and track remediation tasks), evidence collection (gather and organize compliance evidence), control testing (schedule and document control tests), risk updates (update risk scores from new data), reporting (executive and detailed reports on demand). /book-a-demo: "Comprehensive Workflow Management to track, delegate and measure your security improvements in a single platform"; "Continuous automated risk assessment and exposure analysis with actionable recommendations"; "Automated, continuous monitoring across 170+ security controls (CIS, ISO, NIST-CSF, with more to come)".

## Dashboards (place `dashboards`)
Executive (security posture and risk metrics), Operational (day-to-day program management), Compliance (framework status and gaps), Risk (register with prioritization and trends), Vendor (third-party risk overview).
