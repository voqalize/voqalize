# Booking a demo and the forms

Sources: https://enveedo.com/book-a-demo, https://enveedo.com/contact, https://enveedo.com/trust-center, https://enveedo.com/faq, the scheduler at meetings.hubspot.com/elis-martinez.

## The demo (/book-a-demo)
"Request a product demo. Get a Free Risk Assessment with a CISO Strategist, to learn about our product features." FAQ: "a complimentary Cyber Risk Assessment valued at $2,500 USD … a clear understanding of your current cybersecurity posture and how Enveedo can enhance your security strategies."
- The scheduler: HubSpot Meetings, "Meet with Elis Martinez" (co-founder and Chief Revenue Officer). Lengths: 15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour. Location: Microsoft Teams. Times appear in the visitor's own time zone. After a time is chosen it asks for first name, last name and email; then a final confirm button the visitor presses.
- What is on the page around it (`start_with`): continuous automated risk assessment; API integrations; 170+ controls monitored; workflow management; crown-jewel workshops; real-time risk heatmap; risk-based roadmap; CISO Concierge.
- The same scheduler is at meetings.hubspot.com/elis-martinez (a fallback in a new tab).

## The contact form (/contact; also at the foot of /frameworks)
Fields: Name*, Last name*, Phone, Country* (a fixed list of forty countries plus "Others": Antigua and Barbuda, Argentina, Australia, Bahamas, Barbados, Belize, Bolivia, Botswana, Brazil, British Virgin Islands, Canada, Chile, Colombia, Costa Rica, Cuba, Dominica, Dominican Republic, Ecuador, El Salvador, Guatemala, Guyana, Honduras, India, Israel, Jamaica, Marshall Islands, Mexico, Nicaragua, Others, Panama, Paraguay, Peru, Saint Kitts and Nevis, Singapore, Somalia, South Korea, Suriname, Thailand, Trinidad and Tobago, United States, Uruguay — no Spain, no United Kingdom: those are "Others"), Business E-mail*, Organization, Message, and a required consent box ("you consent to receive communications from Enveedo for marketing purposes, and agree to Enveedo's Privacy Policy"). Invisible reCAPTCHA on Submit. The labels are in English on the Spanish page too.

## The SOC 2 report request (/trust-center)
First name*, Last name*, Company*, Job Title*, Description, Corporate Email*, consent box*.

## The rule
Javier fills what the visitor dictates. The visitor ticks the consent box and presses Submit (or the scheduler's final confirm). In this preview the scheduler's confirm is held back and nothing reaches Enveedo's calendar.
