# Frameworks: the 39 cards on /frameworks

Sources: https://enveedo.com/frameworks (and /es/frameworks), https://enveedo.com/ ("Supporting 45+ frameworks").

"With Enveedo's Assessment module, you can quickly assess your current compliance status and monitor it in real time across any framework you choose. From international regulations to well-known industry standards, the path to a stronger cybersecurity and privacy posture is at your fingertips. Below are the frameworks currently supported by Enveedo." The home page says "Supporting 45+ frameworks"; the page lists these 39. find_framework returns a card's key to show. Each card is one place (`fw_…`). The contact form is at the foot of the page (`contact_form`).

## Information Security Frameworks (place `infosec`)
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 (`fw_iso_27001`): international standard for an Information Security Management System.
- SOC 2 Readiness (`fw_soc2`): AICPA framework auditing service providers' controls across five Trust Services Criteria (security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, privacy).
- TISAX ISA (`fw_tisax`): assessment model based on ISO 27001 for the automotive industry.
- NIST SP 800-82 (`fw_nist_800_82`): securing industrial control systems (ICS, SCADA, DCS, PLCs).
- ISO/IEC 9001:2015 (`fw_iso_9001`): quality management system.
- NIST CSF v2.0 (`fw_nist_csf`): the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (identify, protect, detect, respond, recover).
- HIPAA Security Rule (`fw_hipaa`): safeguards for electronic personal health data.
- ISO/IEC 27002:2022 (`fw_iso_27002`): guidance on information security controls, the implementation guide for 27001.
- NIS2 Directive (`fw_nis2`): EU-wide cybersecurity rules for critical and important entities (Directive (EU) 2022/2555, Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2690).
- DORA (`fw_dora`): Regulation (EU) 2022/2554, digital operational resilience of financial entities and their ICT providers.
- PCI DSS v4.0.1 (`fw_pci_dss`): protecting credit card data.
- ISO/IEC 31000:2018 (`fw_iso_31000`): risk management guidelines.
- ISO/IEC 27017:2015 (CSC) (`fw_iso_27017_csc`): cloud security for cloud service customers.
- ENS (`fw_ens`): Spain's National Security Framework (Royal Decree 311/2022); a self-assessment following the CCN-STIC 808 guide.
- CIS CSC V8.1 (`fw_cis`): the CIS Controls.
- ISO/IEC 22301:2019 (`fw_iso_22301`): business continuity management.
- ISO/IEC 27017:2015 (CSP) (`fw_iso_27017_csp`): cloud security for cloud service providers.
- NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 3 (`fw_nist_800_171`): protecting Controlled Unclassified Information for contractors of US federal agencies (May 2024).

## AI Frameworks (place `ai`)
- EU AI Act (`fw_eu_ai_act`): classifies AI systems by risk level; safety, transparency, human oversight.
- NIST AI RMF 600-1 (`fw_nist_ai_rmf`): the NIST AI Risk Management Framework.
- ISO/IEC 42001:2023 (`fw_iso_42001`): an AI Management System (AIMS).

## Privacy Frameworks (place `privacy`)
- GDPR (`fw_gdpr`): EU personal data protection.
- Enveedo PIA (`fw_enveedo_pia`): Enveedo's own privacy impact assessment, aligned with laws like GDPR.
- NIST Privacy Framework v1.0 (`fw_nist_privacy`).
- ISO/IEC 27701:2025 (`fw_iso_27701`): a Privacy Information Management System (PIMS).
- LGPD (`fw_lgpd`): Brazil's data protection law.
- Chile Data Protection Law (21.719) (`fw_chile_21719`): Chile's new law; consent, access, correction and deletion rights; "aligns the country with global data standards by December 2026".
- Chile Data Protection Law (No. 19628) (`fw_chile_19628`): the current law, "in force until December 2026".
- Chile Cybersecurity Law (`fw_chile_cyber_law`): the Cybersecurity Framework Law 21.663 ("Ley Marco de Ciberseguridad"): security measures, incident reporting, coordination with national authorities.
- Mexico Data Protection Law (Public) (`fw_mexico_public`): public entities.
- Mexico Data Protection Law (Private) (`fw_mexico_private`): the LFPDPPP, private entities.
- Anexo 28 RMF 2024 (`fw_anexo_28`): Mexico's tax authority rules for electronic accounting.
- Colombia Data Protection Law (`fw_colombia_data`): Law 1581.
- CE 007/2018 Requirements (`fw_ce_007`): minimum cybersecurity requirements for financial entities in Colombia.
- Circular CO SA 17.18/24 (`fw_circular_co_sa`): supervision and control guidelines for financial entities (the card names no country).
- Argentina Data Protection Law (`fw_argentina_data`): Law 25,326.
- Peru Data Protection Law (`fw_peru_data`): Law 29733.
- Panama Data Protection Law (`fw_panama_data`): Law 81 of 2019.
- Costa Rica Data Protection Law (`fw_costa_rica_data`): Law 8968.

## Not on the list
Anything else (for example SOX, CMMC, FedRAMP, ISO 27018, Chile's CMF rules, India's DPDP) is not on the page. Say it is not among the 39 listed, that the home page claims more than forty-five, and that the team can say in the demo whether it is supported. Never say it is supported.
