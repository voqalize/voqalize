# FAQ ("Enveedopedia") and the glossary ("EncyloCISO")

Sources: https://enveedo.com/faq (answers collapsed in accordions; each question is a place `faq_…` you can show, which opens it).

## General Information (`faq_general`)
- What is Enveedo? (`faq_what_is`): "a cyber risk management platform that evolves your cybersecurity program by empowering you to build a comprehensive defense strategy. Our platform protects your crown jewel assets, builds a risk-based roadmap, and guides you through remediation processes, enabling you to stop being reactive and start being resilient."
- How can Enveedo benefit my organization? (`faq_benefit`): identify and prioritize high-value assets (CJA); API integrations and automated posture analysis; a risk-based approach across people, process and technology; maturity models; integrated incident response.
- What makes Enveedo different? (`faq_different`): "Unlike traditional platforms that focus on compliance-first approaches, Enveedo prioritizes operational efficiency and strategic asset protection" — Real-Time Insights, Crown Jewel Protection, Seamless Integration.
- For MSPs/MSSPs or for individual businesses? (`faq_msp_or_business`): both, plus VARs.
- Does it support MSPs, MSSPs and VARs? (`faq_msp_support`): "Absolutely … tailor-made", with integrations, asset prioritization and risk management tools.
- How can individual businesses use it? (`faq_individual_business`): assess, prioritize and protect; Crown Jewel Analysis and Real-Time Insights.

## Platform Functionality (`faq_platform`)
- Who can benefit? (`faq_who_benefits`): MSPs and MSSPs, vCISOs and IT teams, security leaders.
- Can it integrate with my existing tools? (`faq_integrate`): "Yes … flexible API capabilities that allow for easy integration with your current security infrastructure."
- How does it prioritize assets? (`faq_prioritize`): Crown Jewel Analysis, weighing asset value, vulnerability and business impact.

## Services & Support (`faq_services`)
- How do I start? (`faq_start`): "schedule a complimentary Cyber Risk Assessment valued at $2,500 USD … This assessment will provide you with a clear understanding of your current cybersecurity posture." (The page says the button is on the home page; it is the demo booking on /book-a-demo.)
- What support for partners and clients? (`faq_support`): training, marketing support, regular updates.
- How does it ensure regulatory compliance? (`faq_compliance`): compliance tracking inside the program; automated workflows and real-time monitoring.
- Support during an incident? (`faq_incident`): the Incident Response Manager module, predefined communication templates, incident tracking, expert guidance.
- Training for new users? (`faq_training`): "Yes … from basic navigation to advanced features."
- Security of clients' data? (`faq_data_security`): encryption, strict access controls, regular security audits.

## Glossary (`glossary`, `glossary_terms`), always open
Sixteen terms: CISO, Crown Jewels Analysis (CJA), Cybersecurity, Cybersecurity Program, Cyber Defense Planning & Optimization (CDPO), Cyber Risk Management, Data Breach, Encryption, Incident Response (IR) Management, Information Security Governance, Penetration Testing, Regulatory Compliance, Security Architecture, Security Policies, Threat Intelligence, vCISO, Vulnerability Management. Each pairs a definition with how Enveedo helps.
- CDPO: "strategically designing and enhancing cybersecurity measures … assessing current defenses, identifying vulnerabilities, deploying appropriate security solutions, and continuously monitoring and improving".
- vCISO: "a service that provides an organization with access to a consultant or a team of consultants who perform the duties of a CISO on a part-time or temporary basis."
