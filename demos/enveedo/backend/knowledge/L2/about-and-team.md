## Who are we (`who_we_are`)
"A team of experienced business operators and security practitioners passionate about technology." They saw "a global cybersecurity perfect storm" leaving organizations without resources or skills, and set out "to democratize the strategic practices of experienced CISOs into a SaaS platform". "From Enveedo's early days, we had the privilege to work with world-class security experts from Fortune 500 companies." "That's how Enveedo was born — a Cyber Risk Management Platform that is constantly innovating."
