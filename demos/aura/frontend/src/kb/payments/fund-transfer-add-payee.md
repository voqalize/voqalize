Send money to anyone from the **Aura Mobile** app — add the payee once, then transfer
in seconds.

## On the Aura Mobile app
1. Log in with **MPIN or biometric**, tap **Pay**.
2. Tap **Send Money** — pick an existing payee, or tap **Add Payee** to add a new
   account (you'll need the **account number and IFSC**).
3. Tap the payee, enter the **amount**.
4. Choose **IMPS** (instant, 24×7) or **NEFT**, tap **Proceed**.
5. Enter your **MPIN** to confirm. Done.

> **Tip — new payees & limits.** A newly added payee may have a short cooling
> period and a starting limit for safety. See transfer limits in the related
> article.

**Transaction not going through?** Call **1860-200-0100**.
