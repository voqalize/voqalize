New to digital banking? You can self-register in a few minutes. You'll need your
**registered mobile number**, and one of: **Customer ID + DOB + PAN**, **debit-card
details**, or your **registered e-mail**.

## Aura Mobile app
1. Download **Aura Mobile**, tap **Login to your Aura account**, allow permissions.
2. Choose your **registered-number SIM**, select your **Customer ID**, Continue.
3. Create your **6-digit MPIN** (optionally enable biometric).
4. Tap **Complete Now** and authenticate — OTP option: enter **Customer ID, DOB,
   PAN**, then the two OTPs. Done — full access.

## Internet Banking (aurabank.example)
1. Go to **aurabank.example → Login → Register**.
2. Enter **Customer ID or registered mobile number**.
3. Authenticate via **OTP**, **Debit Card** (number, expiry, ATM PIN), or **Email
   link**.
4. **Set your password**, verify the OTP — you can bank online immediately.

**Stuck during registration?** Call **1860-200-0100** (8 am–8 pm) or visit a branch.
