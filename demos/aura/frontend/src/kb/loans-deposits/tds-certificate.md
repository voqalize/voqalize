If TDS was deducted on your **FD / RD interest**, you'll want the **TDS certificate
(Form 16A)** for filing.

## On the Aura Mobile app / Internet Banking
- **Deposit TDS:** Login → **FD/RD → Summary of Deposit → select the account →
  Download** the FD advice / TDS certificate.
- Same **Services → Loans/Deposits** area as the Interest Certificate.

> This sits right next to the Interest Certificate flow — log in and I'll point you
> to the exact spot. Form 16A is also viewable on the **TRACES** portal.
