Download your **home-loan statement**, **repayment schedule**, or **provisional
interest certificate** anytime.

## On Internet Banking / Aura Mobile app
- **Statement:** Accounts → **Loans** → select the loan account → **Statement**.
- **Repayment schedule:** Accounts → Loans → select account → **View Repayment
  Schedule**.
- For the **interest paid** figure for taxes, use the **Interest Certificate**
  (see related article).

> Loan documents are account-specific — log in first. I can show you the path.
