Open an FD or RD in a couple of minutes from your account — no paperwork.

## On the Aura Mobile app / Internet Banking
1. Go to **Deposits → Open FD / RD**.
2. Enter the **amount** and **tenure**; the **interest rate** and maturity value
   are shown.
3. Choose **payout** (at maturity / periodic) and confirm.

> Premature withdrawal is allowed (a small penalty may apply). Senior citizens
> usually get a **higher rate**. TDS may apply on interest — see the TDS article.
