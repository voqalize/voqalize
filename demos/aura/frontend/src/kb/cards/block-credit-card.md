If your credit card is **lost, stolen, or you spot an unknown transaction**, block
it immediately — your liability stops once it's blocked.

## Fastest ways to block
- **Aura Mobile app / Internet Banking:** Cards → select the card → **Block / Replace**.
- **24×7 helpline:** **1860-200-0100** or **1860-200-0200**.
- **Emergency card-loss line:** **+91 22 2000 0200**.
- **SMS / Aura chat / IVR:** block options are available 24×7.

After blocking you can request a **replacement card** in the same flow; a new card
is dispatched to your registered address.

> **Report fraud separately.** If money has already moved, also raise a dispute
> (see Grievance Redressal) and call the helpline right away.
