You can set or reset your **debit-card PIN**, change **transaction limits**, and
turn **domestic / international / online / ATM usage on or off** yourself on the
Aura Mobile app — the fastest way to **temporarily lock a card you've misplaced**
without permanently blocking it.

## On the Aura Mobile app
1. Log in and tap **More → Debit Cards**.
2. Tap your card, tap the **(i) icon**, enter the **OTP** — your card details show.
3. **Set / Reset PIN:** tap **Set/Reset**, pick the card, enter a 4-digit PIN and
   the OTP.
4. **Manage Usage:** set **domestic and international limits**, or switch usage
   off to lock the card; confirm with your **MPIN**.

> **Lost or stolen card?** To lock it instantly, use **Manage Usage** to switch
> off all usage. To **permanently block & replace** it, use the same Debit Cards
> section or call the 24×7 helpline below — and report any fraud immediately.
