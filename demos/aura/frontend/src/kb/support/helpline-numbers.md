Key Aura Bank numbers (call from any phone in India).

## Numbers
- **24×7 Retail / Cards:** **1860-200-0100** or **1860-200-0200**.
- **Phone Banking (accounts/deposits):** **1860-200-0100** (8 am–8 pm).
- **Report lost/stolen card (emergency):** **+91 22 2000 0200** (24×7).
- **IVR:** available 24×7.

> Account- and card-services teams operate roughly **8 am–8 pm**; IVR and card
> blocking are **24×7**. Never share your **OTP, PIN, CVV, or password** — Aura
> staff will never ask for them.
