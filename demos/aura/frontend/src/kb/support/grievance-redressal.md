If something's gone wrong — a wrong charge, a failed transaction, or fraud — here's
how to get it resolved.

## Steps
1. **Lodge a complaint:** aurabank.example → **Lodge a Complaint**, or the Aura Mobile app
   → Support — you'll get a **complaint reference number**.
2. **Disputed/fraud transaction:** call **1860-200-0100** and block the card
   immediately if fraud is suspected.
3. **Not resolved in time?** Escalate to the **Nodal Officer / Principal Nodal
   Officer**, then to the **RBI Banking Ombudsman**.

> Always keep your **complaint reference number** — it's how the resolution is
> tracked.
