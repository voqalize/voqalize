Find a branch or ATM, and look up the **IFSC code** you need for a transfer.

## How
- **Branch/ATM locator:** aurabank.example → **Locate Us**, or the Aura Mobile app — search
  by city / pin code.
- **IFSC code:** shown on your cheque leaf, in the app under account details, or via
  the locator. Aura IFSC codes look like **AURA0000XXX**.

> For NEFT/IMPS to an Aura account you need the **account number + IFSC**.
