You can open an Aura savings account **fully online** in minutes with **Video KYC**
— no branch visit.

## Steps
1. On aurabank.example or the Aura Mobile app, tap **Open Digital A/C**.
2. Enter your **mobile number, PAN, and Aadhaar** and verify with OTP.
3. Choose the account variant (e.g. **Easy Access**, **Prime**, salary, or a
   zero-balance variant if eligible).
4. Complete **Video KYC** with an agent and fund the account.

> Keep your **Aadhaar-linked mobile, PAN, and a signature** handy. Eligibility and
> minimum balance vary by variant — see the related articles.
