Most savings variants require an **Average Monthly Balance (AMB)**. Falling below
it can attract a **non-maintenance charge**.

## Key points
- AMB **depends on your account variant and city** (metro / semi-urban / rural).
- It's an **average over the month**, not a daily minimum — a few low days are fine
  if the average holds.
- Some variants (e.g. certain salary or zero-balance accounts) have **no AMB**.
- You can check your variant's exact AMB on aurabank.example or in the Aura Mobile app.

> Want a no-minimum-balance option? Ask about eligible **zero-balance** variants.
