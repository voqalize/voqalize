Banks must periodically refresh your **KYC** (Know Your Customer) details. If
yours is due, complete **re-KYC** promptly so your account isn't restricted.

## Ways to complete re-KYC
- **Aura Mobile app / Internet Banking:** Services → **Update KYC** (if no change, you
  can often self-declare).
- Update **address, PAN, name, or nominee** under **Services → My Details**.
- If documents are needed, visit a branch with **PAN + Aadhaar / address proof**.

> If your account is flagged for KYC, acting early avoids a temporary freeze on
> transactions.
