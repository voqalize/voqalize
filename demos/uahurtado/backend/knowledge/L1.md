# Universidad Alberto Hurtado: what the sites say

Every fact here is from the university's own sites (crawled 2026-10-22). The sites are in Spanish;
Spanish names are written exactly as the pages write them, because they are what the visitor sees.
Where pages disagree, say which page says what. **Fees, PAES ponderaciones and minimum scores,
application windows, admission calendars and open visits change: they are never in this file. Read
them live on the page in front of the visitor** (the career's Requisitos y Aranceles tab, the
programme's Requisitos y Valores, Ven y Conócenos), and say the year the page names.

## Who the UAH is
- A **Jesuit university** in Santiago, non-profit, "con vocación pública", whose motto is "para el
  bien común". Part of the Consejo de Rectores (CRUCH) and **adscrita a la gratuidad**. Institutional
  accreditation: 5 years, in 5 areas. Member of the International Association of Jesuit Universities.
- **Seven faculties**: Ciencias Sociales, Derecho, Economía y Negocios, Educación, Filosofía y
  Humanidades, Ingeniería, Psicología. **50 undergraduate careers** and **82 postgraduate
  programmes**.

## Admission routes, one line each
- **Admisión vía PAES** (centralizada): apply through the national system; each career weighs NEM,
  Ranking, Lectora, Matemática, Historia o Ciencias (its tab Requisitos y Aranceles).
- **Admisión Directa**: an alternative route with limited places, for the careers marked for it: pre-
  apply with the form on `/admision-directa/`, upload documents, interview. Modalities include NEM 30%
  o superior, Estudiantes destacados/as, TNS, Pruebas internacionales, Enseñanza media en el
  extranjero, Otra universidad, Pueblos originarios, Deportistas destacadas/os, Prueba de años
  anteriores, Cambio interno, Carreras paralelas.
- **Admisión PACE**: guaranteed places for students from PACE schools, without regard to the PAES score.
- **Ranking 1000**: a quota for the top Ranking de Notas whose PAES falls short; apply at ranking1000.cl.
- **Programa de Atracción de Talento Pedagógico**: for 4° medio students; passing it opens Admisión
  Especial to the pedagogías and the Beca Talento Pedagógico.
- **Habilitación pedagógica**: by law, every first-year pedagogy student meets one of the legal
  requirements (PAES percentile, school grades ranking, or a recognised programme).
- **Continuidad de Estudios / Pedagogía para Profesionales**: for TNS and professionals.

## Becas, beneficios, gratuidad (overview)
- `/becas-y-beneficios/` has seven pill tabs: BECAS DE EXCELENCIA ACADÉMICA UAH (by PAES average: Beca
  Excelencia 90% toda la carrera, Primer año, the Derecho/Ingeniería Comercial/Arqueología/Psicología/
  Ingeniería Civil beca, Beca Pedagogía, Beca Talento Pedagógico) · BECAS DE MATRÍCULA · BECAS DE ARANCEL
  (Beca Alberto Hurtado, Complementaria al CAE) · DESCUENTOS DE ARANCEL (Segundo/a Hermano/a, Cobertura
  de Escolaridad, Segunda Carrera) · BECAS DE MANTENCIÓN (alimentación, transporte, impresiones,
  deportistas) · SERVICIOS UNIVERSITARIOS DE APOYO · OTROS SERVICIOS. The bands and percentages change
  yearly: read the pane. Institutional becas do not combine with each other, nor with Gratuidad.
- **Gratuidad**: the UAH is adscrita. For the 60% of lowest-income households, presencial
  undergraduate careers (diurna or vespertina), no previous professional degree; apply with the FUAS at
  beneficiosestudiantiles.cl. `/gratuidad-universitaria/` answers the common questions.
- State aid named on the pages: Beca Vocación de Profesor (pedagogías), BEA, CAE.
