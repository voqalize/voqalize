# Becas y Beneficios de la UAH

Sources: admision.uahurtado.cl/becas-y-beneficios/, /deportista-destacado/, postgrados.uahurtado.cl/financiamiento/.

The page is titled "Becas y Beneficios 2026." and is a set of vertical pills (Bootstrap): click the
tab, then each beca is an `h3` inside its pane. The score bands and percentages per career change
every year: read them live from the pane, never quote them from memory. Two rules the page states for
the whole set: institutional becas are **not cumulative** with each other or with agreements, and
students with **Gratuidad** cannot also take the becas that discount arancel or matrícula.

## BECAS DE EXCELENCIA ACADÉMICA UAH (`#v-pills-uno-tab` → `#v-pills-uno`)
For students who enter by Admisión Centralizada straight from school, by their **PAES average**
(Competencia Lectora + Competencia Matemática 1). To keep them: stay in the top 30% of your cohort,
pass at least 80% of the semester's load, and keep to the university's rules.
- **Beca Excelencia 90% toda la carrera**: 90% off the arancel for the whole career, for the top PAES
  averages; any career except the pedagogías (which have the state's Beca Vocación de Profesor).
- **Beca Excelencia Primer año**: a percentage off the first year's arancel, by score band and career
  (a table on the page).
- **Beca Derecho, Ingeniería Comercial, Arqueología, Psicología e Ingeniería Civil Toda la Carrera**:
  a percentage for the whole career in those careers, by score band.
- **Beca Talento Pedagógico**: a first-year maintenance grant for those who completed the Programa de
  Atracción de Talento Pedagógico and study a pedagogy.
- **Beca Pedagogía Toda la Carrera**: a discount for pedagogy students in a score band just below the
  Beca Vocación de Profesor (MINEDUC), which covers the higher band.

## BECAS DE MATRÍCULA (`#v-pills-dos-tab`)
- **Beca de matrícula Alberto Hurtado**: 100% of the matrícula for first-year students who entered by
  admisión centralizada (or PACE) and took part in a preparation or inclusive-access programme
  recognised by the UAH, finishing school the year before.

## BECAS DE ARANCEL (`#v-pills-tres-tab`)
Socio-economic; incompatible with each other and with institutional discounts or state becas.
- **Beca Alberto Hurtado**: 10% to 80% off the first year's arancel (preparation / PACE / "Elijo ser
  docente UAH" students).
- **Beca Complementaria al CAE**: for upper-year students with only the CAE (Crédito Ley 20.027), 10%
  to 50% of the copago between the reference and the real arancel.

## DESCUENTOS DE ARANCEL (`#v-pills-cuatro-tab`)
Incompatible with each other and with institutional becas.
- **Segundo/a Hermano/a**: 10% of the annual arancel for the second sibling and later ones.
- **Cobertura de Escolaridad**: 100% of the year's arancel if the financial supporter dies or is fully
  disabled; renewable after review.
- **Segunda Carrera**: 50% of the cheaper career for a student taking a second career in parallel
  (two semesters done, last-semester average 5.0 or more).

## BECAS DE MANTENCIÓN (`#v-pills-cinco-tab`)
- **Beca de Alimentación UAH** (monthly amount on a card for meals), **Beca de Transporte**, **Beca de
  Impresiones**: annual, for students with insufficient socio-economic means, taking at least 30
  credits a semester.
- **Beneficio para Impresiones**: printing credit for every enrolled undergraduate, no application.
- **Beca Deportista Destacado/a**: for new students with a notable sports record in a discipline the
  UAH has teams in; an annual amount paid in instalments, renewable for the career.
- **Beca Mérito Deportivo**: for students from the third semester on who play in a UAH team.

## SERVICIOS UNIVERSITARIOS DE APOYO (`#v-pills-seis-tab`)
Biblioteca, Actividades de Vida saludable, Actividad Física y Deporte (9 selecciones and 4 colectivos),
Cursos de Formación General (OFG, at least 20 credits across the career), Área de Arte y Cultura (four
companies: Teatro, Danza Latinoamericana, Música Tradicional, Coro Polifónico).

## OTROS SERVICIOS DE INTERÉS PARA LOS ESTUDIANTES DE PREGRADO (`#v-pills-siete-tab`)
Consejería y apoyo estudiantil, Sala de Atención Bienestar (DAE: state and institutional benefits,
TNE, credential), Programa de Apoyo a estudiantes padres, madres y gestantes.

## Deportista destacado (`/deportista-destacado/`)
"Admisión directa para deportistas destacados/as": a Admisión Directa route for athletes with a
regional, national or international record, held before PAES results, with places by the sports
unit's needs. Team disciplines include básquetbol damas y varones among others. The page also
describes the Beca Mérito Deportista.

## State benefits the pages mention
Gratuidad (see the gratuidad topic), Beca Vocación de Profesor (BVP, MINEDUC, for pedagogías),
Beca de Excelencia Académica (BEA, MINEDUC), CAE (Crédito Ley 20.027). All are applied for through
the FUAS at beneficiosestudiantiles.cl.

## Postgrado
Postgraduate financing is on postgrados.uahurtado.cl/financiamiento/: **BECAS DE LA AGCI**, **CRÉDITO
CORFO**, **ANID**, with links to **Formas de Pago** and **Descuentos** (UAH alumni benefits, agreements:
read live). Questions: postgrados@uahurtado.cl.
