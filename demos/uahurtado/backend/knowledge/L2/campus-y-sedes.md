- **CAMPUS PATRIMONIAL**: Avda. Bernardo O'Higgins 1825, metro Los Héroes, Santiago de Chile.
  Tel. +56 2 2692 0200. The career pages call it **Casa Central**.
- **CAMPUS B**: Av. Los Leones 1200, Providencia (metro Los Leones). Tel. +56 2 2692 0200.

## Campus Patrimonial (`www…/la-universidad/campus/campus-patrimonial/`)
Headings: "Patrimonio en el centro de la ciudad", "EL CAMPUS UAH EN IMÁGENES" (Vimeo video),
"MAPA CAMPUS CENTRAL" (a "Descargar mapa" PDF), "NUESTRA BIBLIOTECA", "UBICACIÓN", "CAMPUS CENTRAL",
and a Google Maps embed. The campus is in the centre of Santiago, in restored historic buildings of
the barrio; the university opened its seat there in 1997 to integrate students from every part of the
city. The Biblioteca descends from the Compañía de Jesús collections (Bellarmino, San Ignacio, CIDE)
and is among the largest in Chile.

## CampusB (`www…/la-universidad/campus/campusb/`)
"CampusB, el campus de la UAH en Providencia": for undergraduate and postgraduate teaching, extension,
co-work and meetings. Capacity 600 people, 2.200 m², 22 rooms, connected to metro Los Leones,
air-conditioned, Wi-Fi, parking, computer labs. Contador Público Auditor (continuidad, vespertino) is
taught at "Campus B - Providencia".

## Which career is where
Most undergraduate careers say **Sede: Casa Central**. The 100% online continuidad careers say
"100% Online". carreras.json `campus` has each career's value as its page shows it.
