# Visitas y eventos

Sources: admision.uahurtado.cl/ven-y-conocenos/, /admision-visita-la-uah/, /tu-vocacion/,
/audiomallas/, /a-toda-prueba/; www.uahurtado.cl/events/.

What is open, when, and whether sign-up is still possible changes every few weeks: **always read it
live**. This file only says what kinds of activities exist and how the pages show them.

## Ven y Conócenos (`/ven-y-conocenos/`)
"Conoce nuestro calendario de actividades": one card per activity (`.card`, title `h5.card-title`,
then a `p.card-text` with the date, then either a link **"Inscribirse aquí"** or a disabled button
**"Inscripciones cerradas"**). Past activities carry a red **REALIZADO** mark. Kinds of activity on the
page:
- **UAH ABIERTA / ENSAYO PAES**: career fair, sport and exchange stands, a PAES practice test.
- **UAH VIRTUAL**: online talk with teachers and career directors.
- **Visitas guiadas UAH**: a guided walk of the Campus Patrimonial and your career of interest.
- **TALLERES DE INVIERNO** / **TALLERES DE PRIMAVERA**: vocational workshops by career on campus.
- **STAND EN FERIA SIAD**: a stand at the university fair in Estación Mapocho.
Sign-up links leave the site (Google Forms, HubSpot): highlight them, and open one only if the visitor
asks.

## Semana de la Postulación (`/admision-visita-la-uah/`)
The application-week page: "NO TE OLVIDES DE ESTAS FECHAS" (postulación, resultados, matrícula),
"¿NECESITAS AYUDA CALCULANDO TU PUNTAJE?" (link to the simulator), "FERIA DE LA POSTULACIÓN". It still
shows the previous process's dates: say so if you read it.

## Learning about a career from home
- **Audiomallas** (`/audiomallas/`, tabs `#tab1-tab` … `#tab7-tab`, one per faculty): career directors
  and academics narrate their curriculum ("el primer podcast de la UAH"). The same audio is on the
  career page's Malla Curricular tab where `has_audiomalla` is true.
- **La UAH orienta tu vocación** (`/tu-vocacion/`): videos (Storytelling, Voces UAH que te orientan,
  Tesistories, Charlas Vocacionales) and the audiomallas by faculty.
- **A Toda Prueba** (`/a-toda-prueba/`): a free online PAES preparation course (preuniversitario) with
  practice tests.
- **Yo elijo lo que quiero estudiar** (`/que-quiero-estudiar/`): student testimonials and "¿Qué es la
  vocación?".

## University events (`www.uahurtado.cl/events/`)
The institutional calendar (concerts, seminars, talks, Feria Internacional, Academia sabatina Elijo ser
Docente). Listed by date; read live.
