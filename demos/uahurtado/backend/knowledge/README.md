# Universidad Alberto Hurtado knowledge

What Javier knows about the UAH's sites, in three layers. Every fact is the sites' own; nothing is
filled in from general knowledge. Where pages disagree (the career pages say Admisión 2027 while
`/aranceles/` and `/admision-via-paes/` still say 2026; "más de 60" vs "175 instituciones" vs "casi
200 universidades" for the exchange network), the files say which page says what.

The university is three WordPress hosts, and every record says which one it belongs to:
`admision.uahurtado.cl` (undergraduate admissions), `postgrados.uahurtado.cl` (postgraduate
programmes) and `www.uahurtado.cl` (the institution, campus, students). `simula.uahurtado.cl` is the
PAES score simulator, which is a lead form and is never filled.

## Layout

- **`L1.md`**: always in the prompt, in English, keeping the sites' Spanish names. Who the UAH is, the
  three hosts and their menus, the career-page tabs, every career by faculty, the admission routes in
  a line each, the becas and gratuidad overview, campus addresses, contact, postgrado in brief, and
  the list of L2 topics. About 10 kB (2.5k tokens), the size of santanderarena's.
- **`L2/<topic>.md`**: read on demand; the file stem is the topic id; each opens with `# Title` and a
  `Sources:` line.
  - Hand-written (build.py never touches them): `admision`, `becas-y-beneficios`, `gratuidad` (the
    page's FAQ questions as headings, verbatim), `campus-y-sedes`, `visitas-y-eventos`,
    `vida-estudiantil`, `postgrado`.
  - Generated by `build.py`: `facultad-*` (one per faculty: each career with path, duration, jornada,
    sede, título, grado and a short summary), `postgrado-catalogo` (all programmes by type) and
    `site-map` (every page with its headings, tab and accordion labels).
- **`L3/`**: generated by `build.py`, committed, and the only thing the brain reads at runtime.
  - `carreras.json`: the 50 careers: `{slug, name, url, faculty, menu_group, area, title_awarded,
    grado, duration, campus, jornada, modalidad, summary, tabs, has_audiomalla, admision_directa,
    pedagogia, malla_pdf, aliases}`. `faculty` is the Datos Académicos "Facultad" normalised to
    "Facultad de …"; `menu_group` is the group of the Carreras mega-menu ("Continuidad de Estudios"
    for 14 of them); `tabs` are the tab ids present on that page without the `-tab` suffix (`malla`,
    `descripcion_carrera`, `requisitos_carrera`, `beneficios`, `conectados`, `investigando`,
    `titulados`); `aliases` are spoken variants, with any alias that another career's name or alias
    shares dropped.
  - `programas.json`: the 82 programmes: `{slug, name, url, type, especialidad, area, areas, faculty,
    modalidad, duration, summary, aliases}`. `type` is `magister | doctorado | diplomado | postitulo |
    otro` (postítulos are listed under Diplomados on the site; `otro` is the 4 cursos/talleres).
  - `pages.json`: one record per line, in the shape the shared `knowledge.py` loader reads (`path,
    title, description, sections: [{heading, text, items}], headings, faqs: [{q, a}], text`), plus
    `key`, `host`, `url`, `h1`, `kind` (`home | listing | page | template | carrera | programa`),
    `breadcrumbs`, section `level/selector/id/pane/collapse/hidden/dom_text/card`, `tabs [{id, label,
    target, kind, active, selector}]`, `accordions [{label, target, selector, expanded, parent}]`,
    `anchors [{id, tag, pane?, collapse?}]`, `cards`, `documents`, `forms` (field names and labels,
    long selects reduced to a count) and `iframes`. A section's `pane` / `collapse` is the tab pane or
    accordion it sits in: open that first, then scroll. `volatile: true` marks a page whose text is not
    kept (below).
    - **`path` is not unique: the three hosts each have `/`.** `key` (`admision:/`, `www:/`,
      `postgrados:/`, `admision:/carreras/psicologia`, …) is. A loader keyed by `path` alone keeps one
      of the three homes.
    - **Templates.** Career and programme pages share their structure, so it is stored once:
      `admision:/carreras/*` and `postgrados:/programa/*` (`kind: "template"`) carry the sections with
      their selectors and tab panes, the tab ids, the accordion buttons, the anchors (`#cabeza-carrera`,
      `#cuerpo-carrera`, `#admisionForm`, `#programaAccordion`, …) and a `description` explaining the
      page. Then each career has a light record (`kind: "carrera"`, `template: "carrera"`, `slug`,
      `title`, `h1`, `tabs`, `headings`, `has_audiomalla`, no text) and each programme one too (`kind:
      "programa"`, `slug`, `title`). Headings that carry the career's own name ("Beneficios y
      Oportunidades de estudiar Psicología en la UAH") are in the light record's `headings`, not the
      template.
  - `site.json`: the hosts and their roles, contact details and WhatsApp links, each host's menus
    (the admisión main menu with its hover dropdowns, the career mega-menu by faculty with its
    button selector), footer links, the lead form `#admisionForm` (field names; it is a real lead and
    is never submitted), response headers per host (all three send `permissions-policy:
    microphone=()`), redirects, linked UAH sub-hosts, outbound hosts, page kinds and path collisions.

## Deliberately not stored

These change during the year, and Javier reads them live on the page in front of the visitor:

- **Fees**: matrícula, arancel, titulación (UF), online discounts; postgraduate prices, early
  enrolment discounts. The REST reads never request `aranceles_carrera`, `requisitos_program` or
  `horario`, and `/aranceles/`, `postgrados/descuentos/` and `formas-de-pago/` keep no text.
- **PAES ponderaciones and minimum scores** (`requisitos_carrera` is never requested;
  `/requisitos-de-admision/` and `/ranking-1000/` keep no text). The becas page's score bands stay in
  its page text for search but the L2 topics never quote them.
- **Dates**: application windows, start dates, admission and matrícula calendars, events and visits.
  `/admision-via-paes/`, `/admision-visita-la-uah/`, `/ven-y-conocenos/`, `/resultados-ensayo-paes/`
  and `www/events/` keep headings only; everywhere else any line with a peso amount, a UF value, a
  day-and-month date or a clock time is dropped from page text, and a heading or card that is itself a
  date is dropped.
- **Open calls** (which visits are open, "REALIZADO", "Inscripciones cerradas").

## Rebuilding

Run from `voqalize/demos`. Playwright and httpx are pulled in for the run (Chromium once:
`uv run --no-project --with playwright playwright install chromium`):

```sh
uv run --no-project --with playwright --with httpx python uahurtado/backend/knowledge/build.py crawl
uv run --no-project --with playwright --with httpx python uahurtado/backend/knowledge/build.py crawl --refresh
uv run --no-project python uahurtado/backend/knowledge/build.py render
```

`crawl` reads the REST endpoints (about 90 requests, one at a time with a pause: the careers list,
the taxonomies, the programme list without ACF, then each programme's static ACF fields by slug, since
the full list with ACF takes over two minutes) and renders about 125 pages headless, two at a time,
with images, media, fonts and third-party hosts blocked. Both are cached in
`~/.cache/voqalize-uahurtado` (`$XDG_CACHE_HOME`), so a re-run only fetches what is missing;
`--refresh` fetches everything. `render` rebuilds L3 and the generated L2 from the cache. After a
crawl, read the diff of the hand-written L2 sources (becas, gratuidad, vías) and update those files
and L1 if a page changed.

The sites block some countries. A 403, 405, "Not allowed" or a timeout stops the crawl with a message
and writes nothing; reconnect to a US exit and run again.

## Things to know about the sites

- **WordPress, server-rendered, full page loads**, on three origins. Tabs, pills and accordions are
  Bootstrap 5 with stable ids (`#requisitos_carrera-tab`, `#v-pills-uno-tab`, `#collapseRequisitos`);
  `window.bootstrap` is global. The header dropdowns open on hover; clicking the top item navigates.
- **Career pages** vary in which tabs they have (26 have all seven; 12 only Malla, Descripción and
  Requisitos). The audiomalla `<audio>` is in the `#malla` pane on careers with `has_audiomalla`.
- **Redirects**: `/continuidad/` and `/campusb/` go to `/continuidad-de-estudios/`;
  `/visitas-guiadas-uah/` goes to the admisión home; `/indicadores-de-calidad/` goes to
  `www…/la-universidad/calidad-uah/`; `/ingenierias/` leaves for the faculty site
  `ingenieria.uahurtado.cl` (not recorded). `/conocenos/` is a 404.
- **The microphone is blocked** by `permissions-policy` on all three hosts: audio must be captured in an
  extension-owned context.
- **No contact page.** Contact is the footers, the "Conversemos!" link (`#admisionForm`) and WhatsApp
  links. The lead form on career and programme pages is a real lead (Google Forms relay, HubSpot
  tracking); its RUT field checks the check digit.
- **Stale years.** Some titles still say Admisión 2024/2026 and `/aranceles/` is the 2026 table while
  career pages hold 2027 figures.
