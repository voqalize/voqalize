# Santander Arena: what the site says

Every fact here is from www.santanderarena.cl (crawled 2026-10-21). The site is in Spanish;
Spanish names are written exactly as the site writes them, because they are what the visitor
sees on screen. Where pages disagree, both versions are given: say which page says what.

## What it is
- Santander Arena is a covered multipurpose arena in **Parque O'Higgins, Santiago de Chile**
  (Sobre Nosotros). The page calls it "el recinto techado multipropósito más importante de Chile":
  concerts, sport, fairs, conferences and corporate events.
- **It used to be Movistar Arena.** Sobre Nosotros tells the history: construction began in 1958
  for the 1959 Basketball World Cup, sat unfinished for decades ("elefante blanco"), opened in
  2006 as Arena Santiago (with a visit from the Dalai Lama), and was renamed Movistar Arena in
  2008. **The site never says when or why it became Santander Arena.** Many pages still say
  "Movistar Arena" (Terrazas, Cúpula, most Arena News articles, the home page's meta description
  "#MovistarArena", the menu link `/como-llegar-a-movistar-arena`, which redirects to
  `/como-llegar-a-santander-arena`). Treat both names as the same venue.
- Operated within **Be Live** (footer link "Be Live"; an Arena News interview with its CEO
  Daniel Hiller says the group runs the venue, the promoter Bizarro and the ticketing platform
  Punto Ticket).
- **Claims, and where they disagree:**
  - Ranking: the news article "Somos el recinto #1 de Latinoamérica y #5 del mundo" (Pollstar
    Q3 2025): #1 in Latin America by attendance, **#5** in the world. Sobre Nosotros says
    "número uno en asistencia de público en Latinoamérica, **cuarto** en el mundo".
  - Visitors: Sobre Nosotros' text says "más de **1,5 millones** de visitantes al año"; its
    counter strip on the same page says "**1.9** MM DE FANS AL AÑO". It also says "180 SHOWS
    ANUALES" and "#1 RECINTO LATAM".
  - Capacity (Sobre Nosotros, "EL RECINTO"): about 15,500 maximum; 12,000 numbered seats,
    3,200 movable floor chairs, 9 suites, 28 boxes, 10+ configurations, a 50 m high dome,
    44,000 m², evacuation in 16 minutes in Full Arena format.

## Getting there and parking
- **¿Cómo llegar? (`/como-llegar-a-santander-arena`)**, sections "Ingreso peatonal" and
  "Ingreso vehicular". On foot: always through **Parque O'Higgins (Metro) or calle Tupper**.
  By car: **only with a parking ticket bought in advance**, via **Av. Viel or Av. Tupper**,
  whichever the parking ticket says. Plan your arrival to avoid crowds; door times are posted
  on the arena's Instagram.
- **Estacionamientos (`/estacionamientos`)**: the same rule; parking is booked in advance on
  Puntoticket, "el único canal oficial de venta" (puntoticket.com/estacionamientos). The FAQ
  adds that spaces are limited. Event pages have a "Comprar estacionamiento" button for that show.

## What's on
- **Cartelera (`/cartelera`, H1 "Nuestra Cartelera")** lists every event, filtered by month
  ("Anterior" / "Siguiente"), with a search box ("¿Qué evento buscas?"), "Desde/Hasta" dates,
  "Fin de semana", "Este mes" and "Limpiar filtros". Each event card has "Ver más" (the event
  page), "Comprar tickets", and often parking and VIP icons.
- Event pages live at `/nuestra-cartelera/<categoría>/<slug>` (a few at `/nuestra-cartelera/<slug>`
  or `/cartelera/conciertos/<slug>`). Each shows the date as "5 de Noviembre / 21:00 hrs" and up
  to three buttons: **"Comprar tickets"**, **"Comprar estacionamiento"**, **"Compra
  Experiencias VIP"**, plus "Completa tu noche en Santander Arena" and "Te puede interesar".
- As of 2026-10-21 the site lists 66 upcoming events, from Matteo Bocelli (21 Oct 2026) to Eros
  Ramazzotti (3 Nov 2027). Headliners include Los Fabulosos Cadillacs, Camilo, Def Leppard,
  Bunbury, Hayley Williams, Deep Purple, four Anuel AA dates, Jack Johnson, Pixies, four La Oreja
  de Van Gogh dates, Luis Fonsi and Hilary Duff. **Always use `find_events` / the events-index
  topic for dates. Never quote a date from memory.** Almost every event is a concert.

## Tickets: only Puntoticket
The footer on every page: "Les recordamos que **la única venta oficial** para todos los shows en
Santander Arena es a través de **Puntoticket** en su web www.puntoticket.com y en sus puntos de
venta físicos oficiales. Respecto de otros sitios que comercializan tickets…, tanto el recinto
como las productoras no pueden responder frente a problemas que se generen con ellos." The FAQ
adds the arena's own box office ("boletería") on event days. The arena cannot reissue tickets.
Lost e-tickets are re-downloaded from Puntoticket.

## Experiencias Premium (menu)
- **Experiencias VIP (`/experiencias`)**: "Arena Lounge VIP" and "Arena Bar VIP". Both give
  access from door opening (about 18:30) through the show and after, one welcome drink, a Kit VIP
  (lanyard, credential, wristband), a paid bar menu and a VIP parking upgrade (only if you
  already have parking). Valid for one person, no Fast Pass, not tied to the show's producer.
  "¿Cómo funciona?" has four steps, starting "Compra tu Experiencia VIP en Puntoticket".
- **Santander Suites (`/santander-suites`)**: 9 suites in the lower stands (platea baja), 12–16
  people each, with lounge, private bathroom, cable TV and their own bar. Leased **only to
  companies, annually or every two years. No individual tickets.**
- **Boxes (`/box`)**: 28 boxes on the upper level in North and South wings, rented by companies;
  served by Bar Jack Daniel's (north) and Bar Sur.

## Arena Fans and the arena's recognitions
- Menu "Arena Fans" ▸ **Fotos Fans (`/arena-fans`)**: one page per show (e.g. `/arena-fans/aitana`)
  where fans download their photo in high quality after filling in name and email. **Únete a
  Nosotros (`/unete-a-nosotros`)** is the community sign-up (name, email). The top bar's
  "Suscríbete" opens the same form.
- Home carousel: **Cúpula Santander Arena** (`/cupula-santander-arena`, the venue's top award,
  listed by year 2023–2025), **Muro de Momentos en Vivo** (photos signed by the artist the same
  night), **Paseo de las Estrellas** (artists' handprints, e.g. Tony Hawk, Miguel Bosé, Romeo
  Santos, Jack Johnson, Rafael Nadal, Kidd Voodoo, Megadeth).

## Food and drink ("Espacios para disfrutar en Santander Arena")
- **Bares (`/bares`)**: BAR JACK DANIEL´S (Sector Norte, 4° piso), BAR SUR (Sector Sur, 4°
  piso), BAR NORTE (Acceso Norte, main entrance), BAR SCHWEPPES (Sector Norte, 1° piso). Note:
  exclusive services, availability depends on ticket type.
- **Terrazas (`/terrazas`)**: Terraza Norte (north access) and Terraza Escudo (south side).
- **Food trucks (`/food-trucks`)**: inside the Terrazas and at the North and South accesses, with
  self-service checkouts.
- **Puntos de venta (`/puntos-de-venta`) are food stands, not ticket outlets**: Vive Snack, with
  2 cafeterías and 13 puntos de venta inside the venue.
- You may not bring food or drink in (FAQ).

## Santander customer links
The site gives Santander customers no benefits or presales. It only links out: home card
"HAZTE CLIENTE SANTANDER" → banco.santander.cl current-account page; top bar "Santander
Rewards" → banco.santander.cl/rewards; footer "Santander" → banco.santander.cl/personas.

## FAQ in brief (`/preguntas-frecuentes`, 18 questions in 5 groups)
Entradas y Acceso · Ingresos y Seguridad · Estacionamientos y Movilidad · Experiencia en el
Recinto · Otros. Key answers: buy only on Puntoticket or at the box office on event days.
Children usually pay from age 2 (depends on the producer). Bring your ticket plus ID or passport.
Not allowed: bottles, food and drink, sharp objects, umbrellas, professional cameras, banners on
sticks, blunt objects. Everyone goes through security and a metal detector. Accessible entrances,
preferential areas and adapted bathrooms are available, and staff can escort you. No smoking or
vaping inside. Free Wi-Fi in some areas. Door times are on Instagram. Private and corporate
rentals go through Reservas.

## Lost and found, contact, other pages
- **Objetos perdidos (`/objetos-perdidos`)**: a form (name, surname, email, RUT, mobile, the
  show, a comment, contact by Correo or WhatsApp, optional photo). On the night, ask at the info
  stands at the North and South accesses.
- **Contacto (`/formulario-de-contacto`)**: form (name, email, phone, reason, message, file).
  Footer: +562 2770 2300, contacto@santanderarena.cl, prensa@santanderarena.cl.
- **Reservas (`/reservas-santander-arena`)**: "Realiza tus eventos en Santander Arena" plus 10
  PDFs (manuals, rates, rules, drone policy). There is no jobs page on the site.
- **Sustentabilidad**: recycling with Veolia Chile and Imeko (464 tons, 5.3 MM cigarette
  butts); inclusion with Agencia Inclusiva.
- **Arena News (`/arena-news`)**: 14 articles (Cúpula awards, Pollstar ranking, Megadeth on the
  Paseo de las Estrellas, Cadem "Marcas Ciudadanas").
