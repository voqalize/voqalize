# Food and drink: Bares, Terrazas, Food trucks, Puntos de venta

Sources: /bares; /terrazas; /food-trucks; /puntos-de-venta; home section "Espacios para disfrutar en Santander Arena"; /box; /preguntas-frecuentes (Q12).

The home section "Espacios para disfrutar en Santander Arena" has four banner cards: Bares, Terrazas, Food trucks, Puntos de venta. They are reachable only from the home page (they are not in the menu).

## Bares (`/bares`)
Intro: the bars are for the "previa" and to close the night. Each bar is an h2 (BAR NORTE is an h1 in the DOM):
- **BAR JACK DANIEL´S**: Sector Norte, 4° Piso. Jack Daniel's styling, for before or after the show.
- **BAR SUR**: Sector Sur, 4° Piso. Modern and comfortable, with a varied drinks menu.
- **BAR NORTE**: Acceso Norte, main entrance ("Ingreso principal").
- **BAR SCHWEPPES**: Sector Norte, 1° Piso. Cocktails, music, indoor and outdoor space.
- Below them is a banner linking to puntoticket.com/movistar-arena-productos and the note "Nota: Servicios exclusivos, con disponibilidad limitada según el tipo de ticket."
- The names Jack Daniel's and Schweppes link to `/santander-arena/bar-jack-daniel-s-1` and `/santander-arena/bar-schweppes`, which are 404s.
- Bar Jack Daniel's and Bar Sur also serve the Boxes (see /box).

## Terrazas (`/terrazas`)
"Las terrazas de Santander Arena son el complemento perfecto para vivir cada show o concierto de principio a fin", for before going in or after the show. Video, then two bold titles (`span.titulos`, not headings):
- **Terraza Norte**: at the north access. "un espacio fresco y lleno de estilo, pensado para arrancar la noche con buena vibra."
- **Terraza Escudo**: "en el lado SUR de Movistar Arena" (the page still uses the old name). A place to meet before and after the show.

## Food trucks (`/food-trucks`)
Present at every event: burgers, sweet options and cold drinks, with self-service checkouts ("cajas de autoservicio"). Found inside the Terrazas and at the North and South accesses, with music, a bar and places to sit. (Its "Terrazas" link goes to a 404, `/santander-arena/terrazas`.)

## Puntos de venta (`/puntos-de-venta`)
**This page is about food stands, not ticket sales.** "Vive Snack": 2 cafeterías and 13 puntos de venta across the venue, from coffee before the show to burgers, cabritas (popcorn), chocolates and drinks. "Con Vive Snack, cada show viene acompañado de la energía perfecta para vivirlo al máximo." Video at the top.

## Rules
FAQ 12: you cannot bring food or drink in. Inside there are bars and points of sale with food, drinks and snacks. FAQ 18 recommends arriving early for the previa and after in the bars and terraces.
