# Santander Suites and Boxes

Sources: /santander-suites (title "Santander Suites"); /box (title "Boxes"); /sobre-nosotros ("EL RECINTO").

Menu: Experiencias Premium ▸ Santander Suites / Boxes. Neither page has section headings: a page title, a one-line subtitle, then paragraphs (and a video on /box).

## Santander Suites (`/santander-suites`)
Subtitle: "Fideliza y sorprende con experiencias exclusivas para tu empresa".
- 9 suites in the best part of the lower stands ("platea baja"), each for 12 to 16 people.
- Each has its own lounge, private bathroom, cable TV and a bar exclusive to the sector, for before, during or after the show.
- Suite guests get special parking and dedicated service.
- **Leased to companies annually or for two years ("arriendo anual o bienal"). There are no tickets for individual sale.**
- No contact or price on the page. For enquiries, the site's channels are the Contacto form and contacto@santanderarena.cl (footer).

## Boxes (`/box`)
Subtitle: "Disfruta de tus eventos favoritos desde un espacio privado pensado para la comodidad y la atención de tus invitados."
- 28 boxes on the upper level, split into two wings, Norte and Sur, with a panoramic view of the whole venue. Comfortable seats, preferential access, dedicated service.
- Rented by companies for loyalty programmes or special actions. Guests can order from the bars: Bar Jack Daniel's (north) and Bar Sur act as the entrance to the boxes and also serve food and drink inside them.
- The menu label is "Boxes", the path `/box`, and the text says "Box".

Sobre Nosotros also counts "9 suites, 28 boxes".
