## Sobre Nosotros (`/sobre-nosotros`)
Menu: Nosotros ▸ Sobre Nosotros. Intro (no heading), then h2s "EL RECINTO", "LA HISTORIA" and a counter strip titled "SOBRE NOSOTROS".
- Intro: "el recinto techado multipropósito más importante de Chile", "número uno en asistencia de público en Latinoamérica, **cuarto** en el mundo y con más de **1,5 millones** de visitantes al año". Located in Parque O'Higgins, a meeting point for music, sport and culture. Bars and terraces for the previa and after. Concerts, sports, fairs, conferences and corporate events.
- EL RECINTO: maximum capacity about 15,500. 12,000 numbered seats, 3,200 movable floor chairs, 9 suites, 28 boxes, 10+ configurations. A 50 m high dome, 44,000 m² total area, evacuation in 16 minutes in "Full Arena" format.
- LA HISTORIA: work began in 1958 in Parque O'Higgins for the 1959 Basketball World Cup but stayed unfinished for decades ("elefante blanco"). In 2000, after the "sillazos" at a Davis Cup tie, it fell into disrepair again. In the early 2000s the State put it out to tender, won by Arena Bicentenario (a public-private partnership). It opened in 2006 as Arena Santiago with a visit from the Dalai Lama, and in 2008 was renamed Movistar Arena with its main sponsor. **The page stops there and never mentions the change to Santander Arena.**
- Counter strip: "# 1 RECINTO LATAM", "180 SHOWS ANUALES", "1.9 MM DE FANS AL AÑO". (These animate up from 0 when scrolled into view.)

## Disagreements to report, not resolve
- World rank: Sobre Nosotros says **4th** ("cuarto en el mundo"); the article "Somos el recinto #1 de Latinoamérica y #5 del mundo" (Pollstar Q3 2025) says **5th**. Both say #1 in Latin America.

- Name: many articles still say "Movistar Arena". The Megadeth article's browser title is Ricardo Arjona's article title (a CMS slip).

## Be Live
The footer links "Be Live" (LinkedIn). In the Pollstar interview (Daniel Hiller, CEO of Be Live; its overview is in English) Be Live Entertainment Group runs venues including Movistar Arena in Chile and Colombia, the promoter Bizarro, the ticketing platforms Punto Ticket (Chile) and Teleticket (Colombia), hospitality and F&B. Pollstar ranked Movistar Arena Chile #1 and Colombia #2 "as venue operators".
