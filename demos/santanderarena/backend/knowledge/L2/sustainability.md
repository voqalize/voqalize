# Sustainability (Sustentabilidad)

Sources: /sustentabilidad (title "Sustentabilidad").

Menu: Nosotros ▸ Sustentabilidad. Intro: the fans' experience also means a commitment to the environment and the community, so the arena works on two axes, recycling and inclusion. Sections (h2): "RECICLAJE", "INCLUSIÓN", "HEMOS RECICLADO".
- **RECICLAJE**: with strategic partners Veolia Chile and Imeko the arena manages its waste. There are 32 recycling containers and 30 cigarette-butt dispensers across accesses, bars and terraces. Waste is separated, classified, compacted and baled. So far it has recycled 464 tonnes of glass, cans and cardboard, plus more than 5 million cigarette butts.
- **INCLUSIÓN**: accessible routes through every access, and a partnership with Agencia Inclusiva on hiring people with disabilities: 9,000 of the 45,000+ people who have worked at the venue have reduced mobility.
- **HEMOS RECICLADO** (counter strip): "464 TONS. VIDRIO | LATAS | CARTÓN | PLÁSTICO" and "5.3 MM COLILLAS DE CIGARRO". (The counters animate up from 0. The text lists glass, cans and cardboard; the counter adds plastic.)
