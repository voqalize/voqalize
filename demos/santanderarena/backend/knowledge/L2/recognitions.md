# Recognitions: Cúpula, Muro de Momentos en Vivo, Paseo de las Estrellas

Sources: /cupula-santander-arena; /muro-de-momentos-en-vivo; /paseo-de-las-estrellas; the home carousel (no heading, just under "Próximamente"); Arena News articles.

These three pages are reached from the home carousel's cards ("Ver más"), not from the menu.

## Cúpula Santander Arena (`/cupula-santander-arena`)
"El máximo reconocimiento que entregamos a los artistas que han marcado hitos en la historia del recinto…" (the page calls it part of "el legado de Movistar Arena"). Section "¿QUÉ ARTISTAS TIENEN LA CÚPULA SANTANDER ARENA?", then by year (each artist is an h2):
- **2025**: CHAYANNE (career as a Latin icon and 27 sold-out shows at Movistar Arena); KIDD VOODOO (only Chilean and first South American with 7 sold-out shows); FEDE (only digital creator with 5 sold-out shows); EL PUMA (his last performance on the stage).
- **2024**: EMILIA (first woman with 3 sold-out shows); ANDRÉ RIEU ("UPON REACHING THE MILESTONE OF 23 PRESENTATIONS", the only line written in English); LOS TRES (first Chilean band with 4 sold-out shows); EDO CAROE (first comedian with 3 sold-out shows).
- **2023**: GINO MELLA (first Chilean artist with 3 sold-out shows); LUIS MIGUEL (record, 10 sold out); LOS JAIVAS (60 years of career).
Then "Te puede interesar" (Chayanne and Kidd Voodoo articles).
The Arena News article on **Ricardo Arjona** says he received La Cúpula as the artist with the biggest draw in the venue's history (42 sold-out concerts). He is **not** on the Cúpula page's list. The Gino Mella article says the award went earlier to Los Jaivas, Luis Miguel and Romeo Santos. Romeo Santos is not on the page's list either.

## Muro de Momentos en Vivo (`/muro-de-momentos-en-vivo`)
At each show a photo is taken, selected, printed and taken to the artist to sign that same night. Each signed photo joins a wall that grows show after show. No headings on the page.

## Paseo de las Estrellas (`/paseo-de-las-estrellas`)
Handprints of artists and personalities. Named: Tony Hawk, Miguel Bosé, Romeo Santos, Jack Johnson, Rafael Nadal, plus Kidd Voodoo and Megadeth. The Megadeth article explains the tradition: the artist leaves handprints in a mould that is cast into a star, displayed permanently in the venue. Megadeth did this at their 4–5 May 2026 shows (farewell tour "This Was Our Life"). The Arjona article says he left his handprints in 2014.
