# Experiencias VIP

Sources: /experiencias (title "Experiencias VIP"); event pages ("Compra Experiencias VIP"); home "Completa tu noche en Santander Arena".

Menu: Experiencias Premium ▸ Experiencias VIP. The page head is a banner image only (alt "EXPERIENCIAS VIP") that links to puntoticket.com/santander-arena-productos. There is no text title.

## Arena Lounge VIP
Subtitle: "Disfruta de comodidad y exclusividad en un espacio privado". A modern, exclusive space for before, during and after the show, with personal service. Includes:
- Access to the "Arena Salón VIP" from door opening (18:30 approx.; the exact time is confirmed on social media in the days before), during and after the show.
- One welcome drink: Pisco Sour, Copa Espumante, Aperol Spritz, Ramazotti Spritz, Sangría, Mango Sour, Cerveza, Cerveza 0°, Jugos y Bebidas, Tequila Margarita, Mojito Tradicional.
- Kit VIP: lanyard, credential and wristband.
- Bar menu (paid: "Carta de bar (de pago)").
- Upgrade de Estacionamiento VIP: if you already bought parking, move your car to a preferential sector closer to the venue, with preferential access.
- Fine print: the parking upgrade only applies if you already have parking (it does not include a new space). Valid for 1 person. Does not include a Fast Pass. "Producto no asociado a la productora a cargo del evento."
- Button "Ver más" → puntoticket.com/santander-arena-productos.

## Arena Bar VIP
Subtitle: "Vive un ambiente vibrante y exclusivo en tu bar favorito." The same inclusions (access from door opening, welcome drink, Kit VIP, paid bar menu, parking upgrade). Fine print adds: "La experiencia Premium Bar se realizará en uno de los bares habilitados, según cupos vigentes del evento." Button "Ver más" → the same Puntoticket page.

## ¿Cómo funciona?
1. Compra tu Experiencia VIP en Puntoticket. 2. Accede cómodamente al recinto. 3. Nuestros anfitriones te guiarán en todo momento. 4. Recibe tu Kit VIP y disfruta de tu Lounge o Bar exclusivo.

Then "Proximamente" (three event cards: Ludovico Einaudi 9 Marzo, Jungle 3 Abril, Daniel Caesar 10 Diciembre).

## Per event
Event pages that offer it have the button "Compra Experiencias VIP" (and the cartelera card a star icon with tooltip "Compra Experiencias VIP"). Not every event has one: see `vip_url` in events.json. The Bares page also has a banner linking to puntoticket.com/movistar-arena-productos (the old name) with the note "Servicios exclusivos, con disponibilidad limitada según el tipo de ticket."
