# Accessibility and inclusion

Sources: /preguntas-frecuentes (Q11); /sustentabilidad ("INCLUSIÓN"); the header's accessibility control.

The site has no dedicated accessibility page. What it says:
- FAQ 11, **"¿Hay acceso para personas con movilidad reducida?"**: yes. Accessible entrances, preferential areas and adapted bathrooms, and the team can accompany anyone who needs it from the entrance to their seat.
- Sustentabilidad ▸ **INCLUSIÓN**: "¡Porque en SANTANDER ARENA disfrutamos todos!" Accessible routes run through every access of the venue. The arena works with **Agencia Inclusiva** on hiring people with disabilities for events and congresses: of more than 45,000 people who have worked at the venue, 9,000 are people with reduced mobility.
- FAQ 9: in an emergency, go to the help points or a guard; security and health teams are on site.
- The site itself: the header has an accessibility button with text-size up/down and a light/dark switch ("modo claro" / "modo oscuro"; on mobile at the bottom of the menu).
- The site says nothing about wheelchair-space tickets, companion tickets, sign language or assistance animals. Point the visitor to Contacto (`/formulario-de-contacto`, contacto@santanderarena.cl, +562 2770 2300) and to the event's Puntoticket page.
