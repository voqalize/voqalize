# "Puntos de venta": two different things

Sources: /puntos-de-venta; the footer legal line; /preguntas-frecuentes (Q1).

The words mean two different things on this site. Ask which one the visitor means:
1. **The page "Puntos de venta" (`/puntos-de-venta`, home card under "Espacios para disfrutar en Santander Arena") is Vive Snack food and drink**: 2 cafeterías and 13 food points of sale inside the venue. It has nothing to do with tickets. See food-and-drink.
2. **Ticket outlets.** The footer says official sales are only through Puntoticket "en su web www.puntoticket.com y en sus puntos de venta físicos oficiales". The site does not list those physical outlets. FAQ 1 adds the arena's own box office ("boletería del recinto") on event days. For outlet locations, send the visitor to puntoticket.com. Do not invent addresses.
