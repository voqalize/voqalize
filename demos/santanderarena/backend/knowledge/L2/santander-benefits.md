# Santander links on the site

Sources: home "Completa tu noche en Santander Arena"; event pages (same block); header top bar; footer "Nosotros".

The site makes **no stated offer** for Santander customers: no presale, discount or ticket benefit appears in any page text. Its Santander content is three links to the bank's own site (all open in a new tab):
- Card **"HAZTE CLIENTE SANTANDER"** with button "Ver más aquí" → banco.santander.cl/personas/planes/cuenta-corriente. It is on the home page and on every event page under "Completa tu noche en Santander Arena". (The Santander Suites and Boxes pages carry an empty link to the same URL.)
- Top bar **"Santander Rewards"** → banco.santander.cl/rewards.
- Footer "Nosotros" ▸ **"Santander"** → banco.santander.cl/personas.

Santander Suites (`/santander-suites`) are corporate suites leased yearly. They are not a bank-customer benefit. If a visitor asks about bank benefits, say the arena's site doesn't list any and offer to open Santander Rewards. Never invent a presale.
