# FAQ (Preguntas frecuentes)

Sources: /preguntas-frecuentes (title "PREGUNTAS FRECUENTES"; 18 questions in five h2 groups). Menu: Planifica tu visita ▸ Preguntas frecuentes.

The page is not an accordion: every question is a numbered bold paragraph followed by its answer, all visible. Questions 10–11 are an `<ol>` and lose their visible numbers. The Spanish question text is kept verbatim for scrolling to it.

## Entradas y Acceso
1. **"¿Dónde puedo comprar mis entradas?"** Official tickets for every event are available only through PuntoTicket, or at the venue's box office on event days.
2. **"¿Los niños deben pagar entrada?"** It depends on the event and the producer. Generally children pay from age 2. Check the conditions when buying.
3. **"¿Qué documentos debo llevar para ingresar?"** Your ticket (printed or digital) plus your cédula de identidad or passport.
4. **"¿Qué pasa si pierdo mi entrada?"** Most tickets are e-tickets: download or print it again on PuntoTicket. Santander Arena cannot reissue tickets.
5. **"¿Puedo transferir mi entrada a otra persona?"** It depends on the event. Some tickets are nominative and cannot be transferred; others allow a change of holder, as long as the ticket is in the name of the person who will use it.

## Ingresos y Seguridad
6. **"¿Por dónde se ingresa al recinto?"** Pedestrians: Parque O'Higgins (Metro) or calle Tupper. Vehicles (only with a prior ticket): calle Viel or Tupper, as the parking ticket says.
7. **"¿Qué objetos no están permitidos?"** Bottles, food and drink, sharp objects, umbrellas, professional cameras, banners with sticks, blunt objects.
8. **"¿Hay control de seguridad al ingresar?"** Yes. Everyone passes a security check and metal detection.
9. **"¿Qué hago si tengo una emergencia durante el evento?"** Security staff and health teams are inside. Go to the help points or approach a guard.

## Estacionamientos y Movilidad
10. **"¿El recinto cuenta con estacionamientos?"** Yes, but limited. Buy in advance on PuntoTicket with your ticket.
11. **"¿Hay acceso para personas con movilidad reducida?"** Yes: accesses, preferential areas and adapted bathrooms, and the team can accompany anyone who needs it from the entrance to their seat.

## Experiencia en el Recinto
12. **"¿Puedo ingresar con comida o bebida?"** No, but inside there are bars and points of sale with food, drinks and snacks.
13. **"¿Se puede fumar o vapear dentro del Santander Arena?"** No smoking or vaping inside, only in the enabled outdoor areas.
14. **"¿El recinto cuenta con Wi-Fi?"** Yes, free Wi-Fi in parts of the venue.
15. **"¿Qué hago si pierdo un objeto durante el evento?"** Ask at the information stands at the North and South accesses inside the venue, or fill in the Objetos Perdidos form on the website. (The link goes to `/santander-arena/objetos-perdidos`, a 404. The working page is `/objetos-perdidos`.)

## Otros
16. **"¿Dónde encuentro la información de los próximos eventos?"** In the Cartelera section, kept up to date with every confirmed show.
17. **"¿Puedo arrendar Santander Arena para un evento privado?"** Yes, for corporate and private rentals; see the Reservas section. (The link goes to `/santander-arena/reservas`, a 404. The working page is `/reservas-santander-arena`.)
18. **"¿A qué hora debo llegar al show?"** Door opening times are announced on the official Instagram. Arrive early to enjoy the previa and after in the bars and terraces.
