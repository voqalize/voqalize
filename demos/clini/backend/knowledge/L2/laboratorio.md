## Booking and practicalities
- Online: agenda-web `https://agenda-web.clini.cl/?servicio=62&prestacion=30` ("Laboratorio" → "Toma De Muestra", the only prestación, preselected). Regions: RM (default, with sector Todos/Centro/Oriente/Sur/Poniente), O'Higgins, Araucanía, Ñuble. Buttons "Resevar online" (sic) on /laboratorio and "Reserva tu hora" on branch pages. Phone 22 994 4003; WhatsApp +56 9 8246 3202.
- No appointment needed (FAQ "¿Necesito hora para mis exámenes de laboratorio?"): booking is recommended, arrive 15 minutes early; walk-ins are served by order of arrival.
- agenda-web's lab alert ("Entendido"): gynaecological samples must be booked at Los Leones; Cortisol AM must be booked at 9 h and Cortisol PM is not done at any sede; orders written by a nutricionista or enfermera have no Fonasa cover (pay particular), and by a matrona only some items have cover.
- Children from 6 months at every toma de muestras; CLINI does not serve patients with latex allergy. What to bring: cédula de identidad (and the medical order). A boleta for reimbursement is emailed.
- Quotes: /cotizador or cotizador-web.clini.cl, including a photo-of-the-order AI quote; assisted quote by phone or WhatsApp.
- Results: see topic `resultados`. Preparations: topics `preparaciones` and `examenes-laboratorio`.

## Where the site disagrees with itself
- Number of exams: "más de 1.000" (/laboratorio) vs "hasta 900" / "más de 900" (FAQ, home, branch pages).
- Number of lab centres: "14 centros" (/laboratorio) vs 13 branch pages and 9 "Toma de Muestras" entries plus Astra centres on /ubicaciones.
- Sunday hours: "09:00 a 13:00" (/laboratorio) vs 08:30–13:00 on several branch pages; San Miguel is in the Sunday list but its campaign page says "Domingo: Sin Atención".
- Results timing: "24-48 horas" (/laboratorio), "24 horas" plus "express en 4 horas" at Metro Los Leones (/agendamiento FAQ), "en solo horas" (Los Leones and Santa Lucía pages).
- Prices: Examen de orina particular $5.130 (branch card) vs $1.960 (cotizador); Hormonas tiroideas particular $19.110 (branch card) vs Perfil Tiroideo $19.440 (/laboratorio). Quote the cotizador and suggest confirming when paying.
- Addresses: Metro Los Leones Coyancura 2254 (branch page) vs 2244 (/ubicaciones); Tobalaba 085 (branch page) vs 082 (/ubicaciones). Alameda hours differ between its branch page (7:30–15:45) and /ubicaciones (7:00–15:00).
- /laboratorio/maipu's heading says "Metro Los Leones" (a template slip); its address is Maipú.
- Fasting: /laboratorio's example says 8–12 h fasting in general, while the cotizador says "Sin indicaciones" for hemograma, creatinina or vitamina D. Give the exam's own preparation (topic `preparaciones`).
