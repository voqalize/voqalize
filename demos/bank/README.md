# Global Bank

A fictional Indian retail bank: a public website and a complete signed-in banking app, with one
voice assistant who drives the screen while he talks. Global Bank, its products, rates, fees and its
customer (Aditi Sharma) are all sample data; nothing here is a real bank, product or person.

It replaces three older demos (aura, kiosk, lead_qual) with one suite.

## Landing pages and what the assistant does there

| landing | job |
|---|---|
| `/demos/bank/app` | **Banking assistant.** Answers from the customer's own accounts, cards and transactions and opens the screen the answer lives on: "what was this ₹4,999 charge?" opens that transaction; "how much did I spend on food last month?" opens Insights on that category; "block my debit card" puts a confirmation sheet on screen and acts only after a yes (spoken or tapped). Bills, transfers, FDs, card limits and controls, EMI conversion and the pre-approved loan go through the same sheet. Cross-sell offers (a pre-approved personal loan, a card upgrade) sit in the app, and the assistant switches into card or loan mode when the customer goes there. |
| `/demos/bank/open-account` | **Account opening guide.** A six-step savings application (personal details, address, KYC with masked sample PAN and Aadhaar, nominee, account variant, review) that fills in as the customer talks, with a one-line reason for each step. |
| `/demos/bank/cards` | **Card salesperson.** Asks the few questions that matter (monthly income, how they spend), then `recommend_card` puts the one best card for this person beside the runner-up and he says it in two sentences: the card, what it is worth to them a year after fees, that they qualify, and an offer to start the application (prefilled). Fees, the rewards split and the runner-up come only when asked. Also ranks all cards side by side, takes the application, and converts a card spend to EMI with every tenure's EMI on screen. |
| `/demos/bank/loans` (and each loan's page) | **Loan salesperson.** Home, personal, gold and car loans: asks which loan (unless the page says), income and existing EMIs, and the amount or the price, then `recommend_loan` puts the eligibility check and the EMI calculator on screen with their numbers and he pitches the plan that fits in two sentences (amount, tenure, the EMI, that it fits their income, and an offer to apply); total interest, their own share of the price and the interest a shorter tenure saves come only when asked; the application ends in a qualified lead card for the bank's team. |
| `/demos/bank/` | The adviser at the front door: finds out what the visitor came for, takes them there, and sells. |

On the public pages Arjun sells (few right questions, then a two or three sentence pitch: the best option,
the one number that matters, one nudge to apply; more only when asked; never pushy). The pitch tools
return a SAY line (exactly that pitch) above a DETAIL block kept for follow-up questions; in the signed-in app and in account opening he stays a support assistant.

Sign-in has no real authentication, but the customer does it: they type a 10-digit mobile number
(starting 6 to 9) and a 4-digit PIN and press Sign in themselves; any such pair opens the sample
customer, Aditi Sharma. Nothing is pre-filled, and Arjun never asks for, hears, fills or reads back
either: signed out, a request for her own data opens the sign-in screen and he asks her to sign in;
digits said aloud are taken out before the model hears them (`backend/credentials.py`); a submit no
hand made is refused (`frontend/src/state/session.ts`). Once she signs in, he says "Hi Aditi, you're
signed in" and does what she asked.

## How it is built

- `CONTRACT.md` — the agreement between the page and the brain: routes, the Stage moves, the forms'
  field names, the page's events, and the maths both sides share.
- `persona.json` — the character (avatar id, display name, voice per language, languages, default).
- `data/products.json` (authored catalogue) and `data/customer.json` (generated by
  `data/build_customer.py` with a fixed seed). Both sides read the same files. Dates are kept as
  "days ago", so the data is always current.
- `backend/` — `BankBrain`, an `OpenAIBrain` at `reasoning="medium"`. Every on-screen act is a Stage
  move declared in `frontend/src/stage/scene.json` and generated into `backend/stage_gen.py` and
  `frontend/src/stage/scene.gen.ts`. Tools compute from the data, send moves and return at once;
  reads the model must speak from are `@needs_result_now`.
- `frontend/` — a Vite + React app. Every move also works by click alone; the voice layer is Stage's
  performers over the same stores the buttons use. `?debug=1` adds a console that fires any move by
  hand (`window.__bankMove(id, args)`).

## Redial

A call survives the brain's socket dropping (SDK 0.15.0 `Config.redial`, on wherever Voqalize offers
it). The live brain is held by session id in `backend/rejoin.py` for two minutes after a drop; the
brain Voqalize's redial starts adopts the whole call (screen, language, history, a sheet awaiting a
yes, her cards and balances) and does not greet again. Details in `CONTRACT.md`, Redial.

## The assistant widget

Paynora's widget, in the bank's palette (`frontend/src/voice/`): idle, a pill at the bottom right
with the character's still photo (`frontend/src/characters/<character>.webp`), the name, the job on
this page (`persona.json` `roles`, kept short so it fits a phone), EN / हिंदी and "Talk to Arjun"
("Talk" on a phone, where the name is already beside the face). In a call the pill goes away and
the face becomes a draggable tile with the live avatar (`@voqalize/avatar` 0.6.3), the language
toggle, mute, minimise and end. No transcript anywhere. The language is remembered across reloads.
While the confirmation sheet, a transaction's sheet (phone) or the app's menu is open, the pill and
the tile move to a top corner above it (`data-overlay` on each overlay). The dock is the page's only
way to start a call.

## Run it

```sh
# brain tests (no network, no key)
cd demos && uv run pytest tests/test_bank_e2e.py tests/test_demo_voice_contract.py -q

# the page
cd demos/bank/frontend && pnpm install --ignore-workspace && pnpm dev --port 5404
# open http://localhost:5404/demos/bank/  (add ?debug=1 for the move console)
```

For a live call, set `VITE_AGENT_ID` and `VITE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY` (see `frontend/.env.example`) for an
agent whose brain URL points at `wss://<brain host>/bank`. Without them the site works fully by click,
and the dock says voice is not configured in this build.

## Amounts in speech

The screen shows ₹1,25,000 and ₹1.25 crore; the voice has to say them as an Indian banker
would. Voqalize's TTS normalises text before the voice model reads it, and in English it reads
a rupee amount with two decimals before a scale word as rupees and paise: "₹1.25 crore" became
"one rupee and twenty five paisa crore". `backend/spoken.py` rewrites every amount the brain
speaks (the model's words and the brain's own lines) before it goes out, and keeps the screen's
formatting as it is:

- English: an amount with lakh or crore is spelled out ("one point two five crore rupees",
  "eight to twelve lakh rupees"). Plain amounts (₹1,57,332, ₹3,42,815.40) go as written; the
  TTS reads them in lakh and thousand.
- Hindi: lakh and crore in Devanagari with the ₹ mark ("₹1.25 करोड़"), which the TTS says as
  सवा करोड़ / डेढ़ लाख / ढाई लाख. Any other decimal becomes whole rupees ("₹1.85 करोड़" is said
  एक करोड़ पचासी लाख, not एक दशमलव आठ पाँच करोड़).
- Ranges say both ends ("8.65% to 9.4%").

The other way, `parse_amounts` reads what the recogniser heard: digits with either grouping,
lakh / crore / thousand / hazaar / k, number words in English and Hindi (Devanagari or Latin),
"one and a half", dedh, dhai, sava, saade, paune, decimals and Devanagari digits. When the
customer says an amount in words or with a scale, the model gets a note with it in rupees.
Every tool that takes a customer's amount (income, EMIs, loan amount, property or car price,
monthly spends) refuses one no customer would mean (₹15 for a loan) and tells the model to
confirm it back first ("Just to check, ₹1.5 lakh, right?").

## Change the character or add a language

Edit **`persona.json`** only:

- `character` — the avatar character id (add its still photo at `frontend/src/characters/<character>.webp`);
  `name` — the display name the page shows ("Talk to Arjun"); `roles` — the dock's label per landing;
  `spoken_names` — the name as the voice says it in each language.
- `voices` — the voice id per language; `languages` — the languages the toggle offers;
  `default_language`; `language_labels` (the toggle's labels) and `language_names`.

The page reads it for the dock, the toggle and alt text; the brain reads it for the greeting, the
voice and both speech legs, which it sets together in `on_session_start` and again on a switch.
Adding a language also needs its written lines in `backend/prompt.py` (`LINES`, `TIMES` and the
greetings), and a voice that speaks it: check the voice roster (`/api/v1/catalog.get`) first.
