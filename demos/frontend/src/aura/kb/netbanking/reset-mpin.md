Forgotten your 6-digit app MPIN? You can reset it from the login screen — no
branch visit.

## Steps
1. On the **Aura Mobile** login screen, tap **Forgot MPIN**.
2. Verify with **debit-card details** or **OTP** on your registered number.
3. Set a **new 6-digit MPIN**.

The MPIN-creation step is the same one shown in the registration video. If you're
locked out after multiple attempts, wait a while or call **1860-200-0100**.
