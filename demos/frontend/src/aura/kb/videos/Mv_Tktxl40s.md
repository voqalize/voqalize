Official Aura Bank screen recording of the **Aura Mobile** app debit-card section:
viewing the digital card, **setting / resetting the ATM PIN**, and **managing
usage** — turning domestic/international/online usage on or off and setting
transaction limits (the same controls a customer uses to lock a card they have
misplaced).

Path: **Aura Mobile → More → Debit Cards → (i) icon → OTP → card details**, then
either **Set/Reset PIN** or **Manage Usage**.
