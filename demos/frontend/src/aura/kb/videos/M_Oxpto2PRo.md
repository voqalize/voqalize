Official Aura Bank #EASYBANKING walkthrough (Hindi voiceover). A screen recording
of the **Aura Mobile by Aura Bank** mobile app showing exactly where the Interest
Certificate lives and how to view, save as PDF, or e-mail it. Used for tax filing
(home-loan interest under Section 24 / 80C, and deposit interest).

The path shown on screen: **Aura Mobile → profile (name) icon → Services & Support →
Services tab → Loans → Interest Certificate → choose tenor + financial year →
View / View PDF / Email**.
