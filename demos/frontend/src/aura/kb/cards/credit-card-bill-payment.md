Pay your Aura credit-card bill by the **due date** to avoid interest and late fees.

## Ways to pay
- **Aura Mobile app / Internet Banking:** Cards → **Pay Bill** (pay total or minimum due).
- **Set up Auto-Pay / standing instruction** so it's never missed.
- **NEFT** from another bank using your **16-digit card number as the account
  number** and IFSC **AURA0000400**.
- **Net banking of any bank** via the bill-pay (BillDesk) option.

> Paying only the **minimum due** keeps the card active but interest accrues on
> the rest. Pay the **total due** to stay interest-free.
