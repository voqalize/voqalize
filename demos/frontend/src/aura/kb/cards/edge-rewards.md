Your **Aura Reward Points** earned on your debit/credit card can be redeemed for
vouchers, products, or to offset spends.

## Where to see & redeem
- **Aura Mobile app / Internet Banking:** the **Aura Rewards** section — see your balance
  and the redemption catalogue.
- Points have an **expiry**, so redeem before they lapse.

> Reward balances are account-specific — log in to see yours. I can show you where
> the Aura Rewards section is and how redemption works.
