A quick guide to choosing a transfer mode and its limits.

## At a glance
- **UPI** — instant, 24×7; typical limit **₹1 lakh/day** (higher for some merchant
  categories).
- **IMPS** — instant, 24×7; good for everyday transfers.
- **NEFT** — 24×7, settles in batches; no upper cap from the bank for most.
- **RTGS** — for **₹2 lakh and above**, real-time, 24×7.

> **Newly added payees** often start with a **lower limit and a short cooling
> period** for safety; limits step up after that. Exact per-account limits show in
> the Aura Mobile app.
