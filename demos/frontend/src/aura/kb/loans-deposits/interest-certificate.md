You need an **Interest Certificate** at tax-filing time — it shows the interest
you **paid** on a loan (home/education/personal, used for deductions under
Section 24 / 80C / 80E) or the interest you **earned** on deposits. You can get
it yourself in under a minute, no branch visit needed.

## On the Aura Mobile app
1. Log in to the **Aura Mobile** app with your credentials.
2. Tap the **profile / name icon** at the top.
3. Open **Services and Support → Services** tab.
4. Tap **Loans**, then open the **Interest Certificate** section.
5. Choose the **tenor** and the **financial year** you need (e.g. the last
   financial year for this year's filing).
6. Tap **View** to see it, **View PDF** to open the PDF, or **Email** to have it
   sent to your registered e-mail ID.

## On Internet Banking
Login → **Loans → Services → Interest Certificate → Download / E-mail**.

> **Need to log in.** The certificate is account-specific, so you must be logged
> into the app or Internet Banking. I can show you exactly where it is and walk
> you through it — the download happens on your own login.

**Can't log in / no app?** Call **1860-200-0100** or visit your nearest Aura Bank
branch / Loan Centre.
