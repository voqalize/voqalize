# SendPotion (Potion) — what Javier knows

Compiled 2026-10-16 from sendpotion.com (27 English pages) and blog.sendpotion.com (145 posts). Every fact here is the site's own; nothing is from general knowledge. Where the site's pages disagree, the disagreement is written down, not resolved. The product is called **Potion**; the website is sendpotion.com and the app is app.sendpotion.com.

## What Potion is
- An AI video generator that makes videos **in your own face, voice and gestures**. Home: "Generate UNLIMITED AI videos in your own face, voice & gestures." "Potion is an advanced video generation AI… The AI technology learns how you speak, emote, and gesture when you talk and then creates videos on your behalf." Built for personalized outreach at scale.
- Two ways to make videos: (1) **record one video template** with your webcam and let Potion personalize it per recipient (name, company, logo, the prospect's website as a scrolling background, other data points); Potion saves each recorded name so you never record it twice; (2) **type a script** and get a full AI video in your face and voice. It can also turn screen recordings and front-cam videos into AI videos.
- How it works (home, "How Potion works"): **Train video AI (Takes 4 mins)** → **Type video script or record a video template** → **Get videos in your own face & voice**. Training needs under 5 minutes: "a minute-long video and 30-sentences" (/faq/). Videos are typically ready "within a few minutes".
- Delivery: a branded video landing page with your logo, colors, custom domain, CTA button and calendar embed; a 3-second GIF preview goes into emails (no attachments); share by email, LinkedIn, SMS, Slack or a quick-share link; analytics on views, watch time and CTA clicks.
- Who it is for: sales teams, SDRs, agencies, founders doing outbound; also marketing, support, customer success, product teams, real estate agents, e-commerce stores and recruiters. Values page: "We exist to help sales teams win."
- Social proof: "Trusted by 13000+ companies"; logos SAP, PandaDoc, Orum, Opensense, AppsFlyer, CaptivateIQ, Collective, MaintainX. Quotes: Grow Pronto (45 meetings, $168K+ annual), SAP (case study: $600K+), Orum ("response rate by 570%").

## Features in brief (/features/)
Accurate lip-sync; natural gestures; AI screen recordings with unique backgrounds; AI face-cam videos (one video → thousands of versions); custom branded landing pages and custom domains; calendar embeds; interactive previews (stickers, emojis, GIFs); personalized greeting by name; dynamic backgrounds incl. scrolling prospect websites; custom CTA buttons; GIF thumbnails; CSV uploads for thousands of videos; 50+ integrations; multi-channel sending; SOC2. Also: multi-language generation, VSL creator, video analytics, Zapier webhooks (Professional), whitelabel (Enterprise), a Chrome extension.

## Pricing (/pricing/; also on the home page) — per month, per workspace
- **Personal $39** — 250 Dynamic AI videos/mo, 1 Full AI video template/mo. 7-day free trial.
- **Starter $99** — 750 Dynamic AI videos/mo, 3 Full AI video templates/mo. 7-day free trial. Same features as Personal.
- **Professional $299** — **Unlimited** AI videos; adds Zapier webhooks, multi-user workspaces, roles & permissions, custom branding, custom domains, team and admin features, priority support. 7-day free trial.
- **Enterprise "Let's Talk"** — custom needs; whitelabel video AI, strategy consultations, team coaching, highest priority support, SOC2, TLS 1.2. Button "Contact sales" → /demo/.
- No monthly/annual toggle. Terms: monthly, auto-renews, no refund after a 7-day introductory period, one likeness and one voice per license, unused videos don't roll over. AI videos up to 5 minutes (/pricing/ FAQ).
- **Disagreements**: /reviews/ FAQ says "a premium plan for $247 per month" (quote /pricing/ instead). A Feb 2024 blog post announced a free plan; the site today shows trials, not a free plan. /personalized-video-emails/ says there's "no actual time limit" on length (ideally under 2 minutes).

## Security and ethics
SOC 2 Type 1 (independent audit); card data held by Stripe, none on Potion's servers; hosted on AWS; encrypted backups and a disaster-recovery plan; TLS 1.2 in transit (/security/). AI ethics (/ai-ethics/): videos only after the user's consent and webcam recording, deepfakes prohibited, users can delete their avatar and data in account settings, GDPR and SOC2 Type I.
