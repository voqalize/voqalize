# Pricing and plans

Sources: https://sendpotion.com/pricing/ (section "Pricing plans", section "FAQs"); the same four plans are on the home page, section "Pricing plans"; https://sendpotion.com/reviews/ (FAQ "What pricing plans does Potion offer?"); https://sendpotion.com/terms/; https://sendpotion.com/video-sales-letter-software/ (FAQ); https://sendpotion.com/use-cases/ (FAQ).

## The four plans (as shown on /pricing/ and the home page)
Prices are per month, per workspace. Every paid plan's card says "7-day free trial". There is **no monthly/annual toggle** on the site: one price per plan, monthly.

| Plan | Price | Video allowance | Button |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personal | $39 / mo / workspace, 7-day free trial | 250 Dynamic AI videos / mo; 1 Full AI video template / mo | "Try for Free" → app.sendpotion.com (new tab) |
| Starter | $99 / mo / workspace, 7-day free trial | 750 Dynamic AI videos / mo; 3 Full AI video templates / mo | "Try for Free" → app.sendpotion.com (new tab) |
| Professional | $299 / mo / workspace, 7-day free trial | **Unlimited** AI videos / mo | "Try for Free" → app.sendpotion.com (new tab; the highlighted card) |
| Enterprise | "Let's Talk" | "Custom AI video needs" | "Contact sales" → /demo/ (book a demo, same site) |

**Personal and Starter include the same features:** AI Face Cloning, AI Voice Cloning, AI Gesture Cloning, AI Video Personalization, Multi-language Generation, 50+ CRM Integrations, 'Export-less' Integrations, Dynamic Videos, Dynamic Video Backgrounds, Screen Recording to AI Video, Video Analytics, VSL Creator. The difference is the allowance (250 vs 750 dynamic videos, 1 vs 3 full AI video templates).

**Professional:** Generate Unlimited Videos; All Starter Features; Zapier Webhooks; Multi-user Workspaces; Roles & Permissions; Custom Branding; Custom Domains; All Team Features; All Admin Features; Priority Support.

**Enterprise:** All Starter Features; All Professional Features; Whitelabel Video AI; AI Video Strategy Consultations; On-demand Team Coaching; Highest Priority Support; SOC2 Compliant; Data Encryption With TLS 1.2.

Which plan for whom (from the plan contents only): a single person sending up to 250 personalized videos a month → Personal; up to 750 → Starter; a team that needs several users, roles, custom branding/domains, Zapier webhooks or unlimited videos → Professional; white-label, coaching or custom needs → Enterprise (book a demo).

## Pricing FAQs (/pricing/, section "FAQs")
- **What happens if I exceed the limits of my plan?** You'll be prompted to upgrade or manage your usage.
- **Are there any restrictions on the length of AI-generated videos?** Length is flexible; "full-length AI videos can be up to 5-minutes long"; shorter videos often perform better.
- **How does Potion help with boosting engagement rates?** Bulk generation of videos; export multiple videos simultaneously.
- **Does Potion show analytics?** Yes: viewer engagement and video performance.

## Other pricing facts on the site
- /video-sales-letter-software/ FAQ: "our most basic plan gives you 250 video AI credits" (matches Personal).
- /use-cases/ FAQ: depending on the plan you can create an unlimited number of videos (that is the Professional plan).
- /use-cases/ tab "How Potion AI videos help": "as low as $0.1 per video"; /ai-personalized-shopping-cart-abandonment-solutions/: "for less than $0.10 per interaction".
- Terms (/terms/, "Term and termination"): one-month service term that renews automatically unless cancelled at least 30 days before the end of the term; after a 7-day introductory period following payment and activation, no refunds. "User License Restrictions": each user license is a single visual likeness and a single voice; unused monthly video caps do not roll over.
- Affiliates get "15 days free on any plan" (see topic `affiliate-and-partners`).

## Where the site disagrees with itself
- /reviews/ FAQ "What pricing plans does Potion offer?" says: "Potion offers a premium plan for $247 per month." That figure appears nowhere else; the pricing page shows $39 / $99 / $299 / Let's Talk. Quote the pricing page and say the plans are listed at /pricing/.
- The blog announces a free plan (post "Introducing Potion's Free Plan"); the website today shows a 7-day free trial on paid plans and no free plan. Quote the website.
- Video length: /pricing/ FAQ says AI videos up to 5 minutes; /personalized-video-emails/ FAQ says "there's no actual time limit", ideally under 2 minutes.
