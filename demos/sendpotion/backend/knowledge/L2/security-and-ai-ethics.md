# Security, privacy and AI ethics

Sources: https://sendpotion.com/security/; https://sendpotion.com/ai-ethics/; https://sendpotion.com/privacy/; https://sendpotion.com/terms/; https://sendpotion.com/faq/ (FAQ "Is my data safe…"); https://sendpotion.com/pricing/ (Enterprise plan).

## /security/ (footer "Security")
H2 "Security", then "Potion is trusted by thousands of professionals at large enterprises, startups, and SMBs and we use the best tools to keep your data safe." Five cards:
- **SOC2 compliant** — "Potion is SOC 2 TYPE 1 compliant, meaning Potion's products and infrastructure undergoes a SOC 2 TYPE 1 audit which is performed by an independent third-party auditor."
- **Credit Card data safety** — handled by Stripe on PCI-compliant servers; Potion's servers and databases store no credit card information.
- **Secure & Reliable infrastructure** — hosted on AWS (AWS is compliant with SOC1, SOC 2, SOC 3, ISO 27001 and HIPAA — that is AWS's compliance, not a Potion claim).
- **Outage recovery** — business continuity & disaster recovery plan; all assets backed up; backups encrypted.
- **Data encryption** — in transit with TLS 1.2.
The Enterprise plan lists "SOC2 Compliant" and "Data Encryption With TLS 1.2". The home page and /faq/ say "SOC2 Type I compliant".

## /ai-ethics/ (footer "AI Ethics")
H2 "AI Ethics at Potion", subheading "Protecting Identity, Promoting Responsibility". Sections "Protecting Your Privacy" and "Safeguarding Against Identity Theft".
Section **"How Potion practically & ethically uses AI"** — tabs:
- **Consent**: "Consent-Driven Processes" — generation proceeds only after user consent; "you can only record videos using your web camera", so your likeness is never used without permission. "Data Encryption" — TLS 1.2 in transit, secure storage.
- **Misuse Prevention**: "Avoiding Deepfake Misuse" — deceptive or harmful content is prohibited; deepfake creation is against policy and terms. "Continuous AI Monitoring".
- **Ethical Innovation**: "Transparency in Communication" — Potion makes it easy to tell viewers a video is AI-generated. "Ethical Marketing Practices".
- **Transparent Communication**: "Promoting Culture of Ethics"; "Upholding the Principles".
FAQs: can someone misuse my avatar (no; consent required; usage tracked); preventing deepfakes; labelling AI videos (disclaimers or visual cues encouraged); bias (regular audits); privacy ("fully compliant with global data protection standards such as GDPR and SOC2 Type I"); deleting your AI avatar and data (yes, in account settings; data securely erased); handling breaches (affected users alerted; backups encrypted).
