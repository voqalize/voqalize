## What some integrations do (card text, quoted where the tool is named)
- Apollo: "Engage and convert on Apollo's millions of B2B contacts by accessing video directly from their email automation platform."
- Chime: select existing Potion videos within the Chime email composer, or add drips.
- Close: add personalized Potion videos in the Close email composer, or schedule them as drip campaigns.
- Zendesk: create videos inside Zendesk; let the support team send video from within Zendesk.
- Gmail: record and send video messages directly from Gmail.
- GoHighLevel: tailored video content in GoHighLevel workflows.
- Groove: add videos into Groove templates and send them in Flows.
- HubSpot: personalized videos in HubSpot emails, snippets, sequences and inbound programs, with engagement tracking.
- Instantly: customized video messages in Instantly email templates.
- Lemlist: create and send personalized video emails from your Lemlist account, with campaign performance updates.
- LinkedIn: share Potion video emails as DMs via your LinkedIn account.
- Outlook: record and send video emails inside Outlook with tracking.
- Outreach / Reply / SalesLoft: add videos into sales emails.
- Klenty, Quickmail, Customer.io (trigger videos on customer behavior), Mailshake, Smartlead.ai, Yesware, Klaviyo, Interseller, Monday.com, ConvertKit, Pipedrive, Copper, ActiveCampaign, SugarCRM, Dynamics365, Google Sheets (manage outreach campaigns and track results).
