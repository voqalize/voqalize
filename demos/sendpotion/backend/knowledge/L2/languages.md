## Page structure (/supported-languages/, footer "Supported Languages")
H1 **"Speak the Language of Your Audience"** — "Generate AI videos in 30+ languages, with seamless face, voice, and gesture adaptations for a truly personal touch." Then H1 **"Supported languages"** (the flag list), **"How AI videos work"** (Train video AI (Takes 4 mins) → Type or paste your video script → Get videos in your own face & voice), FAQs, and **"Benefits of Using Potion for Multilingual Videos"**: Build Trust and Credibility; Increased Engagement; Scale Seamlessly; Consistent Branding; Localized video landing pages; Region-Specific Video Pages.

FAQs: how Potion adapts face, voice and gestures per language (the page's answer only repeats the question); difficulty (not difficult; minutes, no extra recording); benefits; limit on languages per project (no limit); support for troubleshooting (guides, video tutorials and live support).

The website itself also exists in Spanish (/es/) and Portuguese (/pt-br/) via the EN / ES / PT switch in the top nav; this knowledge covers the English pages only.
