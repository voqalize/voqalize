## Affiliate program (/affiliate/, footer "Affiliate Program")
H1 **"Earn 30% recurring for 12 months"** — "Recommend the AI platform that creates personalized sales videos in your own face, voice and gestures, and get paid every time your referrals pay." Button **"Join the program"** → https://potion-1.getrewardful.com/signup (Rewardful, another site).
- **How it works**: Join (sign up in a minute, no approval wait) → Share (your unique link in reviews, comparisons, newsletters, videos or outreach) → Get paid (30% of each payment for up to 12 months).
- **What you get**: 15 days free on any plan — register on a plan, email support@sendpotion.com, and they activate it; can be extended to a full month.
- **Conditions**: Commission 30% of every payment from referred customers; Duration 12 months from each customer's first payment; Cookie duration 60 days; Minimum payout USD 50; Payment once a month through rewardful.com; Self-referrals not eligible.
- FAQs: who Potion is for (sales teams, SDRs, agencies, founders doing outbound); who can join (bloggers, reviewers, newsletter authors, YouTubers, agencies, consultants); getting the 15 free days; how long commission lasts (12 months); help with content (logos, screenshots, demo video — email them).
- **Disagreement**: the older blog post announces "15% recurring revenue"; the affiliate page today says 30% for 12 months. Quote the affiliate page.
