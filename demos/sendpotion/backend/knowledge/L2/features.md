Clone your face & voice (no retakes); Clone your gestures and expressions; Screen recordings to AI videos (personalized backgrounds); Custom branded video pages (your branding & domain); Custom video player themes (stickers, emojis, gifs, images); Designed to be secure and safe ("Potion AI platform is SOC2 Type I compliant").

## Details stated elsewhere
- Dynamic screen recording from a URL: a review on /personalized-video-emails/ notes you can add a dynamic screen recording "by just entering the URL of a customer's website".
- Multiple video backgrounds in one video: yes (/ai-video-prospecting-software/ FAQ).
- Overlays: text and logos ("basic overlays") (/personalized-video-emails/ FAQ).
- Personalization data points: name, video background, purchase history, "any other data point" (/personalized-video-emails/).
- Analytics: views, watch times, clicks on CTAs, overall engagement (/use-cases/ FAQ).
- Team: team workspaces, shared video templates, team performance tracking (/video-sales-letter-software/ FAQ); multi-user workspaces and roles are Professional-plan features.
- Templates can be saved and reused (/use-cases/ FAQ).
- Chrome extension: "You can even use Potion's chrome extension to add videos to your outreach emails" (/reviews/ FAQ). Footer link "Chrome Extension" opens the Chrome Web Store listing "Potion: video emails to do…" in a new tab.
- Plan feature names (from /pricing/): 'Export-less' Integrations, Dynamic Videos, Dynamic Video Backgrounds, Screen Recording to AI Video, Video Analytics, VSL Creator, Zapier Webhooks, Whitelabel Video AI.
