## /faq/
- **What is Potion?** An AI that creates videos in your own face and voice: digital avatars, text-to-video, AI video prospecting, video translations, bulk personalized video generation, screen recording to AI video.
- **How to generate videos using Potion?** Create an account, choose a video type (screen recordings or dynamic videos), personalize with names or logos (AI syncs your voice to mention the details), customize thumbnails, host on a branded landing page, integrate with your CRM to send and track.
- **How long does it take to process and generate videos?** Generally a few minutes on average; depends on length and complexity.
- **How much video recording is required to train Potion's AI?** Less than 5 minutes: a minute-long video and 30 sentences.
- **Why should I use Potion for my B2B sales?** Personalized videos make outreach more compelling: higher engagement and response.
- **Is my data safe when using Potion's AI technology?** Yes, SOC2 Type I compliant.
- **Is Potion suitable for any industry?** Yes: tech, finance, real estate or any other sector.
- **How does Potion's AI enhance video personalization?** It identifies customizable elements like names and greetings and generates a personalized video per prospect.
- **How can Potion help me save time in my sales workflow?** Record one video; Potion personalizes it for each prospect. (Shown twice.)
- **How does Potion improve response rates in B2B sales?** "AI personalized videos can increase response rates by up to 150%. And for many of our clients, it has even crossed 500%."
- **Do I need to create individual personalized videos for each prospect?** No. Record once, record the names/greetings; Potion saves each greeting so you never record the same name twice.
- **How does Potion ensure that the personalized videos don't feel robotic?** It analyzes speech patterns, facial expressions and other nuances.
- **Do I have to send my videos as an email attachment?** No. A 3-second GIF preview is inserted automatically into campaign emails; no download or upload.
- **How does Potion's AI decide which gestures to use in a video?** Contextual analysis of the speech content, intended emotion and purpose. (Shown twice.)
- **Can I integrate Potion with my existing CRM or marketing tools?** Yes: Salesforce, HubSpot and 50 other products. (Shown twice.)
