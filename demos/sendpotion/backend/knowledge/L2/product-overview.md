# What Potion is and how it works

Sources: https://sendpotion.com/ (sections "Make AI Videos for …", "How Potion works", "Why use Potion?", "Use Potion to generate AI videos in your own personality", "Frequently Asked Questions"); https://sendpotion.com/faq/; https://sendpotion.com/use-cases/ (tab "How to get started"); https://sendpotion.com/examples/ (tab "How to get started"); https://sendpotion.com/about/.

## In one line
Potion (site: sendpotion.com, app: app.sendpotion.com) generates AI videos in your own face, voice and gestures. Home page hero: "Make AI Videos for sales / marketing / support" (the last word is typed and cycles) — "Generate UNLIMITED AI videos in your own face, voice & gestures." Hero badges: "Clone yourself", "Unlimited HD videos", "Supports multiple languages".

FAQ answers on the home page and /faq/:
- "Potion is an advanced video generation AI. It allows you to create AI personalized videos in your own face and voice at scale. The AI technology learns how you speak, emote, and gesture when you talk and then creates videos on your behalf."
- /faq/ "What is Potion?": "an AI that creates videos in your own face and voice", with digital avatars, text-to-video conversion, AI video prospecting, video translations, bulk personalized video generation, and screen recording to AI video conversion.

## Two ways to make a video
- **Self-recorded then personalized ("dynamic videos")**: record one video template with your webcam, then Potion personalizes it per recipient (name, company, logo, website background, other data points). /faq/: "You can record your video once, then record the names (or greetings) for your prospects, and Potion's AI automatically creates personalized videos for each of the recipients. Potion saves every greeting recording as you go so you never have to record the same name twice."
- **AI-generated from text ("full AI videos")**: type a script; the video is generated in your face and voice. Home FAQ: "AI-generated videos … use text input to create videos automatically with your face & voice, enabling you to scale your video production without needing to record each one individually."
- Also: convert front-cam videos and screen recordings into AI videos (home, "Generate videos from a script, convert frontcam videos and screen recordings into AI videos").

## How Potion works (home page section "How Potion works", three steps)
1. **Train video AI (Takes 4 mins)**
2. **Type video script or record a video template**
3. **Get videos in your own face & voice**

Training detail (/faq/ "How much video recording is required to train Potion's AI?"): less than 5 minutes of recording — "a minute-long video and 30-sentences".

Getting started (/use-cases/, tab "How to get started" of "Benefits of 'The Potion Way' of Video Generation"): Sign Up → Train Video AI (a few minutes, face and voice) → Choose a Specific Goal → Share Your Masterpiece (email, CRM integrations, websites).
/examples/ version (tab "How to get started"): 1. It's super easy! 2. Record a video template (webcam; add GIFs, CTA) 3. Add prospects to the template (import names through your CRM; record their names or let AI generate them) 4. Click the Generate button! (use the generated links in campaigns, track performance).

## Why use Potion (home, section "Why use Potion?")
- Personalized AI videos in your own face, voice & gestures — "Generate AI videos with accurate lip-sync for sales, marketing, support & user education".
- Increase engagement on campaigns.
- Improve support and user education — respond to support queries, generate video tutorials.

## Speed, delivery, realism (from /faq/)
- Processing: "On average, videos can be generated and processed within a few minutes."
- No attachments: each video gets an automatic 3-second GIF preview that is inserted into campaign emails; no download/upload needed.
- Gestures are chosen by contextual analysis of the speech content and intended emotion; gestures are synced with the audio (home FAQ). Lip-sync is described as "highly accurate".
- Industry fit: "tech, finance, real estate or any other sector".
