## Who Uno Salud Dental is
- A Chilean network of dental clinics: "la red dental más importante del país". The clinic list has 103 clinics, "de Arica a Punta Arenas". The home page says "+100 Clínicas", "+2.000.000 Pacientes atendidos", "+15 Años Tratando Sonrisas", "+1.600 Odontólogos y especialistas dentales".
- "Primera clínica dental del país en obtener la acreditación y reacreditación ante la Superintendencia de Salud." Each clinic is approved by the SEREMI de Salud, with minor surgery rooms, digital X-ray rooms (panorámica, scanner, periapicales) and its own sterilisation.
- Implants are BioHorizons (US brand, "más de 50 años de experiencia"); aligners are Keep Smiling; supplies Solventum, BioHorizons, Kerr, KeepSmiling.

## The 11 specialties (each has its own page under /especialidades/)
- Limpieza Dental (destartraje): removes tartar, plaque and stains. No list price; only promotions.
- Ortodoncia: brackets metálicos, cerámicos, alineadores invisibles. Evaluación inicial desde $90.210. Metálicos: instalación $383.500, mensualidad $46.900, retiro $382.080. Cerámicos: $582.800, mensualidad $48.900, retiro $382.080. Invisibles (Keep Smiling): evaluación $119.810, instalación $1.273.890, mensualidad $51.470, retiro $198.960. Interceptiva (niños): evaluación y tratamiento desde $92.210.
- Implantes Dentales: implante unitario $361.900 (incluye derecho a pabellón e insumos); corona sobre implante atornillada $476.900; scanner extraoral $64.900 (1 maxilar) or $74.900 (2 maxilares). Lifetime manufacturer guarantee, attending the controls.
- Endodoncia (tratamiento de conducto): removes the damaged nerve and seals the tooth. Price depends on the tooth and the infection; no list price.
- Periodoncia: gums, bone and ligaments (gingivitis, periodontitis, bleeding gums, bad breath).
- Odontopediatría: children and adolescents.
- Blanqueamiento Dental: up to 2 shades lighter. No list price; promotions only.
- Radiografías Dentales: digital X-rays, "potenciadas con IA".
- Cirugía Dental Oral: wisdom teeth, extractions, biopsies, under local anaesthesia.
- Rehabilitación Oral: fixed and removable prostheses, crowns.
- Plano de Relajación (bruxismo): a night guard for clenching or grinding.
Prices are "desde" and referential: "Los valores pueden cambiar según los beneficios de cada paciente." Payment: up to 12 interest-free instalments on specialty pages.

## Booking an appointment (the visitor's experience)
- Every "Agenda tu hora" opens /agenda-tu-hora/, the online booking, on the same site. What is booked is a "hora de diagnóstico". The diagnosis has no cost; the X-rays are paid ("El diagnóstico no tiene costo, sólo se cancelan las radiografías").
- Step 1 is the visitor's alone: they type the patient's RUT, tick "Acepto los Términos y Condiciones", tick "No soy un robot" and press Continuar. Why: the RUT identifies the patient in the clinical record, the terms are their own consent, and the captcha proves a person is booking; the booking shows no clinic or hour before this, so Javier cannot look up hours first.
- Then: a new patient fills "Registro de Nuevo Paciente" (Javier can fill it as they dictate; they press "Registrarse"); a known patient confirms their details; a patient who already has an hour is told to call 600 707 1010.
- Then Javier picks with them: the motivo, the region, comuna and clinic, the day and the hour. The last step shows patient, date, hour, dentist, address and reference: the visitor presses "Confirmar cita".
- Motivos: 14 Limpieza Dental · 15 Urgencia (de ortodoncia, dolor agudo o fractura dental) · 16 Instalación de frenillos o alineadores invisibles · 17 Implantes dentales (solución fija) · 18 Prótesis dental (solución removible) · 19 Estética dental (blanqueamiento o carillas) · 20 Atención infantil (odontopediatría) · 21 Sangrado de encías o mal aliento · 22 Chequeo y diagnóstico general · 23 No estoy seguro.

## Te llamamos, urgencias and the call center
- Te llamamos (/landing/solicitar-diagnostico/): a form (nombre, apellidos, RUT, correo, teléfono, región, comuna; ENVIAR) and an executive calls them back. The visitor types the RUT and presses ENVIAR.
- Call Center 600 707 1010 (from mobiles also (02) 27638900). Phone hours: lunes a viernes 8:00 a 20:00, sábados 9:00 a 17:00.
- Urgencias: attended in every clinic, lunes a viernes 9:00 a 19:00, sábado 9:00 a 13:00; some clinics until 18:00 on Saturdays (Antofagasta, Valparaíso Pedro Montt, Chile España, Estación Central, Maipú, Padre Hurtado, Puente Alto, La Cisterna, San Bernardo, San Fernando, Curicó, Los Ángeles, Puerto Montt, Osorno). X-rays are taken at once if needed. For an urgency: call 600 707 1010 (the executives route them to the clinic with most availability), or book with motivo 15. /urgencias/ also has a callback form.
- Each clinic's own hours are on its page; only Moneda's were read: lunes a viernes 8:30 a 20:00, sábados 9:00 a 14:00. No clinic has its own phone.

## Paying, convenios and plans
- Cash or debit; up to 12 cheques without interest (minimum $50.000); bank cards up to 36 instalments, up to 12 interest-free with Transbank; Isapre excedentes (online at the clinic for Consalud, Banmédica, Colmena, Vida Tres); supplementary insurance reimbursement.
- Convenios: Fonasa up to 50%; most isapres 60% (Colmena up to 70%); cajas de compensación 60% (La Araucana up to 70%); insurers mostly 60% (Confuturo 53%); Coopeuch 60%. PAD (adolescents 12 to 17) with Cruz Blanca and Nueva Más Vida. GES (guaranteed by law for Fonasa and Isapre members): a patient who comes without knowing is referred to their Isapre to start it.
- Planes Sonrisa Protegida LITE / PLUS / FULL: 60 / 65 / 70% off treatments, 100% coverage of ambulatory urgencies; anual $49.990 / $69.990 / $109.990, semestral $37.059 / $51.563 / $81.856.
- Convenio Empresas (for companies): "Planes desde $965 mensuales por colaborador"; a form on /landing/convenio-empresa/ that an adviser answers.

## What the site does not publish
