# Buying a property through Ssilva
Sources: ssilva.cl home page, /centro-de-negocios/, the project pages' heroes, the quote form (site notes §1a, §2, §3). General terms are explained in plain words; the site gives no rates, terms or conditions, so neither do you.

- Prices are in UF (Unidad de Fomento), Chile's inflation-indexed unit of account. Its value in pesos changes daily; you do not know today's value. The /oportunidades/ page has a "Calculadora de UF".
- "Desde X UF" on a card or a hero is the lowest price in the project. A typology's own price shows in the project page's typology section, and the cotizar page lists each unit with its price.
- Pie: the down payment. Projects advertise paying it in instalments ("cuotas"):
  - Edificio Carrera: "COMPRA HOY SIN PIE", "PAGA EL PIE HASTA EN 60 CUOTAS".
  - Piemonte Chicureo: "PAGA EL SALDO DEL PIE HASTA EN 60 CUOTAS", "PAGA SÓLO EL 5% DE PIE".
  - Millalongo 72: "PAGA EL PIE Hasta en 60 Cuotas".
  - Aires de Quintay: "PAGA EL PIE HASTA EN 36 CUOTAS".
  - Edificio Uniq: "PAGA EL 7% DE PIE HASTA EN 24 CUOTAS*".
  - Edificio Rondizzoni: "PAGA EL PIE HASTA EN 20 CUOTAS".
  - Distrito Barroso: "PAGA EL PIE HASTA EN 18 CUOTAS".
  - Parque el Rodeo Encino: "Paga sólo el 10% de pie hasta en 30 cuotas".
  - Centro de Negocios (investors): "pagando desde el 0% de pie y hasta en 60 cuotas".
- The rest of the price is usually financed with a crédito hipotecario (a bank mortgage). Ssilva's Centro de Negocios helps: "Reunimos la información necesaria para apoyarte en la gestión de tu crédito hipotecario."
- Tasa subsidiada: the home page's third slide says "Compra hoy CON TASA SUBSIDIADA" (a subsidised mortgage rate). The site gives no conditions: the visitor asks an executive through the quote form or WhatsApp.
- Estados (construction stages), as the project status bar shows them, in order: En blanco (sold off-plan, construction not started), Inicio de obra, En verde (under construction), Obra gruesa (the structure is built), Término de obra, Próxima entrega, Entrega inmediata (ready to move in). Buying early usually means waiting for delivery; "Entrega inmediata" means it is ready.
- The quote form ("Cotiza"), on every project page beside the typologies, asks: Nombre, Apellidos, N° de Rut, email, teléfono, and why they buy (Invertir or Habitar); then the typology they want, their ingreso líquido mensual (monthly take-home income) and how much they have saved for the pie, both as ranges. An executive then contacts them. See read_topic formulario-cotizacion.
- To visit: each project page's contact block gives its opening hours ("HORARIO DE ATENCIÓN"), phone and email; some offer "AGENDA VISITA A PILOTO" (the show flat) or "VISITA SALA DE VENTAS". Every project page has a WhatsApp button ("CONTÁCTANOS").
- Reservar: each project card also has a "Reservar" link to its cotizar page with ?action=reservar.
