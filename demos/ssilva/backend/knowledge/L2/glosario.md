# Glossary for English speakers
Sources: the site's own words (site notes §0, §2, §3). Meanings are plain-language explanations.

- UF (Unidad de Fomento): an inflation-indexed unit of account; Chilean property prices are quoted in it, and its peso value changes daily.
- Desde: "from" (the starting price).
- Pie: the down payment. Cuotas: instalments. "Paga el pie hasta en 60 cuotas": pay the down payment in up to 60 instalments.
- Crédito hipotecario: a mortgage. Tasa subsidiada: a subsidised interest rate.
- RUT (N° de Rut): the Chilean national ID and tax number, like 12.345.678-5.
- Departamento / depto: apartment. Casa: house. Oficina: office. Local comercial: shop unit. Estudio: studio.
- Tipología: a unit layout. "2D | 2B": 2 dormitorios (bedrooms) and 2 baños (bathrooms). "+ Estar": with an extra living room. Mariposa, Suite, Penthouse, Loft, Twins: layout names.
- Área total / área útil: total and usable floor area, in m². Superficie interior / terraza: interior and terrace area. Orientación: which way it faces (Norte, Sur, Oriente = east, Poniente = west).
- En blanco: sold off-plan, before construction. En verde: under construction. Obra gruesa: structure built. Término de obra: construction finished. Próxima entrega: delivery soon. Entrega inmediata: ready to move in.
- Sala de ventas: sales office. Piloto: show flat. Amenidades / equipamiento: shared amenities (pool, gym, quincho = barbecue area, cowork).
- Segunda vivienda: a second (holiday) home. Para vivir / para invertir: to live in / to invest.
- Comuna: municipality (district). Región Metropolitana: the Santiago region. Región de Valparaíso / V Región: the central coast (Viña del Mar, Reñaca, Algarrobo, Las Cruces, Quintay, Maitencillo).
- Ingreso líquido: monthly take-home income. Ahorro: savings. Cotizar: to get a quote. Reservar: to reserve.
- Arriendo garantizado: guaranteed rental income. Devolución de IVA: VAT refund.
