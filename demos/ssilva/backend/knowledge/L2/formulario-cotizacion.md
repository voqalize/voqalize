# The quote form and the cotizar page
Sources: site notes §3 (the project page's HubSpot form, the /proyectos/cotizar/ flow, the Centro de Ayuda form).

The quote form on a project page:
- Sits in the typology section, beside the floor plan ({show: formulario}). Every project page with typologies has one; Alto Quintay's page does not.
- Step one: Nombre, Apellidos, N° de Rut, "Tu mejor correo" (email), Teléfono (Chile +56 by default; a country picker for others), and "¿Qué te motiva a realizar la compra?" with the options Invertir or Habitar. The button "Siguiente" goes to step two; it does not send anything.
- Step two: "¿Qué tipo de departamento estas buscando?" (options per project, e.g. "2 dormitorios", "3 dormitorios"), "¿Cuál es tu ingreso líquido mensual?" (ranges per project, e.g. "Menos de $1.000.000", "Entre $1.000.000 a $1.200.000", … "Más de $7.000.000"), and "¿Cuánto ahorro puedes destinar para un pie?" ("No tengo ahorro", "Menos de $2.000.000", "Entre $2.000.000 a $5.000.000", "Entre $5.000.000 a $8.000.000", "Entre $8.000.000 a $11.000.000", "Entre $11.000.000 a $15.000.000", "Más de $15.000.000"). Buttons: "Anterior" and "Enviar".
- The project's name and the source are filled in by the site. Pressing "Enviar" accepts the processing of the data (there is no checkbox). The visitor presses Enviar themselves.
- The form checks the email and phone and may say "Introduce una dirección de correo válida." or "Este número de teléfono no es válido o tiene un formato incorrecto."
- Amounts are Chilean pesos ($).

The cotizar page (/proyectos/cotizar/<project>/):
- Step 1 "SELECCIONA LAS TIPOLOGÍAS DE INTERÉS": a tab per typology with its surfaces, orientation and floor plan, and a "Selecciona la unidad para cotizar" list of unit numbers with their prices. "CONTINUAR" goes on.
- Step 2 "INGRESA TUS DATOS PERSONALES": the same form as above.
- Eight newer projects have an empty cotizar page (Estocolmo, Isla Picton, Vistamar, Parque el Rodeo Arrayán and Encino, Mirador Piedra Roja, Vista Golf, Lagunamar Dunas): use the form on the project page.

Without a RUT (a visitor from abroad): offer the project's WhatsApp ("CONTÁCTANOS") or its phone instead.
