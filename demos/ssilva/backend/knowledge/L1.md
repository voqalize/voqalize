## Who Ssilva is
- SSILVA Gestión Inmobiliaria (ssilva.cl, "Ssilva"): a Chilean real-estate sales and management company. It markets and sells projects built by partner developers ("conectando a los constructores de proyectos inmobiliarios con sus potenciales compradores"), and advises from the project's creation to the sale.
- Founded in 1983 as "Stitchkin". The site says "más de 40 años" (the home page still says "más de 30 años") and "+ de 3.000 departamentos vendidos".
- Head office: Augusto Leguía Norte 201, Las Condes. Phone (56 2) 2496 1300. Email contacto@ssilva.cl. Each project also has its own sales phone, email, hours and WhatsApp (read_project).
- Who it is for: first-home buyers ("primera vivienda"), investors (Centro de Negocios, Oportunidades) and second-home buyers on the coast (Región de Valparaíso).
- The site is in Spanish only. Every price is in UF.

## How buying works here (explain simply, never as financial advice)
- UF (Unidad de Fomento): Chile's inflation-indexed unit of account; property prices are quoted in it and its peso value changes every day. You do not know today's value: /oportunidades/ has a "Calculadora de UF".
- Pie: the down payment. Projects advertise paying it "en cuotas" (instalments): e.g. "PAGA EL PIE HASTA EN 60 CUOTAS". The rest is usually a crédito hipotecario (a bank mortgage), which the visitor arranges; the Centro de Negocios offers "asesoría financiera" for it.
- Tasa subsidiada: the home page advertises "Compra hoy CON TASA SUBSIDIADA" (buy with a subsidised mortgage rate). The site gives no conditions: an executive explains them.
- RUT: the Chilean national ID / tax number (e.g. 12.345.678-5). The quote form asks for it. A visitor without one can write or call the project's executive on WhatsApp instead.
- "2D | 2B" on a typology means 2 dormitorios (bedrooms) and 2 baños (bathrooms). "Estudio" is a studio.
- Estado (construction stage), in order: En blanco (sold off-plan, before construction), Inicio de obra, En verde (under construction), Obra gruesa (structure built), Término de obra, Próxima entrega, Entrega inmediata (ready to move in).
- Perfil, as the site tags projects: Para vivir, Para invertir, Segunda vivienda.
- Each project page has a quote form ("Cotiza"): name, apellidos, RUT, email, celular and motivo (Invertir / Habitar), then step two: tipología, ingreso líquido mensual and ahorro para el pie, as ranges. The visitor presses "Enviar" themselves.

## The site
- / (home): hero "Conoce NUESTROS PROYECTOS", "Somos Ssilva", "Te recomendamos" (tabs Oportunidades, Destacados), "Invierte tranquilo, nosotros nos ocupamos".
- /proyectos/: every project, eight per page, filtered by Tipo de propiedad, Región and, in the Región Metropolitana, Comuna. No price, bedroom or status filter: you filter with find_projects.
- /oportunidades/: the same list for investors, with a Calculadora de UF.
- /centro-de-negocios/: for investors: "desde el 0% de pie y hasta en 60 cuotas", "Arriendo garantizado" for the first 20 months, Devolución de IVA, and a process: Evaluación, Asesoría financiera, Acompañamiento integral (read_topic centro-de-negocios).
- /sobre-nosotros/: who Ssilva is, its history and its people. /ayuda-llamanos/: Centro de Ayuda contact form. /vende-tu-terreno/: to sell land to Ssilva.
- Each project page: hero (address, "Desde … UF"), description, galería, tipologías (typology list, price, floor plan, areas, and the quote form beside them), sometimes a tour, amenidades, estado, brochure, contacto (hours, phone, email, map).
- Alto Quintay (/alto-quintay/, Desde 9.950 UF, Club de Golf Santa Augusta, Quintay, 2 and 3 dormitorios) has a page and a card on the home page's Destacados tab, but is not in the project list, its filters or find_projects. Its page has no typology section and no quote form: contact is by WhatsApp ("AGENDAR VISITA A PILOTO").
- Locales Comerciales Distrito Barroso (Santiago, desde 5.408 UF, En verde, para invertir) is listed but has no page of its own: only its cotizar page.
