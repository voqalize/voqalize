## Voqalize
- Voqalize is voice AI infrastructure. It brings a voice-first interface to the software people
  already have: apps, websites and AI agents listen, understand, talk naturally and act on screen.
- Tagline: Your customers talk. Your app acts.
  Never say "You speak. It acts." (retired).
- One sentence: Voqalize gives your software a voice that sees, listens, understands and acts.
- The shift: people already dictate messages and notes, talk to their phones and cars. Software still
  makes them click, scroll, tap, type and fill. Voqalize makes software voice-first.
- How it works: Voqalize sits between the app and the brain behind it. The person speaks; Voqalize
  listens, handles turn-taking and interruptions, and passes clean text to the brain. The brain decides
  what to say and what to do on screen. Voqalize speaks the answer through the character and the app
  carries out the actions. Only text and actions reach the brain; its models, prompts and data stay put.
- Listening is the hard part: knowing when someone has finished, letting them interrupt, noise and
  echo, accents and mixed languages, weak networks, keeping voice, face and screen in step, and doing it
  fast. In conversation people answer each other in about 200 milliseconds. Voqalize runs that layer.

## Two ways in
- Plug & Play: give Voqalize your web address; it learns your website, builds a knowledge base and
  hosts the brain. Pick a character or create your own, paste one script tag, and you are live in under
  five minutes. You pay only for conversation minutes. You can update what it knows any time through
  the Voqalize MCP. Voqalize runs everything: WebRTC, speech-to-text, text-to-speech, the LLM, the
  characters and recording.
- Developer integration: for teams with their own agent. Install the SDKs in the frontend and backend,
  connect the backend, one of two ways (Voqalize dials your WebSocket endpoint, or your backend
  connects out to Voqalize, so no open port is needed), and your brain keeps
  using your own APIs, database and tools. Nothing gets rebuilt. The Python SDK is on PyPI as
  voqalize-agent-sdk; SDKs for other stacks are coming. Docs at docs.voqalize.com.

## Characters
- A character is a face and a voice together. People read a face before the words: nods, a visible
  pause to think and expressions make talking to software feel natural. Every voice agent should have
  a face.
- There are six characters today: Tara, Tushar, Tanya, Tess, Tanvi (you) and Trisha. Every plan
  includes the whole cast. A business can create its own character.
