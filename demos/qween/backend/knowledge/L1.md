## The house
- Qween (brand of Tanzior Jewels Private Limited, Bengaluru). A "self-discovery experiential jewellery" house: the shopper browses and touches pieces themselves; boutiques are open display, not locked counters.
- Fine gold jewellery with natural diamonds and natural gemstones. 100% natural — never lab-grown. Silver is sold only as coins.
- The Signature Orange Sapphire is Qween's mark: a tiny orange sapphire set in almost every piece. Its value is waived off in the price breakup. It is not what a piece is "about" — talk about the piece's own stones.
- Single supply chain: diamonds from one partner, Rosy Blue; no middlemen; traceable "Mine to Marvel".
- "131 pairs of masterful hands" craft each piece (their phrase).
- Prices on the site are in rupees and include taxes; the final price is set on the day of purchase. Free insured shipping on online orders to serviceable pincodes across India, in a Qween jewellery box with the certificates and invoice.
- Founders: Amit Kumar (co-founder and CEO) and Suyash Motarwar (co-founder and CTPO).

## Certification
- Gold: BIS hallmark with its HUID for purity. Silver coins are not hallmarked.
- Diamonds, gemstones and diamond jewellery: IGI certified. Signature solitaires: GIA grading report.
- Every piece comes with a certificate of weight, purity and quality.
- Experience centres have instruments to verify karat purity, diamond authenticity and hearts-and-arrows symmetry in front of the shopper.

## Reading a piece
- Diamond quality on a card reads clarity then colour: "SI - HI" is SI clarity, H to I colour; "SI - GH" is G to H colour, a whiter grade; "SI1 - J" is SI1 clarity, J colour. A shade name in place of the colour — "SI - Cocoa Promise" — means warm coloured diamonds in that shade (below). Solitaires are graded per stone, e.g. "SI1 - G", "VS2 - G", "VVS1 - G". A gemstone piece's card names the stone instead, e.g. "Yellow Sapphire | 9 KT GOLD".
- Purity: 9, 14, 18, 22 or 24 karat. 22 karat is on gold coins and some plain or lightly set pieces. Diamond jewellery in India is generally set in 14 or 18 karat because it holds stones better than 22 karat. Colour and purity are independent: an 18K rose and an 18K white ring hold the same gold.
- Qween's golds, each a different alloy on the same gold base:
  - Desert Noon — classic Indian yellow (balanced copper and silver).
  - Champagne Fizz — a pale, warm gold, lighter than Desert Noon.
  - Rose Bloom — rose gold; copper-rich, among the hardest, suits rings worn every day.
  - Snowfall White — white gold, rhodium plated; the plating thins over two to three years of daily wear. Re-plating is a paid service at the boutique, recommended when the plating has worn; the warranty does not cover plating wear.
  - Cloudy Grey — grey gold, palladium-based, needs no rhodium plating; reads softer and slightly warmer than bright white, and wears very well.
  - Clover Green — a pale green-yellow gold; silver-rich, softer than rose.
  - Solid Sunrise — pure 24 karat; softest, for pieces worn occasionally.
- Sensitive skin: the risk is nickel in some white golds; palladium-based white and grey golds avoid it. Ask what the white metal is.
- Mixing golds: let one colour lead and use another as an accent.
- Warm natural diamond shades, lightest to darkest: Champagne Hint, Golden Crush, Honey Date, Amber Phase, Caramel Sync, Cocoa Promise, Caviar Eternity (black). Graded as fancy colour, not on the D-to-Z scale; the same budget buys more stone than top-colour white. Middle shades glow in warm golds (Rose Bloom, Desert Noon); Champagne Hint and Caviar Eternity read best in Snowfall White or Cloudy Grey. Ask that the certificate state the colour is natural.
- Couple rings: The One collection — a pair of rings, his and hers, whose diamonds were cut from one rough stone.

## Promises and policies
- 5-year stone price protection (on the product page: "5 Year Stone Value Assurance"). For five years from the invoice, the rate of the diamonds and gemstones is held at the invoiced rate when the piece is later exchanged or bought back. It is not a refund, a price match or a cash payment, and it does not cover gold, making charges or taxes.
- Lifetime buyback and exchange, for jewellery bought from Qween, from 30 days after delivery, in good condition (normal wear is fine), with the original receipt and certificates. Gold is valued at 100% of the day's rate; diamonds and gemstones at 100% for an exchange and 90% for a buyback; making charges and taxes are deducted. An exchange is store credit, valid for a year, towards a purchase of equal or higher value; a buyback is paid to the shopper's bank account after a quality check. Very large solitaires are excluded; for the cut-off, the concierge. At any store or through customer service.
- Returns and exchanges: within 30 days of delivery (online) or purchase (in store); one return or exchange per product in that window. Exchange for another size (rings up to one size either way), variant or style, or store credit. Not returnable within the 30 days: customised, engraved or DIY pieces, coins, pieces damaged by misuse, and pieces missing the "Non-Returnable if Label Removed" tag — these still come under lifetime exchange and buyback. Items of ₹20,000 and above must go back in the tamper-proof packaging Qween provides. Every return is quality-checked (purity, stones, certificate). Refunds go to the original payment method; cash-on-delivery refunds go to a bank account verified by penny drop.
- Warranty: one year from purchase, against manufacturing defects only, claimed at a Qween store. Plating wear, scratches and misuse are not covered.
- Assured Gold Exchange: bring old gold of 9 karat or more — jewellery, coins or bars, even from another jeweller — and get an extra 2% on its value. Tested on the spot, valued at the day's gold rate, melted in the shopper's presence after they sign. Paid only as store credit, valid 365 days, not cash and not for coins. A PAN card is required and must match the account name. At Qween stores only. Whether a piece with stones is accepted (for its gold alone): the concierge.
- Self Invest Plan: save monthly (from ₹5,000 up to ₹1,00,000, in multiples of ₹1,000) for 6 or 10 months. Studded jewellery: 1 instalment free on the 6-month plan, 1.5 on the 10-month plan. Plain jewellery: 0.5 and 0.75. No cancellation or refund; not for coins; early redemption after the third instalment through the concierge; missing instalments in a row can forfeit the benefit (for the exact rule, the concierge); benefits valid 6 months after maturity; combinable with other offers.
- Coins: 22 karat gold and 999 silver coins, bought at Qween stores.
- Festive offer (on the site as of 2026-11-02, with no end date): a free gold or silver coin, up to 10 grams, with eligible jewellery in store, or its value as a discount online; the coin depends on the bill. It cannot be combined with Self Invest Plan or gold exchange credit. Whether it still runs, and the slabs: the concierge.
- Services at the boutiques, for life but not free: repairs and restoration with a quotation first; cleaning and polishing (an annual visit is recommended); rhodium re-plating; ring resizing by up to one size up or down (some eternity and delicate designs cannot be resized); engraving on rings and bangles, at the boutique only.
- Gifting: every piece comes in Qween's signature packaging, and the site has a gifting section.
