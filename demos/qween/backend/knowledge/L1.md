## Reading a piece
- Diamond quality on a card reads clarity then colour: "SI - HI" is SI clarity, H to I colour; "VS - GH" is VS clarity, G to H colour (the better of the two). Solitaires are graded per stone, e.g. "SI1 - G", "VS2 - G", "VVS1 - G".
- Purity: 9, 14, 18 or 24 karat. Diamond jewellery in India is generally set in 14 or 18 karat because it holds stones better than 22 karat. Colour and purity are independent: an 18K rose and an 18K white ring hold the same gold.
- Qween's golds, each a different alloy on the same gold base:
  - Desert Noon — classic yellow (balanced copper and silver).
  - Champagne Fizz — a lighter, paler yellow.
  - Rose Bloom — rose gold; copper-rich, among the hardest, suits rings worn every day.
  - Snowfall White — white gold, usually rhodium plated; the plating thins over two to three years of daily wear. Re-plating is part of Qween's lifetime service, not a paid repair.
  - Cloudy Grey — grey gold, palladium-based, needs no rhodium plating; a softer alternative to white.
  - Clover Green — green gold; silver-rich, softer.
  - Solid Sunrise — pure 24 karat; softest, for pieces worn occasionally.
- Sensitive skin: the risk is nickel in some white golds; palladium-based white and grey golds avoid it. Ask what the white metal is.
- Mixing golds: let one colour lead and use another as an accent.
- Warm natural diamond shades, lightest to darkest: Champagne Hint, Golden Crush, Honey Date, Amber Phase, Caramel Sync, Cocoa Promise, Caviar Eternity (black). Graded as fancy colour, not on the D-to-Z scale; the same budget buys more stone than top-colour white. Middle shades glow in warm golds (Rose Bloom, Desert Noon); Champagne Hint and Caviar Eternity read best in Snowfall White or Cloudy Grey. Ask that the certificate state the colour is natural.

## Promises and policies
- 5-year stone price protection.
- Lifetime buyback and exchange: any time, for jewellery bought from Qween, in good condition (normal wear is fine), with the original receipt and certificates. Valued at current gold and diamond rates, so it may differ from the price paid. Given as store credit; an exchange must go towards a purchase of equal or higher value. At any store or through customer service.
- Returns and exchanges: within 30 days of delivery (online) or purchase (in store); one return or exchange per product in that window. Exchange for another size, variant or style, or store credit. Not returnable: customised or personalised pieces, pieces damaged by misuse, and pieces missing the "Non-Returnable if Label Removed" tag. Items of ₹20,000 and above must go back in the special packaging Qween provides. Every return is quality-checked (purity, stones, certificate). Refunds go to the original payment method; cash-on-delivery refunds go to a bank account verified by penny drop.
- Assured Gold Exchange: bring old plain gold — jewellery, coins or bars, nothing with stones. Tested on the spot, valued at the day's gold rate, melted in the shopper's presence after they sign. Paid only as store credit, valid 365 days, not cash and not for coins. A PAN card is required and must match the account name.
- Self Invest Plan: save monthly (from ₹5,000, in multiples of ₹1,000) for 6 or 10 months. Studded jewellery: 1 instalment free on the 6-month plan, 1.5 on the 10-month plan. Plain jewellery: 0.5 and 0.75. No cancellation or refund; not for coins; early redemption after the third instalment through the concierge; missing more than 2 instalments in a row forfeits the benefit; benefits valid 6 months after maturity; combinable with other offers.
- Services at the boutiques: repairs and restoration with a quotation first; cleaning and polishing (an annual visit is recommended); rhodium re-plating; ring resizing by up to one size up or down; engraving, boutique only.
