## Ordering

**Who is Mira?**
Mira is Fernhollow's design adviser. You can talk to her by voice while you browse: tell her about a room, a budget or a piece you like, and she will show you suitable products, compare options, explain materials and help you put a room together. She only tells you what we know about each piece.

**Can I see a product's materials and finishes?**
Yes. Each product page lists what the piece is made of, its highlights and the options available, such as finish or fabric.

**Do you show stock levels?**
No. If a piece is unavailable after you order, we will contact you before taking any further steps.

## Delivery

**How much is delivery?**
Standard delivery is free on orders over $500. Orders of $500 or less have a flat $49 delivery charge per order.

**When will my order arrive?**
We do not promise specific delivery dates at checkout. We will be in touch once your order is on its way, and for large furniture we will agree a delivery day with you.

## Assembly

**Will you assemble my furniture?**
Yes. Our assembly service is available for furniture for a flat fee of $79 per item. Add it when you order.

**Does assembly include wall mounting?**
No. Wall mounting and electrical installation should be done by a qualified professional.

## Returns

**What is your returns policy?**
You can return unused items in their original condition within 30 days of delivery for a refund to your original payment method.

**What if something arrives damaged?**
Tell us as soon as you can, ideally with a photo, and we will arrange a repair, replacement or refund.

**Can I return furniture I have assembled?**
Furniture that has been assembled and taken apart usually cannot be returned unless it was faulty. If you are unsure about a piece, ask us before you build it.

## Care

**How do I look after natural materials?**
Our care page has general guides for wood, linen, boucle, wool, cotton, leather, ceramic, brass and cast iron. Always check the product's own care label first.

**Why does my wooden piece look slightly different from the photo?**
Wood is a natural material. Grain, knots and colour vary from piece to piece and mellow over time, so no two are exactly alike.
