## Our assembly service

Assembly is available for **furniture**: sofas, beds, desks, tables, chairs, shelving and storage. We charge a **flat fee of $79 per item** of furniture assembled, whatever the piece. Add it when you order, or ask Mira to add it for you.

Our team will build the piece in the room of your choice and take the packaging away with them.

## What is included

- Assembly of the furniture in the room you choose.
- Removal of packaging.

## What is not included

- Wall mounting of shelves, mirrors or lighting, or any electrical installation such as ceiling fixtures. These should be done by a qualified professional.
- Moving or disposing of existing furniture.
- Assembly of decor, textiles or table lamps, which arrive ready to use or need no tools.

## Building it yourself

If you prefer to assemble at home, every piece of furniture that needs it comes with instructions. A few tips:

- Unpack in the room where the piece will live, on a rug or blanket to protect both the floor and the finish.
- Lay out and count all the parts before you begin.
- Hand-tighten fixings first, then tighten fully once the piece is square.
- Two people make most furniture assembly quicker and safer.
- Anchor tall shelving and storage to the wall using suitable fixings for your wall type.

## Returns and assembly

Furniture that has been assembled and then taken apart cannot usually be returned unless it was faulty. If you are unsure about a piece, ask us before you build it.
