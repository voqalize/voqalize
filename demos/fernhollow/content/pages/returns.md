## The short version

You have **30 days from delivery** to return any item that is **unused** and in its original condition. Once we receive and check it, we refund you to your original payment method.

## What can be returned

- Furniture, lighting, textiles and decor that is unused, undamaged and has all its parts.
- Items in their original packaging wherever possible. If you no longer have the packaging, let us know and we will talk it through.

## What cannot be returned

- Items that have been used, washed, altered or damaged after delivery.
- Furniture that has been assembled and then disassembled, unless it arrived faulty.

## If something arrives damaged or faulty

Please tell us as soon as you can, ideally within a few days of delivery, with a photo of the problem. We will arrange a repair, replacement or refund. Faulty items are not subject to the unused rule.

## How to start a return

1. Get in touch with us, or ask Mira, and tell us your order and the item you want to return.
2. We will confirm the return and agree the next steps with you, including how the item gets back to us.
3. Once the item arrives and has been checked, we process your refund.

## Exchanges

The simplest way to exchange is to return the original item and place a new order for the one you want. That way you are not waiting for one before receiving the other.
