## Delivery charges

| Order total | Standard delivery |
| --- | --- |
| Over $500 | Free |
| $500 or less | Flat $49 per order |

The charge is per order, not per item, so adding a cushion to a lamp does not add a second fee.

## When it will arrive

Delivery timing depends on the pieces in your order and where you live. We do not promise specific delivery dates at checkout. Once your order is on its way, we will be in touch to let you know what to expect, and for larger furniture we will agree a delivery day with you.

## Before delivery day

- Measure doorways, stairs, corridors and lifts on the route to the room, especially for sofas, beds, desks and tables.
- Clear a path from the door to the room.
- Make sure someone is home to receive the order.

## Large furniture

Large pieces are brought into your home. If you would like us to put them together for you as well, add our assembly service. See the assembly page for details.

## Checking your order

When your delivery arrives, please check it over. If anything is damaged, tell us as soon as you can, ideally with a photo, and we will put it right. See our returns page.

## Stock

We do not show stock levels on the site. If a piece is unavailable when you order, we will contact you before taking any further steps.
