## Why we exist

Fernhollow began with a simple frustration. Furnishing a home had become a choice between two things we did not want: pieces made to look good for a season and then be replaced, or pieces so precious they felt out of place in a real house with real people in it. We wanted a third option. Furniture and objects that are quiet, well made and pleasant to touch, that look better with use, and that sit together easily without trying too hard.

So we started a small design studio in a converted workshop in the countryside, with a long table, a lot of wood offcuts and a rule that nothing goes into the collection until we have lived with it.

## What we believe

**Calm is a design choice.** A room feels restful when there is less in it, when the colours sit close together and when nothing shouts. We design for that feeling first and work backwards to the object.

**Natural materials, honestly used.** Oak, walnut and ash. Linen, wool, cotton and boucle. Stoneware, ceramic, brass and iron. We like materials that show their grain, their weave and their making, and that age rather than wear out.

**Honest pieces.** We describe what a piece is and what it is made of, and we leave it there. No inflated promises, no invented stories. If a desk is oak with metal legs, that is what we will tell you.

**Rooms, not just products.** Most of us do not buy a sofa in isolation; we are trying to make a room work. That is why our collection is organised by room, why our journal is full of practical advice, and why Emily, our design adviser, can help you put a whole room together.

## How we work

Every piece in the collection is chosen or developed by our studio. We look at how it will be used, how it will sit beside the other things in a room, and how it will look in ten years. We favour pieces that can be reconfigured, repaired or refreshed over ones that must be replaced.

We keep our palette deliberately narrow, warm woods, soft neutrals and a few earthy accents, so that what you buy from us this year will sit happily beside what you buy next year.

## Meet Emily

Emily is Fernhollow's design adviser. Ask her, by voice, what you are trying to do with a room, and she will show you pieces that suit it, compare options side by side, explain materials and finishes, and help you stay within budget. She will tell you only what we know about a piece, and she will always point you to our policies when it matters.

## Our promise

We want you to love what you bring home. If something is not right, our returns, delivery, assembly and care pages set out exactly how we will help, in plain language.
