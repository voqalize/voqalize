A dining room starts with its table. Everything else — the chairs, the light above it, the bowl in the
middle — takes its cue from the surface you gather around.

::product{id="weathered-oak-dining-table"}

## Start with the top

Oak and walnut read differently at the end of a long day. Oak keeps a room light; walnut pulls it in.

- Choose the finish first, then the chairs.
- Keep the centre clear for the food.

> The best table is the one you stop noticing, because everyone is talking.
